Cousins Maine Lobster

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Maine lobster roll
Home base: Los Angeles, California
Rolling since 2012

To eaters in Los Angeles, California, Cousins Maine Lobster simply means Seafood done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Cousins Maine Lobster has been rolling since 2012. Founded by Maine cousins Jim Tselikis and Sabin Lomac; a Shark Tank appearance turned one truck into a national fleet.

The maine lobster roll at Cousins Maine Lobster is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Los Angeles, and Cousins Maine Lobster has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Cooking Seafood from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Cousins Maine Lobster works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The featured tier holds the trucks other trucks get measured against. Cousins Maine Lobster earned its standing the only way it can be earned: year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

Regulars will tell you the real signature is the rhythm: the truck posted up, the line forming, the window sliding open. Cousins Maine Lobster has that rhythm down to a ritual, and rituals are what turn a good truck into a beloved one.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Cousins Maine Lobster's content is Seafood cooked in Los Angeles, California, and judged by one dish above the rest: maine lobster roll.

The featured tier is this directory's front row, and Cousins Maine Lobster sits in it because Los Angeles put it there — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

The entry sits in the Los Angeles pages, filed under California: Cousins Maine Lobster. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
