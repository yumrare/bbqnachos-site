Wilmington, Delaware · Directory Entry

Diamond State Dogs

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: First State dogs
Home base: Wilmington, Delaware

The entry for Wilmington, Delaware reads Diamond State Dogs: a Hot dogs truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Diamond State Dogs is carried by its first state dogs. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Wilmington eats above its weight because of trucks like Diamond State Dogs. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Diamond State Dogs: the Hot dogs is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Diamond State Dogs's content is Hot dogs cooked in Wilmington, Delaware, and judged by one dish above the rest: first state dogs.

Filed under Delaware, in the Wilmington pages: Diamond State Dogs. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
