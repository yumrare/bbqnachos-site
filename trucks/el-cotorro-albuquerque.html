Albuquerque, New Mexico · Directory Entry

El Cotorro

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Tacos and elotes
Home base: Albuquerque, New Mexico

Some trucks chase trends; El Cotorro chases its regulars. Based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the Mexican kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at El Cotorro and go straight to the tacos and elotes. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck in Albuquerque answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and El Cotorro thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Everything on the El Cotorro menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Mexican here tastes focused.

Line up every truck in this record and El Cotorro still stands alone on its facts: a Mexican kitchen based in Albuquerque, known first and last for tacos and elotes.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. El Cotorro of Albuquerque takes its place in the record — Mexican, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
