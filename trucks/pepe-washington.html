Washington, District of Columbia · Directory Entry

Pepe

Cuisine: Spanish sandwiches
Signature: Flauta sandwiches by José Andrés
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Ask around Washington where the Spanish sandwiches cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Pepe. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in District of Columbia, service after service.

flauta sandwiches by josé andrés: that is the signature at Pepe, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Washington crosses town for.

A Washington truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Pepe draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Spanish sandwiches cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Pepe leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

No other truck in this record runs on Pepe's exact combination: Spanish sandwiches out of Washington, with flauta sandwiches by josé andrés as the order its name is attached to.

The entry sits in the Washington pages, filed under District of Columbia: Pepe. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
