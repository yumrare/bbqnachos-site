Fancheezical

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Rhody melts
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Home base Providence, Rhode Island; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Fancheezical cooks Grilled cheese the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

The signature at Fancheezical is rhody melts, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Providence crosses town for.

A good truck is town news in Providence, and Fancheezical has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Fancheezical: the Grilled cheese is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Fancheezical would still stand alone on its facts: a Grilled cheese kitchen based in Providence, known first and last for rhody melts.
