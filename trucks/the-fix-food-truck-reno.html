Reno, Nevada · Directory Entry

The Fix Food Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: The fix plates
Home base: Reno, Nevada

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Fix Food Truck of Reno, Nevada is no exception. A American comfort kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Reno the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at The Fix Food Truck and go straight to the the fix plates. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Ask in Reno where to eat and the directions often end at one window: The Fix Food Truck's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

Space on the The Fix Food Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the American comfort here tastes so focused.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and The Fix Food Truck's is easy to write: American comfort from Reno, Nevada, with the fix plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like The Fix Food Truck matter to the places they feed. Home base Reno, Nevada; cuisine American comfort; signature: the fix plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
