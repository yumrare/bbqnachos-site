Jackson, Mississippi · Directory Entry

The Pig & Pint Truck

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Fondren smoked plates
Home base: Jackson, Mississippi

Read the Jackson, Mississippi pages and you will find The Pig & Pint Truck: a Barbecue truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

fondren smoked plates: that is the signature at The Pig & Pint Truck, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Jackson crosses town for.

Trucks like The Pig & Pint Truck are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Jackson may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Speed is part of the recipe at The Pig & Pint Truck — Barbecue cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Pig & Pint Truck's writes itself: Barbecue from Jackson, Mississippi, with fondren smoked plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. The Pig & Pint Truck of Jackson holds its place on Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
