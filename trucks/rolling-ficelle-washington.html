Rolling Ficelle

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: Ficelle sandwiches
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Some trucks chase trends; Rolling Ficelle chases its regulars. Based in Washington, District of Columbia, the Sandwiches kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Rolling Ficelle is carried by its ficelle sandwiches. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Survival in Washington means being somebody's favorite, and Rolling Ficelle is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Space on the Rolling Ficelle menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Sandwiches here tastes so focused.

What makes Rolling Ficelle itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Sandwiches out of Washington, with ficelle sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

When the quarterly review comes back to Rolling Ficelle, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
