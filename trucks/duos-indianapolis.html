Indianapolis, Indiana · Directory Entry

Duos

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Ever-changing duos plates
Home base: Indianapolis, Indiana

To eaters in Indianapolis, Indiana, Duos simply means Global comfort done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

If Duos served nothing else, the record would read the same: ever-changing duos plates. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Ask in Indianapolis where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Duos's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Speed is part of the recipe at Duos — Global comfort cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Duos's writes itself: Global comfort from Indianapolis, Indiana, with ever-changing duos plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

The quarterly review will come back to Duos the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
