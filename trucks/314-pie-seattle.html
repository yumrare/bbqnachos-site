Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

314 Pie

Cuisine: Pies
Signature: Savory hand pies
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Ask around Seattle where the Pies cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to 314 Pie. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Washington, service after service.

Ask at the window what 314 Pie is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: savory hand pies. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

In a scene as crowded as Seattle's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and 314 Pie draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Pies was made for the truck form, and 314 Pie leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The originality of 314 Pie is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Seattle as the stage, Pies as the craft, and savory hand pies as the headline.

When the quarterly review comes back to 314 Pie, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
