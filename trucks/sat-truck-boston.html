Boston, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

Saté Truck

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Saté plates
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts

Saté Truck rolls out of Boston, Massachusetts, serving Vietnamese to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Saté Truck is carried by its saté plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Boston has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and Saté Truck is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Saté Truck cooks like it knows it: Vietnamese with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Saté Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Boston as the stage, Vietnamese as the craft, and saté plates as the headline act.

That is the entry for Saté Truck: Boston's own, a Vietnamese truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
