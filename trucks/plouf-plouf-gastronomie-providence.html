Plouf Plouf Gastronomie

Cuisine: French
Signature: Duck confit and French plates
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Plouf Plouf Gastronomie of Providence, Rhode Island earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A French kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

The duck confit and french plates at Plouf Plouf Gastronomie is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A good truck is town news in Providence, and Plouf Plouf Gastronomie has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

French cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Plouf Plouf Gastronomie leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

What makes Plouf Plouf Gastronomie itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: French out of Providence, with duck confit and french plates as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands for Plouf Plouf Gastronomie of Providence, Rhode Island: French by trade, duck confit and french plates by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
