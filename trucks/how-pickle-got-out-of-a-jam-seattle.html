How Pickle Got Out of a Jam

Cuisine: Pickles
Signature: Pickle plates
Home base: Seattle, Washington

How Pickle Got Out of a Jam is a Pickles food truck based in Seattle, Washington. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Seattle, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The pickle plates at How Pickle Got Out of a Jam is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Seattle runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and How Pickle Got Out of a Jam holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at How Pickle Got Out of a Jam: the Pickles is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

How Pickle Got Out of a Jam does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Seattle, Pickles, pickle plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: How Pickle Got Out of a Jam serves Seattle its Pickles, and the pickle plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
