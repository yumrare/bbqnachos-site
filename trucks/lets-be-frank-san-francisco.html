Let's Be Frank

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Grass-fed frankfurters
Home base: San Francisco, California

Let's Be Frank rolls out of San Francisco, California, serving Hot dogs to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The grass-fed frankfurters at Let's Be Frank is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the San Francisco scene rewards, and it is the reputation Let's Be Frank carries — a stop locals give directions by.

At Let's Be Frank the Hot dogs is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The originality of Let's Be Frank is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: San Francisco as the stage, Hot dogs as the craft, and grass-fed frankfurters as the headline.

When the quarterly review comes back to Let's Be Frank, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
