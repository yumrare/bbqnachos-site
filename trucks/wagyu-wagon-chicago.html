Wagyu Wagon

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Wagyu burgers
Home base: Chicago, Illinois

Wagyu Wagon holds its home base in Chicago, Illinois and serves Burgers from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the Wagyu Wagon window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: wagyu burgers. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A Chicago truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Wagyu Wagon draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

At Wagyu Wagon, Burgers is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Wagyu Wagon's writes itself: Burgers from Chicago, Illinois, with wagyu burgers as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Wagyu Wagon — Chicago's own, a Burgers truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
