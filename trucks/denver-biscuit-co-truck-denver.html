Denver Biscuit Co Truck

Cuisine: Biscuits
Signature: The Franklin biscuit sandwich
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Denver Biscuit Co Truck rolls out of Denver, Colorado, serving Biscuits to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Denver Biscuit Co Truck is carried by its the franklin biscuit sandwich. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

A truck in Denver is a neighborhood before it is a business — Denver Biscuit Co Truck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

Space on the Denver Biscuit Co Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Biscuits here tastes so focused.

Denver Biscuit Co Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Denver, Biscuits, the franklin biscuit sandwich — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

So the record stands for Denver Biscuit Co Truck of Denver, Colorado: Biscuits by trade, the franklin biscuit sandwich by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
