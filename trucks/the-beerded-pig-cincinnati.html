Cincinnati, Ohio · Directory Entry

The Beerded Pig

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Queen City pulled pork
Home base: Cincinnati, Ohio

Ask around Cincinnati where the Barbecue cooking is and somebody points to The Beerded Pig before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Ohio, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at The Beerded Pig and go straight to the queen city pulled pork. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Cincinnati is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and The Beerded Pig has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Cincinnati gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Barbecue cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and The Beerded Pig leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Line up every truck in this record and The Beerded Pig still stands alone on its facts: a Barbecue kitchen based in Cincinnati, known first and last for queen city pulled pork.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. The Beerded Pig of Cincinnati takes its place in the record — Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
