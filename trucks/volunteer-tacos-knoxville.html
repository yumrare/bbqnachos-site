Knoxville, Tennessee · Directory Entry

Volunteer Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Volunteer tacos
Home base: Knoxville, Tennessee

Volunteer Tacos is a Mexican food truck based in Knoxville, Tennessee. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Knoxville, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Volunteer Tacos it is volunteer tacos. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Trucks like Volunteer Tacos are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Knoxville may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Space on the Volunteer Tacos menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Mexican here tastes so focused.

Line up every truck in this record and Volunteer Tacos still stands alone on its facts: a Mexican kitchen based in Knoxville, known first and last for volunteer tacos.

That is the entry for Volunteer Tacos: Knoxville's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
