Slidin' Thru

Cuisine: Sliders
Signature: Vegas sliders
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

Slidin' Thru holds its home base in Las Vegas, Nevada and serves Sliders from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the window what Slidin' Thru is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: vegas sliders. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Las Vegas counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Slidin' Thru is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

The kitchen at Slidin' Thru treats Sliders as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No theme song, no mascot: Slidin' Thru's identity is written in three facts — Las Vegas, Sliders, vegas sliders — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

So the record stands: Slidin' Thru, of Las Vegas, Nevada — Sliders by trade, vegas sliders by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
