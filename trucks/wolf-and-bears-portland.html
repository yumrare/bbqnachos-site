Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

Wolf & Bear's

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Falafel and sabich wraps
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Home base Portland, Oregon; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Wolf & Bear's cooks Middle Eastern the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

If Wolf & Bear's could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: falafel and sabich wraps. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Portland counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Wolf & Bear's is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

Speed is part of the recipe at Wolf & Bear's — Middle Eastern cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Wolf & Bear's's writes itself: Middle Eastern from Portland, Oregon, with falafel and sabich wraps as the dish the city attached to its name.

So the record stands: Wolf & Bear's, of Portland, Oregon — Middle Eastern by trade, falafel and sabich wraps by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
