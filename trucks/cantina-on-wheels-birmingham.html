Birmingham, Alabama · Directory Entry

Cantina on Wheels

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Birmingham tacos
Home base: Birmingham, Alabama

Cantina on Wheels holds its home base in Birmingham, Alabama and serves Mexican from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

If Cantina on Wheels could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: birmingham tacos. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A good truck is town news in Birmingham, and Cantina on Wheels has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Mexican was made for the truck form, and Cantina on Wheels leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

No theme song, no mascot: Cantina on Wheels's identity is written in three facts — Birmingham, Mexican, birmingham tacos — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Cantina on Wheels: Birmingham's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
