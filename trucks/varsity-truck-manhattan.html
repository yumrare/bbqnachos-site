Manhattan, Kansas · Directory Entry

Varsity Truck

Cuisine: Donuts
Signature: Varsity donuts
Home base: Manhattan, Kansas

Varsity Truck is a Donuts food truck based in Manhattan, Kansas. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Manhattan, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Varsity Truck is carried by its varsity donuts. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In Manhattan, a good truck is town news, and Varsity Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Donuts cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Varsity Truck leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

What makes Varsity Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Donuts out of Manhattan, with varsity donuts as the order its name is attached to.

The entry sits in the Manhattan pages, filed under Kansas: Varsity Truck. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
