Shenandoah Smoke Wagon

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Valley smoked plates
Home base: Staunton, Virginia

Shenandoah Smoke Wagon is a Barbecue food truck based in Staunton, Virginia. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Staunton, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

What the window at Shenandoah Smoke Wagon is famous for handing out is valley smoked plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

A truck in a place the size of Staunton becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Shenandoah Smoke Wagon is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

There is a discipline to Barbecue cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Shenandoah Smoke Wagon works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Shenandoah Smoke Wagon's content is Barbecue cooked in Staunton, Virginia, and judged by one dish above the rest: valley smoked plates.
