Funky Monkey Munchies

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Funky monkey plates
Home base: Wichita, Kansas

Funky Monkey Munchies holds its home base in Wichita, Kansas and serves Global comfort from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

funky monkey plates: that is the signature at Funky Monkey Munchies, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Wichita crosses town for.

A truck in Wichita answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Funky Monkey Munchies thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

There is a discipline to Global comfort cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Funky Monkey Munchies works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

No other truck in this record runs on Funky Monkey Munchies's exact combination: Global comfort out of Wichita, with funky monkey plates as the order its name is attached to.
