Rapid City, South Dakota · Directory Entry

Eddie's Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Eddie's tacos
Home base: Rapid City, South Dakota

Read the Rapid City, South Dakota pages and you will find Eddie's Tacos: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

What the window at Eddie's Tacos is famous for handing out is eddie's tacos — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Ask in Rapid City where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Eddie's Tacos's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Space on the Eddie's Tacos menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Mexican here tastes so focused.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Eddie's Tacos's content is Mexican cooked in Rapid City, South Dakota, and judged by one dish above the rest: eddie's tacos.

That is the entry for Eddie's Tacos: Rapid City's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
