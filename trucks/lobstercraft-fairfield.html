Fairfield, Connecticut · Directory Entry

LobsterCraft

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Captain Mike's lobster rolls
Home base: Fairfield, Connecticut

LobsterCraft rolls out of Fairfield, Connecticut, serving Seafood to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and LobsterCraft is carried by its captain mike's lobster rolls. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In Fairfield, a good truck is town news, and LobsterCraft has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

The kitchen at LobsterCraft treats Seafood as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

LobsterCraft does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Fairfield, Seafood, captain mike's lobster rolls — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. LobsterCraft of Fairfield takes its place in the record — Seafood, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
