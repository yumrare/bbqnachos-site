Baltimore, Maryland · Directory Entry

The Smoking Swine

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Smoked meats by the plate
Home base: Baltimore, Maryland

The Smoking Swine holds its home base in Baltimore, Maryland and serves Barbecue from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and The Smoking Swine is carried by its smoked meats by the plate. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

In a place the size of Baltimore, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what The Smoking Swine is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

There is a discipline to Barbecue cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Smoking Swine works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The originality of The Smoking Swine is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Baltimore as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and smoked meats by the plate as the headline.

The entry sits in the Baltimore pages, filed under Maryland: The Smoking Swine. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
