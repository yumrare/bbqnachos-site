Denver, Colorado · Directory Entry

The Bastiche

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: Bastiche stacks
Home base: Denver, Colorado

The Bastiche rolls out of Denver, Colorado, serving Sandwiches to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the bastiche stacks. At The Bastiche, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Denver has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and The Bastiche is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Sandwiches was made for the truck form, and The Bastiche leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

No theme song, no mascot: The Bastiche's identity is written in three facts — Denver, Sandwiches, bastiche stacks — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Filed under Colorado, in the Denver pages: The Bastiche. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
