Dallas, Texas · Directory Entry

Texas Burrito Company

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Texas burritos
Home base: Dallas, Texas

Texas Burrito Company rolls out of Dallas, Texas, serving Mexican to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Texas Burrito Company and go straight to the texas burritos. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A Dallas truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Texas Burrito Company draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Mexican was made for the truck form, and Texas Burrito Company leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Texas Burrito Company does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Dallas, Mexican, texas burritos — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Texas Burrito Company serves Dallas its Mexican, and the texas burritos is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
