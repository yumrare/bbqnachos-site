Biloxi, Mississippi · Directory Entry

Hog Wild Eats

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Coast smoked plates
Home base: Biloxi, Mississippi

Ask around Biloxi where the Barbecue cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Hog Wild Eats. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Mississippi, service after service.

What the window at Hog Wild Eats is famous for handing out is coast smoked plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

What Hog Wild Eats gives Biloxi is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Hog Wild Eats cooks like it knows it: Barbecue with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

No other truck in this record runs on Hog Wild Eats's exact combination: Barbecue out of Biloxi, with coast smoked plates as the order its name is attached to.

When the quarterly review comes back to Hog Wild Eats, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
