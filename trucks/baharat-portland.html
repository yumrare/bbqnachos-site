Portland, Maine · Directory Entry

Baharat

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Shawarma and falafel
Home base: Portland, Maine

Some trucks chase trends; Baharat chases its regulars. Based in Portland, Maine, the Middle Eastern kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Baharat is carried by its shawarma and falafel. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Competition in Portland is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Baharat has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

There is a discipline to Middle Eastern cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Baharat works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Baharat's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Portland as the stage, Middle Eastern as the craft, and shawarma and falafel as the headline act.

For the permanent record: Baharat serves Portland its Middle Eastern, and the shawarma and falafel is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
