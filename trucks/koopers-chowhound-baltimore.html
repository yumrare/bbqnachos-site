Baltimore, Maryland · Directory Entry

Kooper's Chowhound

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Chowhound burgers
Home base: Baltimore, Maryland

Kooper's Chowhound is a Burgers food truck based in Baltimore, Maryland. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Baltimore, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the chowhound burgers. At Kooper's Chowhound, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck in a place the size of Baltimore becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Kooper's Chowhound is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Kooper's Chowhound serves Burgers stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Kooper's Chowhound's is easy to write: Burgers from Baltimore, Maryland, with chowhound burgers as the dish the city attached to its name.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Kooper's Chowhound matter to the places they feed. Home base Baltimore, Maryland; cuisine Burgers; signature: chowhound burgers. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
