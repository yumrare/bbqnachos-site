San Diego, California · Directory Entry

God Save the Cuisine

Cuisine: British
Signature: Fish and chips
Home base: San Diego, California

Some trucks chase trends; God Save the Cuisine chases its regulars. Based in San Diego, California, the British kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The fish and chips at God Save the Cuisine is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in San Diego, and God Save the Cuisine has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

God Save the Cuisine serves British stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

What makes God Save the Cuisine itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: British out of San Diego, with fish and chips as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: God Save the Cuisine serves San Diego its British, and the fish and chips is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
