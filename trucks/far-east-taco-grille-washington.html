Far East Taco Grille

Cuisine: Asian-Mexican
Signature: Far East tacos
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Out of Washington, District of Columbia, Far East Taco Grille serves Asian-Mexican to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

far east tacos: that is the signature at Far East Taco Grille, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Washington crosses town for.

In a scene as crowded as Washington's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Far East Taco Grille draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Asian-Mexican cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Far East Taco Grille leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Far East Taco Grille would still stand alone on its facts: a Asian-Mexican kitchen based in Washington, known first and last for far east tacos.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Far East Taco Grille of Washington holds its place on Asian-Mexican, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
