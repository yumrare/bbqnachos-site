Houston, Texas · Directory Entry

The Modular

Cuisine: Experimental
Signature: Trailer cuisine experiments
Home base: Houston, Texas

Ask around Houston where the Experimental cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to The Modular. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Texas, service after service.

The signature at The Modular is trailer cuisine experiments, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Houston crosses town for.

Competition in Houston is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and The Modular has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Experimental cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and The Modular leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

What makes The Modular itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Experimental out of Houston, with trailer cuisine experiments as the order its name is attached to.

The quarterly review will come back to The Modular the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
