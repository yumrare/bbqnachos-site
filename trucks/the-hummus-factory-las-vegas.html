Las Vegas, Nevada · Directory Entry

The Hummus Factory

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Hummus plates
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

The Hummus Factory rolls out of Las Vegas, Nevada, serving Middle Eastern to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at The Hummus Factory is famous for handing out is hummus plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Las Vegas, and The Hummus Factory has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Hummus Factory: the Middle Eastern is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No other truck in this record runs on The Hummus Factory's exact combination: Middle Eastern out of Las Vegas, with hummus plates as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: The Hummus Factory serves Las Vegas its Middle Eastern, and the hummus plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
