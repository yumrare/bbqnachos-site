Fort Collins, Colorado · Directory Entry

Umami Ninjas

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Ninja bowls
Home base: Fort Collins, Colorado

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Umami Ninjas of Fort Collins, Colorado is no exception. A Asian fusion kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Fort Collins the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Umami Ninjas is carried by its ninja bowls. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In a place the size of Fort Collins, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Umami Ninjas is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

What Umami Ninjas serves is Asian fusion stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Line up every truck in this record and Umami Ninjas still stands alone on its facts: a Asian fusion kitchen based in Fort Collins, known first and last for ninja bowls.

For the permanent record: Umami Ninjas serves Fort Collins its Asian fusion, and the ninja bowls is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
