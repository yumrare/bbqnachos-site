Piaggio on Wheels

Cuisine: Argentine
Signature: Chimichurri steak sandwiches
Home base: Irvine, California

In Irvine, California, the name Piaggio on Wheels means Argentine done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Piaggio on Wheels holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Ask at the Piaggio on Wheels window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: chimichurri steak sandwiches. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck in a place the size of Irvine becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Piaggio on Wheels is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Argentine was made for the truck form, and Piaggio on Wheels leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Line up every truck in this record and Piaggio on Wheels still stands alone on its facts: a Argentine kitchen based in Irvine, known first and last for chimichurri steak sandwiches.

For the permanent record: Piaggio on Wheels serves Irvine its Argentine, and the chimichurri steak sandwiches is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
