Don't Look Back Truck

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Don't look back tacos
Home base: Richmond, Virginia

Read the Richmond, Virginia pages and you will find Don't Look Back Truck: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Don't Look Back Truck is carried by its don't look back tacos. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Ask in Richmond where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Don't Look Back Truck's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Mexican was made for the truck form, and Don't Look Back Truck leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Don't Look Back Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Richmond as the stage, Mexican as the craft, and don't look back tacos as the headline act.

The quarterly review will come back to Don't Look Back Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
