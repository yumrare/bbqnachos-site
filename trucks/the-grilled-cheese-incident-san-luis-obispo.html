The Grilled Cheese Incident

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Incident melts
Home base: San Luis Obispo, California

Ask around San Luis Obispo where the Grilled cheese cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to The Grilled Cheese Incident. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in California, service after service.

Ask at the window what The Grilled Cheese Incident is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: incident melts. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Everybody knows everybody at a San Luis Obispo truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. The Grilled Cheese Incident thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

Grilled cheese cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and The Grilled Cheese Incident leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and The Grilled Cheese Incident's is easy to write: Grilled cheese from San Luis Obispo, California, with incident melts as the dish the city attached to its name.

Records like this one exist because trucks like The Grilled Cheese Incident matter to the places they feed. Home base San Luis Obispo, California; cuisine Grilled cheese; signature: incident melts — a small, complete portrait.
