Odd Duck

Cuisine: Farm-to-trailer
Signature: Wood-grilled seasonal plates
Home base: Austin, Texas

To eaters in Austin, Texas, Odd Duck simply means Farm-to-trailer done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Odd Duck is carried by its wood-grilled seasonal plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

In a scene as crowded as Austin's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Odd Duck draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Odd Duck. The Farm-to-trailer cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Odd Duck's is easy to write: Farm-to-trailer from Austin, Texas, with wood-grilled seasonal plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: Odd Duck serves Austin its Farm-to-trailer, and the wood-grilled seasonal plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
