The Kilted Kod

Cuisine: Fish and chips
Signature: Kilted fish suppers
Home base: Boise, Idaho

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Kilted Kod of Boise, Idaho is no exception. A Fish and chips kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Boise the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

kilted fish suppers: that is the signature at The Kilted Kod, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Boise crosses town for.

In Boise, a good truck is town news, and The Kilted Kod has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

At The Kilted Kod, Fish and chips is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Kilted Kod's writes itself: Fish and chips from Boise, Idaho, with kilted fish suppers as the dish the city attached to its name.

So the record stands: The Kilted Kod, of Boise, Idaho — Fish and chips by trade, kilted fish suppers by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
