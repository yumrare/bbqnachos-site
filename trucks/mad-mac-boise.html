Mad Mac

Cuisine: Mac and cheese
Signature: Mad mac bowls
Home base: Boise, Idaho

To eaters in Boise, Idaho, Mad Mac simply means Mac and cheese done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

If Mad Mac served nothing else, the record would read the same: mad mac bowls. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Boise; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Mad Mac has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

At Mad Mac the Mac and cheese is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Mad Mac's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Boise as the stage, Mac and cheese as the craft, and mad mac bowls as the headline act.

Mad Mac stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
