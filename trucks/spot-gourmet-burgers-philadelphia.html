Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Spot Gourmet Burgers

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: The Spot burger
Home base: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Spot Gourmet Burgers of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Burgers kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Spot Gourmet Burgers and go straight to the the spot burger. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A Philadelphia truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Spot Gourmet Burgers draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Everything on the Spot Gourmet Burgers menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Burgers here tastes focused.

No other truck in this record runs on Spot Gourmet Burgers's exact combination: Burgers out of Philadelphia, with the spot burger as the order its name is attached to.

Spot Gourmet Burgers is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
