Los Angeles, California · Featured Truck

Kogi BBQ

Cuisine: Korean-Mexican
Signature: Short rib taco
Home base: Los Angeles, California
Rolling since 2008

Kogi BBQ keeps its home base in Los Angeles, California, and cooks Korean-Mexican the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Kogi BBQ has been rolling since 2008. Widely credited with launching the modern gourmet food truck movement; chef Roy Choi's Korean-Mexican original.

The short rib taco at Kogi BBQ is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

In a scene as crowded as Los Angeles's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Kogi BBQ draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Kogi BBQ serves Korean-Mexican stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The featured tier is this directory's front row, and Kogi BBQ sits in it because Los Angeles put it there — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

Part of what keeps Kogi BBQ in the record is consistency — the signature tastes like itself every time, the welcome at the window is the same, and the truck shows up where it says it will. In the food truck world, that reliability is its own kind of fame.

Kogi BBQ's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Los Angeles as the stage, Korean-Mexican as the craft, and short rib taco as the headline act.

Featured entries in this directory are the trucks other trucks get measured against, and Kogi BBQ earned that standing in Los Angeles the only way it can be earned — year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

That is the entry for Kogi BBQ: Los Angeles's own, a Korean-Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
