Holler Smoke Wagon

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: River city smoked plates
Home base: Huntington, West Virginia

The entry for Huntington, West Virginia reads Holler Smoke Wagon: a Barbecue truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Holler Smoke Wagon it is river city smoked plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In a place the size of Huntington, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Holler Smoke Wagon is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Cooking Barbecue from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Holler Smoke Wagon works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The originality of Holler Smoke Wagon is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Huntington as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and river city smoked plates as the headline.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Holler Smoke Wagon of Huntington holds its place on Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
