St. Louis, Missouri · Directory Entry

2 Girls 4 Wheels

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Truck-made comfort plates
Home base: St. Louis, Missouri

Ask around St. Louis where the American comfort cooking is and somebody points to 2 Girls 4 Wheels before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Missouri, service after service, the only honest way there is.

If 2 Girls 4 Wheels served nothing else, the record would read the same: truck-made comfort plates. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Ask in St. Louis where to eat and the directions often end at a window: 2 Girls 4 Wheels's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

What 2 Girls 4 Wheels serves is American comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

2 Girls 4 Wheels does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — St. Louis, American comfort, truck-made comfort plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

When the quarterly review comes back to 2 Girls 4 Wheels, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
