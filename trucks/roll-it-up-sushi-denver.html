Roll It Up Sushi

Cuisine: Sushi
Signature: Rolled sushi plates
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Roll It Up Sushi is a Sushi food truck based in Denver, Colorado. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Denver, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the rolled sushi plates. At Roll It Up Sushi, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the Denver scene rewards, and it is the reputation Roll It Up Sushi carries — a stop locals give directions by.

What Roll It Up Sushi serves is Sushi stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Roll It Up Sushi's content is Sushi cooked in Denver, Colorado, and judged by one dish above the rest: rolled sushi plates.

Roll It Up Sushi is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps.
