Dover, Delaware · Directory Entry

Flavor'D

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: First State plates
Home base: Dover, Delaware

Flavor'D is a American comfort food truck based in Dover, Delaware. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Dover, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

What the window at Flavor'D is famous for handing out is first state plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

A truck in Dover answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Flavor'D thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

At Flavor'D, American comfort is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Flavor'D's is easy to write: American comfort from Dover, Delaware, with first state plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: Flavor'D serves Dover its American comfort, and the first state plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
