Corvallis, Oregon · Directory Entry

The Local Boyz

Cuisine: Hawaiian
Signature: Local boyz plates
Home base: Corvallis, Oregon

Home base Corvallis, Oregon; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. The Local Boyz cooks Hawaiian the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

The signature at The Local Boyz is local boyz plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Corvallis crosses town for.

In a place the size of Corvallis, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what The Local Boyz is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

The kitchen at The Local Boyz treats Hawaiian as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Local Boyz's writes itself: Hawaiian from Corvallis, Oregon, with local boyz plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

The Local Boyz is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
