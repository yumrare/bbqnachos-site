Tacopocolypse

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Vegan and classic tacos
Home base: Des Moines, Iowa

Tacopocolypse never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Mexican kitchen out of Des Moines, Iowa runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the Tacopocolypse window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: vegan and classic tacos. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck in Des Moines answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Tacopocolypse thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Tacopocolypse cooks like it knows it: Mexican with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

The originality of Tacopocolypse is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Des Moines as the stage, Mexican as the craft, and vegan and classic tacos as the headline.

When the quarterly review comes back to Tacopocolypse, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
