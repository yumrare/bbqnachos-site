Los Angeles, California · Directory Entry

Ta Bom

Cuisine: Brazilian
Signature: Coxinha and pastel
Home base: Los Angeles, California

Ta Bom keeps its home base in Los Angeles, California, and cooks Brazilian the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Ta Bom it is coxinha and pastel. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Los Angeles counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Ta Bom is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

Brazilian cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Ta Bom leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Ta Bom's content is Brazilian cooked in Los Angeles, California, and judged by one dish above the rest: coxinha and pastel.

That is the entry for Ta Bom: Los Angeles's own, a Brazilian truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
