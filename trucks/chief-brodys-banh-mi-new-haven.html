New Haven, Connecticut · Directory Entry

Chief Brody's Banh Mi

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Banh mi sandwiches
Home base: New Haven, Connecticut

Ask around New Haven where the Vietnamese cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Chief Brody's Banh Mi. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Connecticut, service after service.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the banh mi sandwiches. At Chief Brody's Banh Mi, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

What Chief Brody's Banh Mi gives New Haven is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

Everything on the Chief Brody's Banh Mi menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Vietnamese here tastes focused.

No other truck in this record runs on Chief Brody's Banh Mi's exact combination: Vietnamese out of New Haven, with banh mi sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

The quarterly review will come back to Chief Brody's Banh Mi the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
