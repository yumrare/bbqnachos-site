The Crafthouse Gastrotruck

Cuisine: Gastropub
Signature: Hub City burgers
Home base: Lubbock, Texas

Ask around Lubbock where the Gastropub cooking is and somebody points to The Crafthouse Gastrotruck before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Texas, service after service, the only honest way there is.

The hub city burgers at The Crafthouse Gastrotruck is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A good truck is town news in Lubbock, and The Crafthouse Gastrotruck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and The Crafthouse Gastrotruck cooks like it knows it: Gastropub with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Crafthouse Gastrotruck would still stand alone on its facts: a Gastropub kitchen based in Lubbock, known first and last for hub city burgers.

That is the entry for The Crafthouse Gastrotruck — Lubbock's own, a Gastropub truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
