The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke

Cuisine: American
Signature: Seacoast plates
Home base: Portsmouth, New Hampshire

Out of Portsmouth, New Hampshire, The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke serves American to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the seacoast plates. At The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck in a place the size of Portsmouth becomes a landmark with a schedule, and The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke. The American cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The originality of The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Portsmouth as the stage, American as the craft, and seacoast plates as the headline.

The Food Truck at Strawbery Banke is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
