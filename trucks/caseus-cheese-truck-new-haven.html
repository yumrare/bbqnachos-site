Caseus Cheese Truck

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Caseus classic melt
Home base: New Haven, Connecticut

Caseus Cheese Truck holds its home base in New Haven, Connecticut and serves Grilled cheese from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Caseus Cheese Truck it is caseus classic melt. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

New Haven eats above its weight because of trucks like Caseus Cheese Truck. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Cooking Grilled cheese from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Caseus Cheese Truck works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Caseus Cheese Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — New Haven, Grilled cheese, caseus classic melt — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Caseus Cheese Truck — New Haven's own, a Grilled cheese truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
