Kansas City, Missouri · Directory Entry

Cheesy Street

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Kansas City melts
Home base: Kansas City, Missouri

Some trucks chase trends; Cheesy Street chases its regulars. Based in Kansas City, Missouri, the Grilled cheese kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the window what Cheesy Street is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: kansas city melts. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

In Kansas City, a good truck is town news, and Cheesy Street has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Cheesy Street. The Grilled cheese cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Cheesy Street's content is Grilled cheese cooked in Kansas City, Missouri, and judged by one dish above the rest: kansas city melts.

The quarterly review will come back to Cheesy Street the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
