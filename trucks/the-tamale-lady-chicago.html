The Tamale Lady

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Virginia's tamales
Home base: Chicago, Illinois

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Tamale Lady of Chicago, Illinois is no exception. A Mexican kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Chicago the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the virginia's tamales at The Tamale Lady lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

In a scene as crowded as Chicago's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and The Tamale Lady draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Speed is part of the recipe at The Tamale Lady — Mexican cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Tamale Lady's writes itself: Mexican from Chicago, Illinois, with virginia's tamales as the dish the city attached to its name.

Records like this one exist because trucks like The Tamale Lady matter to the places they feed. Home base Chicago, Illinois; cuisine Mexican; signature: virginia's tamales — a small, complete portrait.
