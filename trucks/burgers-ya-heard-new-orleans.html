New Orleans, Louisiana · Directory Entry

Burgers Ya Heard

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Smash burgers
Home base: New Orleans, Louisiana

Burgers Ya Heard of New Orleans, Louisiana earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Burgers kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

If Burgers Ya Heard served nothing else, the record would read the same: smash burgers. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

New Orleans counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Burgers Ya Heard is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

At Burgers Ya Heard, Burgers is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Burgers Ya Heard would still stand alone on its facts: a Burgers kitchen based in New Orleans, known first and last for smash burgers.

For the permanent record: Burgers Ya Heard serves New Orleans its Burgers, and the smash burgers is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
