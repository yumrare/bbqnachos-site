Heavenly Donut Truck

Cuisine: Donuts
Signature: Heavenly donuts
Home base: Birmingham, Alabama

Heavenly Donut Truck is a Donuts food truck based in Birmingham, Alabama. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Birmingham, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the window what Heavenly Donut Truck is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: heavenly donuts. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A good truck is town news in Birmingham, and Heavenly Donut Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Space on the Heavenly Donut Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Donuts here tastes so focused.

Heavenly Donut Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Birmingham as the stage, Donuts as the craft, and heavenly donuts as the headline act.

For the permanent record: Heavenly Donut Truck serves Birmingham its Donuts, and the heavenly donuts is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
