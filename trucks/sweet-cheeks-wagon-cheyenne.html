Sweet Cheeks Wagon

Cuisine: Desserts
Signature: Frontier sweets
Home base: Cheyenne, Wyoming

In Cheyenne, Wyoming, the name Sweet Cheeks Wagon means Desserts done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Sweet Cheeks Wagon holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

frontier sweets: that is the signature at Sweet Cheeks Wagon, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Cheyenne crosses town for.

Ask in Cheyenne where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Sweet Cheeks Wagon's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

At Sweet Cheeks Wagon the Desserts is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Sweet Cheeks Wagon's is easy to write: Desserts from Cheyenne, Wyoming, with frontier sweets as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Sweet Cheeks Wagon — Cheyenne's own, a Desserts truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
