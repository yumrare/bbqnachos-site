Appalachian Trail Eats

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Trail town plates
Home base: Harpers Ferry, West Virginia

Appalachian Trail Eats is a American comfort food truck based in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Harpers Ferry, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Appalachian Trail Eats is carried by its trail town plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Everybody knows everybody at a Harpers Ferry truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Appalachian Trail Eats thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

Everything on the Appalachian Trail Eats menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the American comfort here tastes focused.

What makes Appalachian Trail Eats itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: American comfort out of Harpers Ferry, with trail town plates as the order its name is attached to.

The entry sits in the Harpers Ferry pages, filed under West Virginia: Appalachian Trail Eats. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
