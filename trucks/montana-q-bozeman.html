Bozeman, Montana · Directory Entry

Montana Q

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Big Sky brisket
Home base: Bozeman, Montana

Montana Q is a Barbecue food truck based in Bozeman, Montana. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Bozeman, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the window what Montana Q is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: big sky brisket. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Trucks like Montana Q are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Bozeman may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Speed is part of the recipe at Montana Q — Barbecue cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Montana Q's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Bozeman as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and big sky brisket as the headline act.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Montana Q matter to the places they feed. Home base Bozeman, Montana; cuisine Barbecue; signature: big sky brisket — a small, complete portrait.
