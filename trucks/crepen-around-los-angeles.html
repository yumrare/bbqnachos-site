Crepen' Around

Cuisine: Crepes
Signature: Crepes around town
Home base: Los Angeles, California

In Los Angeles, California, the name Crepen' Around means Crepes done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Crepen' Around holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The famous hand-out at the Crepen' Around window is crepes around town. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Los Angeles has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and Crepen' Around is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

The kitchen at Crepen' Around treats Crepes as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Crepen' Around's is easy to write: Crepes from Los Angeles, California, with crepes around town as the dish the city attached to its name.
