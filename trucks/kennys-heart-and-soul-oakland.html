Kenny's Heart and Soul

Cuisine: Soul food
Signature: Heart and soul plates
Home base: Oakland, California

Kenny's Heart and Soul is a Soul food food truck based in Oakland, California. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Oakland, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the window what Kenny's Heart and Soul is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: heart and soul plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A truck survives in Oakland by being somebody's favorite, and Kenny's Heart and Soul is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Space on the Kenny's Heart and Soul menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Soul food here tastes so focused.

Kenny's Heart and Soul does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Oakland, Soul food, heart and soul plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Kenny's Heart and Soul serves Oakland its Soul food, and the heart and soul plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
