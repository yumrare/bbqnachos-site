Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Reindeer and rattlesnake dogs
Home base: Denver, Colorado
Rolling since 2005

Some trucks chase trends; Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs chases its regulars. Based in Denver, Colorado, the Hot dogs kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs has been rolling since 2005. Jim Pittenger's cart redefined the hot dog with reindeer, rattlesnake, and elk sausages.

The signature at Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs is reindeer and rattlesnake dogs, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Denver crosses town for.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the Denver scene rewards, and it is the reputation Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs carries — a stop locals give directions by.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs cooks like it knows it: Hot dogs with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

The featured tier holds the trucks other trucks get measured against. Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs earned its standing the only way it can be earned: year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs runs on that kind of memory.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs would still stand alone on its facts: a Hot dogs kitchen based in Denver, known first and last for reindeer and rattlesnake dogs.

Start with a handful of names if you want to understand Denver's truck scene; Biker Jim's Gourmet Dogs is on the first page. Its influence shows in the trucks that followed and the routes eaters plan around its window.
