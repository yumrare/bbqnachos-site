Battle Born Food Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Battle Born plates
Home base: Reno, Nevada

Battle Born Food Truck never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The American comfort kitchen out of Reno, Nevada runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the battle born plates. At Battle Born Food Truck, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Reno is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Battle Born Food Truck has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Reno gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Battle Born Food Truck. The American comfort cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Battle Born Food Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Reno, American comfort, battle born plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Battle Born Food Truck serves Reno its American comfort, and the battle born plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
