Gulf Shores Grill

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Gulf plates
Home base: Gulf Shores, Alabama

Out of Gulf Shores, Alabama, Gulf Shores Grill serves Seafood to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Ask at the window what Gulf Shores Grill is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: gulf plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Ask in Gulf Shores where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Gulf Shores Grill's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

At Gulf Shores Grill, Seafood is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Gulf Shores Grill would still stand alone on its facts: a Seafood kitchen based in Gulf Shores, known first and last for gulf plates.

For the permanent record: Gulf Shores Grill serves Gulf Shores its Seafood, and the gulf plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
