BowTie BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Lowcountry smoked meats
Home base: Savannah, Georgia

BowTie BBQ never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Barbecue kitchen out of Savannah, Georgia runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and BowTie BBQ is carried by its lowcountry smoked meats. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Everybody knows everybody at a Savannah truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. BowTie BBQ thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

Space on the BowTie BBQ menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Barbecue here tastes so focused.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and BowTie BBQ's content is specific — Barbecue cooked in Savannah, Georgia, judged by one dish above the rest: lowcountry smoked meats.

So the record stands for BowTie BBQ of Savannah, Georgia: Barbecue by trade, lowcountry smoked meats by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
