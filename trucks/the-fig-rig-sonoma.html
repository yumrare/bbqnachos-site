Sonoma, California · Directory Entry

The Fig Rig

Cuisine: California French
Signature: Fig rig plates
Home base: Sonoma, California

The Fig Rig keeps its home base in Sonoma, California, and cooks California French the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the fig rig plates at The Fig Rig lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Trucks like The Fig Rig are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Sonoma may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Fig Rig. The California French cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The Fig Rig does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Sonoma, California French, fig rig plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: The Fig Rig serves Sonoma its California French, and the fig rig plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
