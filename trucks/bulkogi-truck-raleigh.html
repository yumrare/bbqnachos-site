Bulkogi Truck

Cuisine: Korean barbecue
Signature: Bulkogi bowls
Home base: Raleigh, North Carolina

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Bulkogi Truck of Raleigh, North Carolina is no exception. A Korean barbecue kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Raleigh the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

The bulkogi bowls at Bulkogi Truck is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A truck in Raleigh answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Bulkogi Truck thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

The kitchen at Bulkogi Truck treats Korean barbecue as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No theme song, no mascot: Bulkogi Truck's identity is written in three facts — Raleigh, Korean barbecue, bulkogi bowls — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Bulkogi Truck — Raleigh's own, a Korean barbecue truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
