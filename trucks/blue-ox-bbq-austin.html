Blue Ox BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Blue ox brisket
Home base: Austin, Texas

Blue Ox BBQ holds its home base in Austin, Texas and serves Barbecue from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the window what Blue Ox BBQ is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: blue ox brisket. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Austin, and Blue Ox BBQ has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Cooking Barbecue from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Blue Ox BBQ works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Blue Ox BBQ does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Austin, Barbecue, blue ox brisket — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Blue Ox BBQ serves Austin its Barbecue, and the blue ox brisket is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
