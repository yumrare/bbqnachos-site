San Francisco, California · Directory Entry

The Boneyard

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Boneyard smoked meats
Home base: San Francisco, California

In San Francisco, California, the name The Boneyard means Barbecue done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. The Boneyard holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

What the window at The Boneyard is famous for handing out is boneyard smoked meats — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the San Francisco scene rewards, and it is the reputation The Boneyard carries — a stop locals give directions by.

Everything on the The Boneyard menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Barbecue here tastes focused.

The originality of The Boneyard is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: San Francisco as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and boneyard smoked meats as the headline.

So the record stands: The Boneyard, of San Francisco, California — Barbecue by trade, boneyard smoked meats by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
