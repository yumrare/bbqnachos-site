The Denver Cupcake Truck

Cuisine: Cupcakes
Signature: Denver cupcakes
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Ask around Denver where the Cupcakes cooking is and somebody points to The Denver Cupcake Truck before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Colorado, service after service, the only honest way there is.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and The Denver Cupcake Truck is carried by its denver cupcakes. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck in Denver is a neighborhood before it is a business — The Denver Cupcake Truck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Denver Cupcake Truck. The Cupcakes cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Denver Cupcake Truck would still stand alone on its facts: a Cupcakes kitchen based in Denver, known first and last for denver cupcakes.

The quarterly review will come back to The Denver Cupcake Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
