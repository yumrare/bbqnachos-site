DC Slices

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Jumbo slices
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

To eaters in Washington, District of Columbia, DC Slices simply means Pizza done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The signature at DC Slices is jumbo slices, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Washington crosses town for.

The Washington scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation DC Slices carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

DC Slices cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and DC Slices's writes itself: Pizza from Washington, District of Columbia, with jumbo slices as the dish the city attached to its name.

The quarterly review will come back to DC Slices the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
