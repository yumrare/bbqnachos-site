Irvine, California · Featured Truck

The Lime Truck

Cuisine: California fusion
Signature: Lime ceviche and ahi nachos
Home base: Irvine, California
Rolling since 2010

The Lime Truck rolls out of Irvine, California, serving California fusion to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The Lime Truck has been rolling since 2010. Winners of The Great Food Truck Race season two, from Orange County.

If The Lime Truck could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: lime ceviche and ahi nachos. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Ask in Irvine where to eat and the directions often end at a window: The Lime Truck's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

There is a discipline to California fusion cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Lime Truck works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points. The Lime Truck is one for Irvine — the name the city reaches for when it explains its truck scene to a stranger.

Regulars will tell you the real signature is the rhythm: the truck posted up, the line forming, the window sliding open. The Lime Truck has that rhythm down to a ritual, and rituals are what turn a good truck into a beloved one.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Lime Truck's writes itself: California fusion from Irvine, California, with lime ceviche and ahi nachos as the dish the city attached to its name.

To understand Irvine's truck scene, you start with a handful of names, and The Lime Truck is on that first page. Its influence shows up in the trucks that came after it and in the eaters who plan their routes around its window.

So the record stands for The Lime Truck of Irvine, California: California fusion by trade, lime ceviche and ahi nachos by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
