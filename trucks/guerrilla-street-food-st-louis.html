St. Louis, Missouri · Directory Entry

Guerrilla Street Food

Cuisine: Filipino
Signature: The Flying Pig (sisig over rice)
Home base: St. Louis, Missouri

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Guerrilla Street Food of St. Louis, Missouri is no exception. A Filipino kitchen on wheels, it built its name in St. Louis the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the the flying pig (sisig over rice) at Guerrilla Street Food lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Ask in St. Louis where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Guerrilla Street Food's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Filipino was made for the truck form, and Guerrilla Street Food leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The originality of Guerrilla Street Food is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: St. Louis as the stage, Filipino as the craft, and the flying pig (sisig over rice) as the headline.

For the permanent record: Guerrilla Street Food serves St. Louis its Filipino, and the the flying pig (sisig over rice) is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
