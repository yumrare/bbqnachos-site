Big Green Q

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Green Q smoked meats
Home base: Omaha, Nebraska

To eaters in Omaha, Nebraska, Big Green Q simply means Barbecue done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

green q smoked meats: that is the signature at Big Green Q, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Omaha crosses town for.

Everybody knows everybody at a Omaha truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Big Green Q thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

At Big Green Q the Barbecue is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Line up every truck in this record and Big Green Q still stands alone on its facts: a Barbecue kitchen based in Omaha, known first and last for green q smoked meats.

That is the entry for Big Green Q: Omaha's own, a Barbecue truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
