The Funky Taco

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Funky tacos
Home base: Boise, Idaho

Out of Boise, Idaho, The Funky Taco serves Mexican to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The funky tacos at The Funky Taco is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A good truck is town news in Boise, and The Funky Taco has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Everything on the The Funky Taco menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Mexican here tastes focused.

Line up every truck in this record and The Funky Taco still stands alone on its facts: a Mexican kitchen based in Boise, known first and last for funky tacos.

When the quarterly review comes back to The Funky Taco, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
