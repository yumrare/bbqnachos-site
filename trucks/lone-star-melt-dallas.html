Lone Star Melt

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Lone Star melts
Home base: Dallas, Texas

Lone Star Melt is a Grilled cheese food truck based in Dallas, Texas. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Dallas, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the lone star melts at Lone Star Melt lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Competition in Dallas is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Lone Star Melt has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Lone Star Melt. The Grilled cheese cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Lone Star Melt's content is Grilled cheese cooked in Dallas, Texas, and judged by one dish above the rest: lone star melts.

That is the entry for Lone Star Melt — Dallas's own, a Grilled cheese truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
