Little Rock, Arkansas · Directory Entry

Rollin' Pig

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Rollin' pig plates
Home base: Little Rock, Arkansas

Home base Little Rock, Arkansas; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Rollin' Pig cooks Barbecue the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

What the window at Rollin' Pig is famous for handing out is rollin' pig plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

A truck in Little Rock answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Rollin' Pig thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

There is a discipline to Barbecue cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Rollin' Pig works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Rollin' Pig does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Little Rock, Barbecue, rollin' pig plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Rollin' Pig: Little Rock's own, a Barbecue truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
