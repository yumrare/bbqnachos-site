Breaking Burrito

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Breaking burritos
Home base: Sioux Falls, South Dakota

Breaking Burrito holds its home base in Sioux Falls, South Dakota and serves Mexican from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Breaking Burrito is carried by its breaking burritos. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck in a place the size of Sioux Falls becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Breaking Burrito is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Speed is part of the recipe at Breaking Burrito — Mexican cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Breaking Burrito's content is Mexican cooked in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, and judged by one dish above the rest: breaking burritos.

That is the entry for Breaking Burrito: Sioux Falls's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
