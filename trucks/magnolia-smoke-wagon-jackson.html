Jackson, Mississippi · Directory Entry

Magnolia Smoke Wagon

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Magnolia smoked plates
Home base: Jackson, Mississippi

Ask around Jackson where the Barbecue cooking is and somebody points to Magnolia Smoke Wagon before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Mississippi, service after service, the only honest way there is.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Magnolia Smoke Wagon is carried by its magnolia smoked plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Ask in Jackson where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Magnolia Smoke Wagon's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Magnolia Smoke Wagon cooks like it knows it: Barbecue with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

What makes Magnolia Smoke Wagon itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Barbecue out of Jackson, with magnolia smoked plates as the order its name is attached to.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Magnolia Smoke Wagon matter to the places they feed. Home base Jackson, Mississippi; cuisine Barbecue; signature: magnolia smoked plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
