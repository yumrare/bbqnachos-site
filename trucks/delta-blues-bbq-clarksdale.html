Clarksdale, Mississippi · Directory Entry

Delta Blues BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Delta smoked plates
Home base: Clarksdale, Mississippi

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Delta Blues BBQ of Clarksdale, Mississippi is no exception. A Barbecue kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Clarksdale the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Delta Blues BBQ the dish is delta smoked plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Ask in Clarksdale where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Delta Blues BBQ's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

At Delta Blues BBQ the Barbecue is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Delta Blues BBQ would still stand alone on its facts: a Barbecue kitchen based in Clarksdale, known first and last for delta smoked plates.

For the permanent record: Delta Blues BBQ serves Clarksdale its Barbecue, and the delta smoked plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
