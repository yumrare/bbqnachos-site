Macky's Sweet Shrimp

Cuisine: Shrimp
Signature: Sweet shrimp plates
Home base: Kahuku, Hawaii

Home base Kahuku, Hawaii; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Macky's Sweet Shrimp cooks Shrimp the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

sweet shrimp plates: that is the signature at Macky's Sweet Shrimp, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Kahuku crosses town for.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Kahuku; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Macky's Sweet Shrimp has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Macky's Sweet Shrimp: the Shrimp is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Macky's Sweet Shrimp would still stand alone on its facts: a Shrimp kitchen based in Kahuku, known first and last for sweet shrimp plates.

So the record stands: Macky's Sweet Shrimp, of Kahuku, Hawaii — Shrimp by trade, sweet shrimp plates by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
