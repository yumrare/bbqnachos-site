Sioux Falls, South Dakota · Directory Entry

Swamp Daddy's

Cuisine: Cajun
Signature: Swamp daddy plates
Home base: Sioux Falls, South Dakota

Out of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, Swamp Daddy's serves Cajun to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the Swamp Daddy's window is swamp daddy plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Sioux Falls eats above its weight because of trucks like Swamp Daddy's. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

There is a discipline to Cajun cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Swamp Daddy's works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

No other truck in this record runs on Swamp Daddy's's exact combination: Cajun out of Sioux Falls, with swamp daddy plates as the order its name is attached to.

Swamp Daddy's stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
