Charleston, South Carolina · Directory Entry

Outta My Huevos

Cuisine: Breakfast
Signature: Huevos plates
Home base: Charleston, South Carolina

Ask around Charleston where the Breakfast cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Outta My Huevos. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in South Carolina, service after service.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Outta My Huevos it is huevos plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Charleston eats above its weight because of trucks like Outta My Huevos. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

At Outta My Huevos the Breakfast is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

No other truck in this record runs on Outta My Huevos's exact combination: Breakfast out of Charleston, with huevos plates as the order its name is attached to.

The quarterly review will come back to Outta My Huevos the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
