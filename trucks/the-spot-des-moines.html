Des Moines, Iowa · Directory Entry

The Spot

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: The Spot smash burger
Home base: Des Moines, Iowa

The Spot rolls out of Des Moines, Iowa, serving Burgers to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the the spot smash burger at The Spot lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

What The Spot gives Des Moines is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

At The Spot, Burgers is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Spot would still stand alone on its facts: a Burgers kitchen based in Des Moines, known first and last for the spot smash burger.

Records like this one exist because trucks like The Spot matter to the places they feed. Home base Des Moines, Iowa; cuisine Burgers; signature: the spot smash burger — a small, complete portrait.
