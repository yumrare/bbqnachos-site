Austin, Texas · Featured Truck

Chi'Lantro

Cuisine: Korean-Mexican
Signature: Kimchi fries
Home base: Austin, Texas
Rolling since 2010

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Chi'Lantro of Austin, Texas is no exception. A Korean-Mexican kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Austin the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Chi'Lantro has been rolling since 2010. Home of the original kimchi fries; a Shark Tank appearance carried the Austin truck national.

The famous hand-out at the Chi'Lantro window is kimchi fries. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A Austin truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Chi'Lantro draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Space on the Chi'Lantro menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Korean-Mexican here tastes so focused.

Featured entries in this directory are the trucks other trucks get measured against, and Chi'Lantro earned that standing in Austin the only way it can be earned — year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Chi'Lantro runs on that kind of memory.

The originality of Chi'Lantro is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Austin as the stage, Korean-Mexican as the craft, and kimchi fries as the headline.

There is a reason Chi'Lantro sits in the featured tier of this record. Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points — the name a city reaches for when it explains its food truck scene to a stranger. Chi'Lantro is one of those names for Austin.

Start with a handful of names if you want to understand Austin's truck scene; Chi'Lantro is on the first page. Its influence shows in the trucks that followed and the routes eaters plan around its window.

So the record stands: Chi'Lantro, of Austin, Texas — Korean-Mexican by trade, kimchi fries by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
