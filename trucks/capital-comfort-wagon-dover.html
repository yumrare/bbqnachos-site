Capital Comfort Wagon

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Capital comfort plates
Home base: Dover, Delaware

Capital Comfort Wagon of Dover, Delaware earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A American comfort kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the capital comfort plates at Capital Comfort Wagon lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

What Capital Comfort Wagon gives Dover is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

At Capital Comfort Wagon the American comfort is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Capital Comfort Wagon would still stand alone on its facts: a American comfort kitchen based in Dover, known first and last for capital comfort plates.

Capital Comfort Wagon stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
