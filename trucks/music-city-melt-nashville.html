Music City Melt

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Honky-tonk melts
Home base: Nashville, Tennessee

Music City Melt is a Grilled cheese food truck based in Nashville, Tennessee. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Nashville, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the honky-tonk melts. At Music City Melt, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

The Nashville scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation Music City Melt carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

Grilled cheese cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Music City Melt leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Music City Melt does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Nashville, Grilled cheese, honky-tonk melts — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Music City Melt serves Nashville its Grilled cheese, and the honky-tonk melts is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
