Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Heibeck's dogs
Home base: Georgetown, Connecticut

Heibeck's Stand of Georgetown, Connecticut earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Hot dogs kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.
