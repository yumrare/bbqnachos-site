Honolulu, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Blue Water Shrimp

Cuisine: Shrimp
Signature: Blue water shrimp plates
Home base: Honolulu, Hawaii

Read the Honolulu, Hawaii pages and you will find Blue Water Shrimp: a Shrimp truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Blue Water Shrimp is carried by its blue water shrimp plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

In a place the size of Honolulu, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Blue Water Shrimp is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Blue Water Shrimp serves Shrimp stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The originality of Blue Water Shrimp is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Honolulu as the stage, Shrimp as the craft, and blue water shrimp plates as the headline.

When the quarterly review comes back to Blue Water Shrimp, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
