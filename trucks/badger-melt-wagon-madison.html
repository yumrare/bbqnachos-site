Badger Melt Wagon

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Badger melts
Home base: Madison, Wisconsin

Badger Melt Wagon holds its home base in Madison, Wisconsin and serves Grilled cheese from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The badger melts at Badger Melt Wagon is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Ask in Madison where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Badger Melt Wagon's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Badger Melt Wagon serves Grilled cheese stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The originality of Badger Melt Wagon is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Madison as the stage, Grilled cheese as the craft, and badger melts as the headline.
