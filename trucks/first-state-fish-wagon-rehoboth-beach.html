First State Fish Wagon

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: First State fish plates
Home base: Rehoboth Beach, Delaware

Read the Rehoboth Beach, Delaware pages and you will find First State Fish Wagon: a Seafood truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Ask at the First State Fish Wagon window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: first state fish plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

In a place the size of Rehoboth Beach, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what First State Fish Wagon is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

First State Fish Wagon serves Seafood stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and First State Fish Wagon's content is specific — Seafood cooked in Rehoboth Beach, Delaware, judged by one dish above the rest: first state fish plates.
