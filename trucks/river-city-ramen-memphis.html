River City Ramen

Cuisine: Ramen
Signature: River City ramen bowls
Home base: Memphis, Tennessee

In Memphis, Tennessee, the name River City Ramen means Ramen done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. River City Ramen holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and River City Ramen is carried by its river city ramen bowls. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck in a place the size of Memphis becomes a landmark with a schedule, and River City Ramen is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

There is a discipline to Ramen cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. River City Ramen works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and River City Ramen's writes itself: Ramen from Memphis, Tennessee, with river city ramen bowls as the dish the city attached to its name.

Records like this one exist because trucks like River City Ramen matter to the places they feed. Home base Memphis, Tennessee; cuisine Ramen; signature: river city ramen bowls — a small, complete portrait.
