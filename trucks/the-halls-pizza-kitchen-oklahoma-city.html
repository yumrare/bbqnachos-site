The Hall's Pizza Kitchen

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Wood-fired truck pies
Home base: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

The Hall's Pizza Kitchen rolls out of Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, serving Pizza to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the wood-fired truck pies. At The Hall's Pizza Kitchen, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

In Oklahoma City, a good truck is town news, and The Hall's Pizza Kitchen has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

There is a discipline to Pizza cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Hall's Pizza Kitchen works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Line up every truck in this record and The Hall's Pizza Kitchen still stands alone on its facts: a Pizza kitchen based in Oklahoma City, known first and last for wood-fired truck pies.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. The Hall's Pizza Kitchen of Oklahoma City holds its place on Pizza, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
