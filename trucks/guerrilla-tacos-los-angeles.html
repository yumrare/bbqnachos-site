Los Angeles, California · Featured Truck

Guerrilla Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Sweet potato taco
Home base: Los Angeles, California

Guerrilla Tacos rolls out of Los Angeles, California, serving Mexican to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the sweet potato taco. At Guerrilla Tacos, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Survival in Los Angeles means being somebody's favorite, and Guerrilla Tacos is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Mexican from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Guerrilla Tacos works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

There is a reason Guerrilla Tacos sits in the featured tier of this record. Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points — the name a city reaches for when it explains its food truck scene to a stranger. Guerrilla Tacos is one of those names for Los Angeles.

A stop at Guerrilla Tacos has a small ceremony to it: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers about to agree with you. Rituals outlive meals.

Part of what keeps Guerrilla Tacos in the record is consistency — the signature tastes like itself every time, the welcome at the window is the same, and the truck shows up where it says it will. In the food truck world, that reliability is its own kind of fame.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Guerrilla Tacos's is easy to write: Mexican from Los Angeles, California, with sweet potato taco as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Guerrilla Tacos — Los Angeles's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
