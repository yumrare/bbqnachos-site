Denver, Colorado · Directory Entry

Basic Kneads

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Wood-fired pies
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Ask around Denver where the Pizza cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Basic Kneads. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Colorado, service after service.

If Basic Kneads served nothing else, the record would read the same: wood-fired pies. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

What Basic Kneads understands about Denver is that a truck here is a neighborhood before it is a business. It feeds offices at noon and families at dusk, and both crowds claim it as their own.

There is a discipline to Pizza cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Basic Kneads works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Line up every truck in this record and Basic Kneads still stands alone on its facts: a Pizza kitchen based in Denver, known first and last for wood-fired pies.

The quarterly review will come back to Basic Kneads the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
