The Chuck Truck

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Chuck burgers
Home base: Greenville, South Carolina

The Chuck Truck is a Burgers food truck based in Greenville, South Carolina. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Greenville, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The chuck burgers at The Chuck Truck is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A truck in Greenville answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and The Chuck Truck thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Cooking Burgers from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and The Chuck Truck works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Line up every truck in this record and The Chuck Truck still stands alone on its facts: a Burgers kitchen based in Greenville, known first and last for chuck burgers.

The quarterly review will come back to The Chuck Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
