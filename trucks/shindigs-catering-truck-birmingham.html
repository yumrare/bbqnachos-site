Birmingham, Alabama · Directory Entry

Shindigs Catering Truck

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Shindigs plates
Home base: Birmingham, Alabama

Read the Birmingham, Alabama pages and you will find Shindigs Catering Truck: a Asian fusion truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Shindigs Catering Truck it is shindigs plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In Birmingham, a good truck is town news, and Shindigs Catering Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Everything on the Shindigs Catering Truck menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Asian fusion here tastes focused.

Shindigs Catering Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Birmingham as the stage, Asian fusion as the craft, and shindigs plates as the headline act.
