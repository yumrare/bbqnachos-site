Kansas City, Missouri · Directory Entry

Jerusalem Café Truck

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Falafel and shawarma
Home base: Kansas City, Missouri

Jerusalem Café Truck rolls out of Kansas City, Missouri, serving Middle Eastern to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

If Jerusalem Café Truck served nothing else, the record would read the same: falafel and shawarma. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Kansas City is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Jerusalem Café Truck has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Kansas City gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Jerusalem Café Truck cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Jerusalem Café Truck's is easy to write: Middle Eastern from Kansas City, Missouri, with falafel and shawarma as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Jerusalem Café Truck: Kansas City's own, a Middle Eastern truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
