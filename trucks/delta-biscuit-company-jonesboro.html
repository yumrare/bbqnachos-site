Delta Biscuit Company

Cuisine: Biscuits
Signature: Biscuit sandwiches
Home base: Jonesboro, Arkansas

Delta Biscuit Company holds its home base in Jonesboro, Arkansas and serves Biscuits from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The signature at Delta Biscuit Company is biscuit sandwiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Jonesboro crosses town for.

What Delta Biscuit Company gives Jonesboro is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

Space on the Delta Biscuit Company menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Biscuits here tastes so focused.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Delta Biscuit Company's writes itself: Biscuits from Jonesboro, Arkansas, with biscuit sandwiches as the dish the city attached to its name.

Delta Biscuit Company is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
