Viva La Waffle

Cuisine: Waffles
Signature: Viva waffle sandwiches
Home base: Lafayette, Louisiana

Viva La Waffle holds its home base in Lafayette, Louisiana and serves Waffles from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The famous hand-out at the Viva La Waffle window is viva waffle sandwiches. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A truck in Lafayette answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Viva La Waffle thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Viva La Waffle serves Waffles stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Viva La Waffle's content is Waffles cooked in Lafayette, Louisiana, and judged by one dish above the rest: viva waffle sandwiches.

That is the entry for Viva La Waffle — Lafayette's own, a Waffles truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
