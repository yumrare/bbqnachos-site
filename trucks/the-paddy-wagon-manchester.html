Manchester, New Hampshire · Directory Entry

The Paddy Wagon

Cuisine: Irish
Signature: Paddy plates
Home base: Manchester, New Hampshire

The Paddy Wagon never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Irish kitchen out of Manchester, New Hampshire runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

If The Paddy Wagon served nothing else, the record would read the same: paddy plates. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Manchester eats above its weight because of trucks like The Paddy Wagon. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

The Paddy Wagon cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

The Paddy Wagon does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Manchester, Irish, paddy plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: The Paddy Wagon serves Manchester its Irish, and the paddy plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
