Hoangie's Banh Mi

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Classic banh mi
Home base: San Diego, California

Hoangie's Banh Mi of San Diego, California earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Vietnamese kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Ask at the window what Hoangie's Banh Mi is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: classic banh mi. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Survival in San Diego means being somebody's favorite, and Hoangie's Banh Mi is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Vietnamese from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Hoangie's Banh Mi works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Hoangie's Banh Mi's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: San Diego as the stage, Vietnamese as the craft, and classic banh mi as the headline act.

Hoangie's Banh Mi is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps.
