Smoky Mountain Smoke

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Smoky smoked plates
Home base: Gatlinburg, Tennessee

Smoky Mountain Smoke rolls out of Gatlinburg, Tennessee, serving Barbecue to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the smoky smoked plates at Smoky Mountain Smoke lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

A truck in a place the size of Gatlinburg becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Smoky Mountain Smoke is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

At Smoky Mountain Smoke, Barbecue is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

What makes Smoky Mountain Smoke itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Barbecue out of Gatlinburg, with smoky smoked plates as the order its name is attached to.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Smoky Mountain Smoke of Gatlinburg holds its place on Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
