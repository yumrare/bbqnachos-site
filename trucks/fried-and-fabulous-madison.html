Fried and Fabulous

Cuisine: Fried comfort
Signature: Deep-fried cheese curds
Home base: Madison, Wisconsin

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Fried and Fabulous of Madison, Wisconsin is no exception. A Fried comfort kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Madison the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

The signature at Fried and Fabulous is deep-fried cheese curds, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Madison crosses town for.

What Fried and Fabulous gives Madison is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

At Fried and Fabulous, Fried comfort is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Fried and Fabulous's content is Fried comfort cooked in Madison, Wisconsin, and judged by one dish above the rest: deep-fried cheese curds.
