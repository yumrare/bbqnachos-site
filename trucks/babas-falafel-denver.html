Baba's Falafel

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Falafel wraps
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Baba's Falafel is a Middle Eastern food truck based in Denver, Colorado. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Denver, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the window what Baba's Falafel is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: falafel wraps. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

The Denver scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation Baba's Falafel carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Baba's Falafel: the Middle Eastern is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No other truck in this record runs on Baba's Falafel's exact combination: Middle Eastern out of Denver, with falafel wraps as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: Baba's Falafel, of Denver, Colorado — Middle Eastern by trade, falafel wraps by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
