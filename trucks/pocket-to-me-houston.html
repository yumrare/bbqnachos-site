Pocket to Me

Cuisine: Empanadas
Signature: Handheld empanadas
Home base: Houston, Texas

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Pocket to Me of Houston, Texas is no exception. A Empanadas kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Houston the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Ask at the Pocket to Me window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: handheld empanadas. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

The Houston scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation Pocket to Me carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

Empanadas cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Pocket to Me leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Pocket to Me's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Houston as the stage, Empanadas as the craft, and handheld empanadas as the headline act.

That is the entry for Pocket to Me: Houston's own, a Empanadas truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
