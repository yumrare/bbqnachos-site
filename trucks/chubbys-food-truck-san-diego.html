The entry for San Diego, California reads Chubby's Food Truck: a Burgers truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

San Diego runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Chubby's Food Truck holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

The kitchen at Chubby's Food Truck treats Burgers as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Chubby's Food Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: San Diego as the stage, Burgers as the craft, and chubby burgers as the headline act.
