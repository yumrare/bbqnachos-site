Omaha, Nebraska · Directory Entry

The Chayo

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Chayo tacos
Home base: Omaha, Nebraska

Some trucks chase trends; The Chayo chases its regulars. Based in Omaha, Nebraska, the Mexican kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and The Chayo is carried by its chayo tacos. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Trucks like The Chayo are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Omaha may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Chayo: the Mexican is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and The Chayo's content is specific — Mexican cooked in Omaha, Nebraska, judged by one dish above the rest: chayo tacos.

The Chayo is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
