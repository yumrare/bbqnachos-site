Dark Shark Tacosicle

Cuisine: Mexican and popsicles
Signature: Tacos and pops
Home base: Savannah, Georgia

To eaters in Savannah, Georgia, Dark Shark Tacosicle simply means Mexican and popsicles done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the tacos and pops at Dark Shark Tacosicle lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Trucks like Dark Shark Tacosicle are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Savannah may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

What Dark Shark Tacosicle serves is Mexican and popsicles stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Dark Shark Tacosicle's content is Mexican and popsicles cooked in Savannah, Georgia, and judged by one dish above the rest: tacos and pops.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Dark Shark Tacosicle of Savannah holds its place on Mexican and popsicles, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
