Pierogi Wagon

Cuisine: Polish
Signature: Pierogi plates
Home base: Chicago, Illinois

Out of Chicago, Illinois, Pierogi Wagon serves Polish to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at Pierogi Wagon is famous for handing out is pierogi plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Chicago has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and Pierogi Wagon is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Pierogi Wagon cooks like it knows it: Polish with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Pierogi Wagon's content is Polish cooked in Chicago, Illinois, and judged by one dish above the rest: pierogi plates.

Filed under Illinois, in the Chicago pages: Pierogi Wagon. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
