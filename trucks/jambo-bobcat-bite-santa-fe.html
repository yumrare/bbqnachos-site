Jambo Bobcat Bite

Cuisine: African-Caribbean
Signature: Jerk chicken and curry goat
Home base: Santa Fe, New Mexico

Some trucks chase trends; Jambo Bobcat Bite chases its regulars. Based in Santa Fe, New Mexico, the African-Caribbean kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the Jambo Bobcat Bite window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: jerk chicken and curry goat. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck in a place the size of Santa Fe becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Jambo Bobcat Bite is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

African-Caribbean cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Jambo Bobcat Bite leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

No theme song, no mascot: Jambo Bobcat Bite's identity is written in three facts — Santa Fe, African-Caribbean, jerk chicken and curry goat — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Jambo Bobcat Bite serves Santa Fe its African-Caribbean, and the jerk chicken and curry goat is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
