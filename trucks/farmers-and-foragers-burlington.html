Farmers & Foragers

Cuisine: Farm-to-table
Signature: Vermont farm plates
Home base: Burlington, Vermont

Farmers & Foragers never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Farm-to-table kitchen out of Burlington, Vermont runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Farmers & Foragers is carried by its vermont farm plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck in Burlington answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Farmers & Foragers thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

At Farmers & Foragers the Farm-to-table is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Farmers & Foragers's content is Farm-to-table cooked in Burlington, Vermont, and judged by one dish above the rest: vermont farm plates.

Farmers & Foragers is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps.
