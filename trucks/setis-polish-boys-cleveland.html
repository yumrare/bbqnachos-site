Cleveland, Ohio · Directory Entry

Seti's Polish Boys

Cuisine: Polish-American
Signature: The Polish Boy sandwich
Home base: Cleveland, Ohio

Ask around Cleveland where the Polish-American cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Seti's Polish Boys. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Ohio, service after service.

If Seti's Polish Boys served nothing else, the record would read the same: the polish boy sandwich. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Cleveland; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Seti's Polish Boys has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Seti's Polish Boys cooks like it knows it: Polish-American with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

The originality of Seti's Polish Boys is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Cleveland as the stage, Polish-American as the craft, and the polish boy sandwich as the headline.

Seti's Polish Boys is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
