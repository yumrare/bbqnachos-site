Kiko's Place

Cuisine: Mexican seafood
Signature: Smoked tuna tacos
Home base: San Diego, California

Some trucks chase trends; Kiko's Place chases its regulars. Based in San Diego, California, the Mexican seafood kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The signature at Kiko's Place is smoked tuna tacos, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one San Diego crosses town for.

Survival in San Diego means being somebody's favorite, and Kiko's Place is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Everything on the Kiko's Place menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Mexican seafood here tastes focused.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Kiko's Place's content is specific — Mexican seafood cooked in San Diego, California, judged by one dish above the rest: smoked tuna tacos.

The quarterly review will come back to Kiko's Place the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
