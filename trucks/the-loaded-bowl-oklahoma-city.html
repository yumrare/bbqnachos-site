Oklahoma City, Oklahoma · Directory Entry

The Loaded Bowl

Cuisine: Vegan
Signature: Loaded bowls
Home base: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

Ask around Oklahoma City where the Vegan cooking is and somebody points to The Loaded Bowl before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Oklahoma, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at The Loaded Bowl the dish is loaded bowls. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Oklahoma City is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and The Loaded Bowl has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Oklahoma City gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Vegan cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and The Loaded Bowl leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

At The Loaded Bowl the vegan menu is the whole point, built from the ground up — which is why its line includes plenty of people who never expected to queue for a plant-based truck.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Loaded Bowl's writes itself: Vegan from Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, with loaded bowls as the dish the city attached to its name.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like The Loaded Bowl matter to the places they feed. Home base Oklahoma City, Oklahoma; cuisine Vegan; signature: loaded bowls. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
