Phoenix, Arizona · Directory Entry

Frites Street

Cuisine: Belgian fries
Signature: Street frites
Home base: Phoenix, Arizona

Home base Phoenix, Arizona; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Frites Street cooks Belgian fries the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

The signature at Frites Street is street frites, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Phoenix crosses town for.

A truck survives in Phoenix by being somebody's favorite, and Frites Street is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

What Frites Street serves is Belgian fries stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Frites Street's writes itself: Belgian fries from Phoenix, Arizona, with street frites as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Frites Street: Phoenix's own, a Belgian fries truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
