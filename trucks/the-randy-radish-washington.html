The Randy Radish

Cuisine: Vegan
Signature: Randy radish plates
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

The Randy Radish is a Vegan food truck based in Washington, District of Columbia. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Washington, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

What the window at The Randy Radish is famous for handing out is randy radish plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Washington has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and The Randy Radish is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

Space on the The Randy Radish menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Vegan here tastes so focused.

The whole menu at The Randy Radish is plant-based — this is a vegan kitchen by design, not by substitution, and it is part of why the truck's following includes people who drive past a dozen other windows to reach this one.

No other truck in this record runs on The Randy Radish's exact combination: Vegan out of Washington, with randy radish plates as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: The Randy Radish, of Washington, District of Columbia — Vegan by trade, randy radish plates by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
