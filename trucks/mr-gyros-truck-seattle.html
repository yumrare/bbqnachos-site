Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Mr. Gyros Truck

Cuisine: Greek
Signature: Mr. Gyros plates
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Mr. Gyros Truck holds its home base in Seattle, Washington and serves Greek from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the Mr. Gyros Truck window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: mr. gyros plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Seattle, and Mr. Gyros Truck has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Mr. Gyros Truck cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

What makes Mr. Gyros Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Greek out of Seattle, with mr. gyros plates as the order its name is attached to.

Mr. Gyros Truck is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
