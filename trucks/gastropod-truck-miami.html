Gastropod Truck

Cuisine: Gastropub
Signature: Pubbelly plates
Home base: Miami, Florida

Gastropod Truck never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Gastropub kitchen out of Miami, Florida runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

If Gastropod Truck could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: pubbelly plates. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in Miami is a neighborhood before it is a business — Gastropod Truck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

At Gastropod Truck the Gastropub is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Gastropod Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Miami, Gastropub, pubbelly plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Gastropod Truck serves Miami its Gastropub, and the pubbelly plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
