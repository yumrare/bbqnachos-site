Washington, District of Columbia · Directory Entry

PhoWheels

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Pho and banh mi
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Some trucks chase trends; PhoWheels chases its regulars. Based in Washington, District of Columbia, the Vietnamese kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

If PhoWheels could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: pho and banh mi. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A Washington truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and PhoWheels draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Vietnamese was made for the truck form, and PhoWheels leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Line up every truck in this record and PhoWheels still stands alone on its facts: a Vietnamese kitchen based in Washington, known first and last for pho and banh mi.

So the record stands: PhoWheels, of Washington, District of Columbia — Vietnamese by trade, pho and banh mi by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
