Cupbop (Provo)

Cuisine: Korean barbecue
Signature: Cupbop bowls
Home base: Provo, Utah

Home base Provo, Utah; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Cupbop (Provo) cooks Korean barbecue the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Cupbop (Provo) is carried by its cupbop bowls. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Ask in Provo where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Cupbop (Provo)'s. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

What Cupbop (Provo) serves is Korean barbecue stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Cupbop (Provo) would still stand alone on its facts: a Korean barbecue kitchen based in Provo, known first and last for cupbop bowls.

Filed under Utah, in the Provo pages: Cupbop (Provo). The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
