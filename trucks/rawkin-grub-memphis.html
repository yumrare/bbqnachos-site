Memphis, Tennessee · Directory Entry

Rawkin' Grub

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Rawkin' plates
Home base: Memphis, Tennessee

Some trucks chase trends; Rawkin' Grub chases its regulars. Based in Memphis, Tennessee, the Global comfort kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the Rawkin' Grub window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: rawkin' plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Ask in Memphis where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Rawkin' Grub's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

Rawkin' Grub cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Rawkin' Grub's is easy to write: Global comfort from Memphis, Tennessee, with rawkin' plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Rawkin' Grub of Memphis holds its place on Global comfort, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
