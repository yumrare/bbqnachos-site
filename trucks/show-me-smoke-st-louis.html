Show-Me Smoke

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Show-Me smoked plates
Home base: St. Louis, Missouri

The entry for St. Louis, Missouri reads Show-Me Smoke: a Barbecue truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Show-Me Smoke the dish is show-me smoked plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

St. Louis is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Show-Me Smoke has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever St. Louis gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

At Show-Me Smoke, Barbecue is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Show-Me Smoke's content is specific — Barbecue cooked in St. Louis, Missouri, judged by one dish above the rest: show-me smoked plates.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Show-Me Smoke of St. Louis takes its place in the record — Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
