Birmingham, Alabama · Directory Entry

Melt Birmingham

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Magic City melts
Home base: Birmingham, Alabama

Ask around Birmingham where the Grilled cheese cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Melt Birmingham. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Alabama, service after service.

magic city melts: that is the signature at Melt Birmingham, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Birmingham crosses town for.

A truck in Birmingham answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Melt Birmingham thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

There is a discipline to Grilled cheese cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Melt Birmingham works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Melt Birmingham does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Birmingham, Grilled cheese, magic city melts — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Melt Birmingham — Birmingham's own, a Grilled cheese truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
