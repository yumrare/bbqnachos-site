Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Revolution Taco

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Revolution tacos
Home base: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Revolution Taco keeps its home base in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and cooks Mexican the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

The famous hand-out at the Revolution Taco window is revolution tacos. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Philadelphia runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Revolution Taco holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Revolution Taco. The Mexican cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

No other truck in this record runs on Revolution Taco's exact combination: Mexican out of Philadelphia, with revolution tacos as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: Revolution Taco, of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania — Mexican by trade, revolution tacos by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
