Memphis, Tennessee · Directory Entry

Say Cheese Truck

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Memphis melts
Home base: Memphis, Tennessee

Say Cheese Truck keeps its home base in Memphis, Tennessee, and cooks Grilled cheese the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

memphis melts: that is the signature at Say Cheese Truck, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Memphis crosses town for.

A good truck is town news in Memphis, and Say Cheese Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Speed is part of the recipe at Say Cheese Truck — Grilled cheese cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Say Cheese Truck's writes itself: Grilled cheese from Memphis, Tennessee, with memphis melts as the dish the city attached to its name.

Filed under Tennessee, in the Memphis pages: Say Cheese Truck. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
