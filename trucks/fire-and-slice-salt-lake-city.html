Salt Lake City, Utah · Directory Entry

Fire and Slice

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Fire-sliced pies
Home base: Salt Lake City, Utah

Fire and Slice rolls out of Salt Lake City, Utah, serving Pizza to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Fire and Slice and go straight to the fire-sliced pies. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

What Fire and Slice gives Salt Lake City is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

The kitchen at Fire and Slice treats Pizza as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Fire and Slice's content is Pizza cooked in Salt Lake City, Utah, and judged by one dish above the rest: fire-sliced pies.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Fire and Slice of Salt Lake City takes its place in the record — Pizza, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
