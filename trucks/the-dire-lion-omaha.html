Omaha, Nebraska · Directory Entry

The Dire Lion

Cuisine: British
Signature: Fish and chips and meat pies
Home base: Omaha, Nebraska

The Dire Lion of Omaha, Nebraska earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A British kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at The Dire Lion it is fish and chips and meat pies. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Trucks like The Dire Lion are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Omaha may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Dire Lion. The British cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Dire Lion's writes itself: British from Omaha, Nebraska, with fish and chips and meat pies as the dish the city attached to its name.

When the quarterly review comes back to The Dire Lion, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
