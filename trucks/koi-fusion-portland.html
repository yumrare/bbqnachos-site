Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

Koi Fusion

Cuisine: Korean-Mexican
Signature: Korean tacos and sliders
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Koi Fusion of Portland, Oregon earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Korean-Mexican kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

If Koi Fusion served nothing else, the record would read the same: korean tacos and sliders. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Competition in Portland is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Koi Fusion has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

There is a discipline to Korean-Mexican cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Koi Fusion works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

What makes Koi Fusion itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Korean-Mexican out of Portland, with korean tacos and sliders as the order its name is attached to.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Koi Fusion of Portland holds its place on Korean-Mexican, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
