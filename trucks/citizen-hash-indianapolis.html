Indianapolis, Indiana · Directory Entry

Citizen Hash

Cuisine: Breakfast hash
Signature: Citizen hash plates
Home base: Indianapolis, Indiana

Ask around Indianapolis where the Breakfast hash cooking is and somebody points to Citizen Hash before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Indiana, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Ask at the window what Citizen Hash is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: citizen hash plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A good truck is town news in Indianapolis, and Citizen Hash has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Citizen Hash cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Citizen Hash would still stand alone on its facts: a Breakfast hash kitchen based in Indianapolis, known first and last for citizen hash plates.

The entry sits in the Indianapolis pages, filed under Indiana: Citizen Hash. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
