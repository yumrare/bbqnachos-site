Verde Food Truck

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Verde burritos
Home base: Boulder, Colorado

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Verde Food Truck of Boulder, Colorado is no exception. A Mexican kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Boulder the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

The famous hand-out at the Verde Food Truck window is verde burritos. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

In Boulder, a good truck is town news, and Verde Food Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Verde Food Truck cooks like it knows it: Mexican with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Verde Food Truck's content is specific — Mexican cooked in Boulder, Colorado, judged by one dish above the rest: verde burritos.
