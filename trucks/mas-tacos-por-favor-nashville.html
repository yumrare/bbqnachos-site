Nashville, Tennessee · Featured Truck

Mas Tacos Por Favor

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Quinoa sweet potato tacos
Home base: Nashville, Tennessee

Home base Nashville, Tennessee; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Mas Tacos Por Favor cooks Mexican the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

quinoa sweet potato tacos: that is the signature at Mas Tacos Por Favor, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Nashville crosses town for.

Survival in Nashville means being somebody's favorite, and Mas Tacos Por Favor is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

At Mas Tacos Por Favor the Mexican is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Start with a handful of names if you want to understand Nashville's truck scene; Mas Tacos Por Favor is on the first page. Its influence shows in the trucks that followed and the routes eaters plan around its window.

The regulars say the real signature is the rhythm — truck posted up, line forming, window sliding open. Mas Tacos Por Favor has it down to a ritual, and rituals turn good trucks into beloved ones.

There is a small ceremony to a stop at Mas Tacos Por Favor: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers who are about to agree with you. Trucks that create rituals outlive trucks that only create meals.

What makes Mas Tacos Por Favor itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Mexican out of Nashville, with quinoa sweet potato tacos as the order its name is attached to.

This directory's front row is the featured tier, and Nashville put Mas Tacos Por Favor in it — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

The quarterly review will come back to Mas Tacos Por Favor the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
