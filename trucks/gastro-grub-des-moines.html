Des Moines, Iowa · Directory Entry

Gastro Grub

Cuisine: Gastropub
Signature: Iowa gastropub plates
Home base: Des Moines, Iowa

Gastro Grub rolls out of Des Moines, Iowa, serving Gastropub to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Ask at the window what Gastro Grub is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: iowa gastropub plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

In Des Moines, a good truck is town news, and Gastro Grub has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

What Gastro Grub serves is Gastropub stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The originality of Gastro Grub is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Des Moines as the stage, Gastropub as the craft, and iowa gastropub plates as the headline.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Gastro Grub matter to the places they feed. Home base Des Moines, Iowa; cuisine Gastropub; signature: iowa gastropub plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
