Wilson, Wyoming · Directory Entry

Streetfood at the Stagecoach

Cuisine: Global street food
Signature: Stagecoach plates
Home base: Wilson, Wyoming

Streetfood at the Stagecoach of Wilson, Wyoming earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Global street food kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Streetfood at the Stagecoach is carried by its stagecoach plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In a place the size of Wilson, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Streetfood at the Stagecoach is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Streetfood at the Stagecoach: the Global street food is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No other truck in this record runs on Streetfood at the Stagecoach's exact combination: Global street food out of Wilson, with stagecoach plates as the order its name is attached to.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Streetfood at the Stagecoach matter to the places they feed. Home base Wilson, Wyoming; cuisine Global street food; signature: stagecoach plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
