Hattiesburg, Mississippi · Directory Entry

Smokin' Aces BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Hub City smoked meats
Home base: Hattiesburg, Mississippi

Ask around Hattiesburg where the Barbecue cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Smokin' Aces BBQ. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Mississippi, service after service.

If Smokin' Aces BBQ could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: hub city smoked meats. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

What Smokin' Aces BBQ gives Hattiesburg is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Smokin' Aces BBQ: the Barbecue is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

What makes Smokin' Aces BBQ itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Barbecue out of Hattiesburg, with hub city smoked meats as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: Smokin' Aces BBQ serves Hattiesburg its Barbecue, and the hub city smoked meats is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
