Amanda Bananas

Cuisine: Frozen bananas
Signature: Banana whip soft serve
Home base: Hoboken, New Jersey

Ask around Hoboken where the Frozen bananas cooking is and somebody points to Amanda Bananas before long. It earned its line out at the curb in New Jersey, service after service, the only honest way there is.

If Amanda Bananas could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: banana whip soft serve. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in a place the size of Hoboken becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Amanda Bananas is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

At Amanda Bananas the Frozen bananas is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Line up every truck in this record and Amanda Bananas still stands alone on its facts: a Frozen bananas kitchen based in Hoboken, known first and last for banana whip soft serve.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Amanda Bananas matter to the places they feed. Home base Hoboken, New Jersey; cuisine Frozen bananas; signature: banana whip soft serve. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
