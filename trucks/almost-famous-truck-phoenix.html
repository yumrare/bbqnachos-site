Phoenix, Arizona · Directory Entry

Almost Famous Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Almost famous plates
Home base: Phoenix, Arizona

Almost Famous Truck keeps its home base in Phoenix, Arizona, and cooks American comfort the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

almost famous plates: that is the signature at Almost Famous Truck, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Phoenix crosses town for.

A truck in Phoenix is a neighborhood before it is a business — Almost Famous Truck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

The kitchen at Almost Famous Truck treats American comfort as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No other truck in this record runs on Almost Famous Truck's exact combination: American comfort out of Phoenix, with almost famous plates as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: Almost Famous Truck, of Phoenix, Arizona — American comfort by trade, almost famous plates by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
