The Local Bison

Cuisine: American
Signature: Bison burgers
Home base: Tulsa, Oklahoma

Read the Tulsa, Oklahoma pages and you will find The Local Bison: a American truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the bison burgers. At The Local Bison, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Ask in Tulsa where to eat and the directions often end at a window: The Local Bison's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and The Local Bison cooks like it knows it: American with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Line up every truck in this record and The Local Bison still stands alone on its facts: a American kitchen based in Tulsa, known first and last for bison burgers.

So the record stands for The Local Bison of Tulsa, Oklahoma: American by trade, bison burgers by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
