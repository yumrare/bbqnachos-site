Cranston, Rhode Island · Directory Entry

Poco Loco Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Rhode Island tacos
Home base: Cranston, Rhode Island

Ask around Cranston where the Mexican cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Poco Loco Tacos. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Rhode Island, service after service.

The famous hand-out at the Poco Loco Tacos window is rhode island tacos. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Trucks like Poco Loco Tacos are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Cranston may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

At Poco Loco Tacos the Mexican is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Poco Loco Tacos's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Cranston as the stage, Mexican as the craft, and rhode island tacos as the headline act.

For the permanent record: Poco Loco Tacos serves Cranston its Mexican, and the rhode island tacos is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
