San Francisco, California · Featured Truck

Curry Up Now

Cuisine: Indian street food
Signature: Tikka masala burrito
Home base: San Francisco, California

Read the San Francisco, California pages and you will find Curry Up Now: a Indian street food truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

The famous hand-out at the Curry Up Now window is tikka masala burrito. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A truck survives in San Francisco by being somebody's favorite, and Curry Up Now is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Space on the Curry Up Now menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Indian street food here tastes so focused.

Popular is one thing; foundational is another. A generation of San Francisco eaters learned what a food truck could be at the Curry Up Now window, and the lesson stuck.

A stop at Curry Up Now has a small ceremony to it: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers about to agree with you. Rituals outlive meals.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Curry Up Now runs on that kind of memory.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Curry Up Now would still stand alone on its facts: a Indian street food kitchen based in San Francisco, known first and last for tikka masala burrito.

There is a reason Curry Up Now sits in the featured tier of this record. Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points — the name a city reaches for when it explains its food truck scene to a stranger. Curry Up Now is one of those names for San Francisco.

When the quarterly review comes back to Curry Up Now, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
