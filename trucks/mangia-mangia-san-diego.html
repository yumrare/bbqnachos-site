Mangia Mangia

Cuisine: Italian
Signature: Arancini and meatball subs
Home base: San Diego, California

Some trucks chase trends; Mangia Mangia chases its regulars. Based in San Diego, California, the Italian kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Mangia Mangia is carried by its arancini and meatball subs. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

A truck survives in San Diego by being somebody's favorite, and Mangia Mangia is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Italian from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Mangia Mangia works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Mangia Mangia's content is Italian cooked in San Diego, California, and judged by one dish above the rest: arancini and meatball subs.

Mangia Mangia is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
