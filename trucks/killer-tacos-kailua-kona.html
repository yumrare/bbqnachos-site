Kailua-Kona, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Killer Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Killer tacos
Home base: Kailua-Kona, Hawaii

Killer Tacos rolls out of Kailua-Kona, Hawaii, serving Mexican to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Killer Tacos it is killer tacos. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Kailua-Kona; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Killer Tacos has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Mexican was made for the truck form, and Killer Tacos leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The originality of Killer Tacos is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Kailua-Kona as the stage, Mexican as the craft, and killer tacos as the headline.

That is the entry for Killer Tacos — Kailua-Kona's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
