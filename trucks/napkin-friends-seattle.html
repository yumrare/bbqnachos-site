Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Napkin Friends

Cuisine: Jewish deli fusion
Signature: Latke sandwiches
Home base: Seattle, Washington

The entry for Seattle, Washington reads Napkin Friends: a Jewish deli fusion truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

If Napkin Friends served nothing else, the record would read the same: latke sandwiches. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Survival in Seattle means being somebody's favorite, and Napkin Friends is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Jewish deli fusion from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Napkin Friends works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

What makes Napkin Friends itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Jewish deli fusion out of Seattle, with latke sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: Napkin Friends, of Seattle, Washington — Jewish deli fusion by trade, latke sandwiches by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
