The Springs Cupcake Truck

Cuisine: Cupcakes
Signature: Springs cupcakes
Home base: Colorado Springs, Colorado

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Springs Cupcake Truck of Colorado Springs, Colorado is no exception. A Cupcakes kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Colorado Springs the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and The Springs Cupcake Truck is carried by its springs cupcakes. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A good truck is town news in Colorado Springs, and The Springs Cupcake Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and The Springs Cupcake Truck cooks like it knows it: Cupcakes with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

The Springs Cupcake Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Colorado Springs as the stage, Cupcakes as the craft, and springs cupcakes as the headline act.

For the permanent record: The Springs Cupcake Truck serves Colorado Springs its Cupcakes, and the springs cupcakes is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
