Haleiwa, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Opal Thai

Cuisine: Thai
Signature: Opal Thai plates
Home base: Haleiwa, Hawaii

Out of Haleiwa, Hawaii, Opal Thai serves Thai to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the Opal Thai window is opal thai plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Ask in Haleiwa where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Opal Thai's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Opal Thai serves Thai stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No other truck in this record runs on Opal Thai's exact combination: Thai out of Haleiwa, with opal thai plates as the order its name is attached to.

Opal Thai is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
