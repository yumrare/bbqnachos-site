Twisted Sisters Cupcakes

Cuisine: Cupcakes
Signature: Twisted cupcakes
Home base: Virginia Beach, Virginia

Twisted Sisters Cupcakes holds its home base in Virginia Beach, Virginia and serves Cupcakes from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

twisted cupcakes: that is the signature at Twisted Sisters Cupcakes, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Virginia Beach crosses town for.

Virginia Beach eats above its weight because of trucks like Twisted Sisters Cupcakes. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

There is a discipline to Cupcakes cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Twisted Sisters Cupcakes works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The originality of Twisted Sisters Cupcakes is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Virginia Beach as the stage, Cupcakes as the craft, and twisted cupcakes as the headline.

That is the entry for Twisted Sisters Cupcakes — Virginia Beach's own, a Cupcakes truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
