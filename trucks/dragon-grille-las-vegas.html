Dragon Grille

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Dragon bowls
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

To eaters in Las Vegas, Nevada, Dragon Grille simply means Asian fusion done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Dragon Grille it is dragon bowls. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Competition in Las Vegas is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Dragon Grille has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Dragon Grille cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

Dragon Grille does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Las Vegas, Asian fusion, dragon bowls — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Dragon Grille stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
