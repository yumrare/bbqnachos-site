Bang Bite

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Bang burgers
Home base: Santa Fe, New Mexico

Bang Bite is a Burgers food truck based in Santa Fe, New Mexico. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Santa Fe, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the window what Bang Bite is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: bang burgers. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Trucks like Bang Bite are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Santa Fe may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Everything on the Bang Bite menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Burgers here tastes focused.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Bang Bite's content is Burgers cooked in Santa Fe, New Mexico, and judged by one dish above the rest: bang burgers.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Bang Bite of Santa Fe holds its place on Burgers, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
