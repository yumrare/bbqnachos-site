The Rib Whip

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Midwestern BBQ plates
Home base: San Francisco, California

To eaters in San Francisco, California, The Rib Whip simply means Barbecue done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at The Rib Whip and go straight to the midwestern bbq plates. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

The San Francisco scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation The Rib Whip carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

Speed is part of the recipe at The Rib Whip — Barbecue cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The originality of The Rib Whip is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: San Francisco as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and midwestern bbq plates as the headline.
