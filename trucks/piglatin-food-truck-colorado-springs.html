PigLatin Food Truck

Cuisine: Latin-Caribbean
Signature: PigLatin plates
Home base: Colorado Springs, Colorado

To eaters in Colorado Springs, Colorado, PigLatin Food Truck simply means Latin-Caribbean done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

If PigLatin Food Truck could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: piglatin plates. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in a place the size of Colorado Springs becomes a landmark with a schedule, and PigLatin Food Truck is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

What PigLatin Food Truck serves is Latin-Caribbean stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and PigLatin Food Truck's is easy to write: Latin-Caribbean from Colorado Springs, Colorado, with piglatin plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

PigLatin Food Truck stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
