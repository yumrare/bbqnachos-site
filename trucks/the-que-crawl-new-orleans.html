The Que Crawl

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Que crawl plates
Home base: New Orleans, Louisiana

Out of New Orleans, Louisiana, The Que Crawl serves Barbecue to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and The Que Crawl is carried by its que crawl plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Survival in New Orleans means being somebody's favorite, and The Que Crawl is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Everything on the The Que Crawl menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Barbecue here tastes focused.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Que Crawl would still stand alone on its facts: a Barbecue kitchen based in New Orleans, known first and last for que crawl plates.

The Que Crawl is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
