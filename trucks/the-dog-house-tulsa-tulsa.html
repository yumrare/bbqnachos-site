The Dog House Tulsa

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Dog house franks
Home base: Tulsa, Oklahoma

The Dog House Tulsa rolls out of Tulsa, Oklahoma, serving Hot dogs to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The signature at The Dog House Tulsa is dog house franks, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Tulsa crosses town for.

Everybody knows everybody at a Tulsa truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. The Dog House Tulsa thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Dog House Tulsa. The Hot dogs cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. The Dog House Tulsa's content is Hot dogs cooked in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and judged by one dish above the rest: dog house franks.

When the quarterly review comes back to The Dog House Tulsa, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
