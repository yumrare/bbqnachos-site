The Gravy Train

Cuisine: Southern comfort
Signature: Gravy train plates
Home base: Greenville, South Carolina

The Gravy Train is a Southern comfort food truck based in Greenville, South Carolina. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Greenville, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at The Gravy Train it is gravy train plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In a place the size of Greenville, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what The Gravy Train is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

At The Gravy Train the Southern comfort is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Gravy Train's writes itself: Southern comfort from Greenville, South Carolina, with gravy train plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

The Gravy Train stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
