Washington, District of Columbia · Directory Entry

Cajunators

Cuisine: Cajun
Signature: Cajun plates
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Cajunators of Washington, District of Columbia is no exception. A Cajun kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Washington the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

The famous hand-out at the Cajunators window is cajun plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Washington counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Cajunators is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

There is a discipline to Cajun cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Cajunators works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

No theme song, no mascot: Cajunators's identity is written in three facts — Washington, Cajun, cajun plates — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

That is the entry for Cajunators: Washington's own, a Cajun truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
