Cleveland, Ohio · Directory Entry

Motor Mouth

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Motor-mouth burgers
Home base: Cleveland, Ohio

Out of Cleveland, Ohio, Motor Mouth serves Global comfort to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at Motor Mouth is famous for handing out is motor-mouth burgers — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

In Cleveland, a good truck is town news, and Motor Mouth has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

The kitchen at Motor Mouth treats Global comfort as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No theme song, no mascot: Motor Mouth's identity is written in three facts — Cleveland, Global comfort, motor-mouth burgers — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Motor Mouth matter to the places they feed. Home base Cleveland, Ohio; cuisine Global comfort; signature: motor-mouth burgers. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
