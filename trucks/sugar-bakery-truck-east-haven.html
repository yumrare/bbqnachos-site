Sugar Bakery Truck

Cuisine: Cupcakes
Signature: Sugar cupcakes
Home base: East Haven, Connecticut

Sugar Bakery Truck never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Cupcakes kitchen out of East Haven, Connecticut runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Sugar Bakery Truck it is sugar cupcakes. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In East Haven, a good truck is town news, and Sugar Bakery Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Speed is part of the recipe at Sugar Bakery Truck — Cupcakes cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

What makes Sugar Bakery Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Cupcakes out of East Haven, with sugar cupcakes as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Sugar Bakery Truck — East Haven's own, a Cupcakes truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
