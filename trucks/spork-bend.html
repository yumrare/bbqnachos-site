Bend, Oregon · Directory Entry

Spork

Cuisine: Global fusion
Signature: Spicy pork tacos
Home base: Bend, Oregon

Spork keeps its home base in Bend, Oregon, and cooks Global fusion the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

What the window at Spork is famous for handing out is spicy pork tacos — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Spork brings Bend the wider truck world without the drive: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait their turn.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Global fusion was made for the truck form, and Spork leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The originality of Spork is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Bend as the stage, Global fusion as the craft, and spicy pork tacos as the headline.

That is the entry for Spork: Bend's own, a Global fusion truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
