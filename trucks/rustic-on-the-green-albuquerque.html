Rustic on the Green

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Rustic plates
Home base: Albuquerque, New Mexico

Rustic on the Green of Albuquerque, New Mexico earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A American comfort kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Rustic on the Green is carried by its rustic plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In Albuquerque, a good truck is town news, and Rustic on the Green has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Rustic on the Green cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Rustic on the Green's content is American comfort cooked in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and judged by one dish above the rest: rustic plates.

Filed under New Mexico, in the Albuquerque pages: Rustic on the Green. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
