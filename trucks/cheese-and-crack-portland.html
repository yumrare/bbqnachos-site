Cheese & Crack

Cuisine: Cheese plates
Signature: Cheese and charcuterie boards
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Out of Portland, Oregon, Cheese & Crack serves Cheese plates to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the cheese and charcuterie boards at Cheese & Crack lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Portland runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Cheese & Crack holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

There is a discipline to Cheese plates cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Cheese & Crack works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Cheese & Crack would still stand alone on its facts: a Cheese plates kitchen based in Portland, known first and last for cheese and charcuterie boards.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Cheese & Crack matter to the places they feed. Home base Portland, Oregon; cuisine Cheese plates; signature: cheese and charcuterie boards — a small, complete portrait.
