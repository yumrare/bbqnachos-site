Eugene's Hot Chicken

Cuisine: Hot chicken
Signature: Nashville-style hot chicken
Home base: Birmingham, Alabama

Out of Birmingham, Alabama, Eugene's Hot Chicken serves Hot chicken to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the Eugene's Hot Chicken window is nashville-style hot chicken. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A truck in Birmingham answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Eugene's Hot Chicken thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Eugene's Hot Chicken: the Hot chicken is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Eugene's Hot Chicken does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Birmingham, Hot chicken, nashville-style hot chicken — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Eugene's Hot Chicken of Birmingham holds its place on Hot chicken, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
