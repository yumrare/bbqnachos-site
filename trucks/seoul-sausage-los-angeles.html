Los Angeles, California · Featured Truck

Seoul Sausage

Cuisine: Korean sausages
Signature: Kalbi sausages and balls
Home base: Los Angeles, California
Rolling since 2012

Ask around Los Angeles where the Korean sausages cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Seoul Sausage. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in California, service after service.

Seoul Sausage has been rolling since 2012. Winners of The Great Food Truck Race season three.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Seoul Sausage it is kalbi sausages and balls. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Los Angeles's, and Seoul Sausage holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Korean sausages was made for the truck form, and Seoul Sausage leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

This directory's front row is the featured tier, and Los Angeles put Seoul Sausage in it — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

A stop at Seoul Sausage has a small ceremony to it: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers about to agree with you. Rituals outlive meals.

What makes Seoul Sausage itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Korean sausages out of Los Angeles, with kalbi sausages and balls as the order its name is attached to.

Ask a Los Angeles local which trucks matter and Seoul Sausage comes early — not out of habit, but out of years of lunches that landed right, one after another.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Seoul Sausage matter to the places they feed. Home base Los Angeles, California; cuisine Korean sausages; signature: kalbi sausages and balls — a small, complete portrait.
