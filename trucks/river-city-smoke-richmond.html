River City Smoke

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: River City smoked plates
Home base: Richmond, Virginia

River City Smoke is a Barbecue food truck based in Richmond, Virginia. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Richmond, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The signature at River City Smoke is river city smoked plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Richmond crosses town for.

In Richmond, a good truck is town news, and River City Smoke has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Cooking Barbecue from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and River City Smoke works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

No other truck in this record runs on River City Smoke's exact combination: Barbecue out of Richmond, with river city smoked plates as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: River City Smoke serves Richmond its Barbecue, and the river city smoked plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
