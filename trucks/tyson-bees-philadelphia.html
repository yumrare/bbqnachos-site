Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Tyson Bees

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Korean BBQ tacos
Home base: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Tyson Bees of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Asian fusion kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

The famous hand-out at the Tyson Bees window is korean bbq tacos. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

What Tyson Bees understands about Philadelphia is that a truck here is a neighborhood before it is a business. It feeds offices at noon and families at dusk, and both crowds claim it as their own.

What Tyson Bees serves is Asian fusion stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Tyson Bees would still stand alone on its facts: a Asian fusion kitchen based in Philadelphia, known first and last for korean bbq tacos.

When the quarterly review comes back to Tyson Bees, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
