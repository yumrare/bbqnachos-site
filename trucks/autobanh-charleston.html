Charleston, South Carolina · Directory Entry

AutoBanh

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Banh mi sandwiches
Home base: Charleston, South Carolina

Read the Charleston, South Carolina pages and you will find AutoBanh: a Vietnamese truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and AutoBanh is carried by its banh mi sandwiches. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Ask in Charleston where to eat and the directions often end at one window: AutoBanh's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

At AutoBanh the Vietnamese is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and AutoBanh's is easy to write: Vietnamese from Charleston, South Carolina, with banh mi sandwiches as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: AutoBanh serves Charleston its Vietnamese, and the banh mi sandwiches is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
