LoLo's Bowls

Cuisine: Bowls
Signature: Grain and poke bowls
Home base: Wichita, Kansas

Out of Wichita, Kansas, LoLo's Bowls serves Bowls to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the LoLo's Bowls window is grain and poke bowls. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Ask in Wichita where to eat and the directions often end at a window: LoLo's Bowls's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

The kitchen at LoLo's Bowls treats Bowls as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

What makes LoLo's Bowls itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Bowls out of Wichita, with grain and poke bowls as the order its name is attached to.

Filed under Kansas, in the Wichita pages: LoLo's Bowls. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
