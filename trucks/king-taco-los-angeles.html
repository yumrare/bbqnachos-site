Los Angeles, California · Directory Entry

King Taco

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Suadero tacos
Home base: Los Angeles, California

King Taco holds its home base in Los Angeles, California and serves Mexican from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The signature at King Taco is suadero tacos, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Los Angeles crosses town for.

Los Angeles runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and King Taco holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

What King Taco serves is Mexican stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and King Taco's is easy to write: Mexican from Los Angeles, California, with suadero tacos as the dish the city attached to its name.

So the record stands: King Taco, of Los Angeles, California — Mexican by trade, suadero tacos by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
