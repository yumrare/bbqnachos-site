Madison, Wisconsin · Directory Entry

Blowin' Smoke BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Blowin' smoke meats
Home base: Madison, Wisconsin

Ask around Madison where the Barbecue cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Blowin' Smoke BBQ. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Wisconsin, service after service.

What the window at Blowin' Smoke BBQ is famous for handing out is blowin' smoke meats — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Madison; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Blowin' Smoke BBQ has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Cooking Barbecue from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Blowin' Smoke BBQ works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The originality of Blowin' Smoke BBQ is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Madison as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and blowin' smoke meats as the headline.

Blowin' Smoke BBQ is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
