Atlanta, Georgia · Directory Entry

Tex's Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Nueva Texicana tacos
Home base: Atlanta, Georgia

Ask around Atlanta where the Mexican cooking is and somebody points to Tex's Tacos before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Georgia, service after service, the only honest way there is.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Tex's Tacos is carried by its nueva texicana tacos. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In a scene as crowded as Atlanta's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Tex's Tacos draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Tex's Tacos. The Mexican cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Tex's Tacos's writes itself: Mexican from Atlanta, Georgia, with nueva texicana tacos as the dish the city attached to its name.

Tex's Tacos stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
