Huntsville, Alabama · Directory Entry

Rocket City Ramen

Cuisine: Ramen
Signature: Rocket City ramen
Home base: Huntsville, Alabama

Rocket City Ramen keeps its home base in Huntsville, Alabama, and cooks Ramen the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Ask at the Rocket City Ramen window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: rocket city ramen. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

In Huntsville, a good truck is town news, and Rocket City Ramen has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Ramen was made for the truck form, and Rocket City Ramen leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Rocket City Ramen's writes itself: Ramen from Huntsville, Alabama, with rocket city ramen as the dish the city attached to its name.

When the quarterly review comes back to Rocket City Ramen, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
