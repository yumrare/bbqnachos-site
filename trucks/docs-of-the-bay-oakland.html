Oakland, California · Directory Entry

Doc's of the Bay

Cuisine: Diner comfort
Signature: The Doc's burger
Home base: Oakland, California

Some trucks chase trends; Doc's of the Bay chases its regulars. Based in Oakland, California, the Diner comfort kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Doc's of the Bay it is the doc's burger. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In a scene as crowded as Oakland's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Doc's of the Bay draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Doc's of the Bay serves Diner comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Line up every truck in this record and Doc's of the Bay still stands alone on its facts: a Diner comfort kitchen based in Oakland, known first and last for the doc's burger.

That is the entry for Doc's of the Bay — Oakland's own, a Diner comfort truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
