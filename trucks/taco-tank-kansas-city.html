Kansas City, Missouri · Directory Entry

Taco Tank

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Tank tacos
Home base: Kansas City, Missouri

Home base Kansas City, Missouri; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Taco Tank cooks Mexican the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

What the window at Taco Tank is famous for handing out is tank tacos — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

A truck in a place the size of Kansas City becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Taco Tank is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Space on the Taco Tank menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Mexican here tastes so focused.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Taco Tank's content is Mexican cooked in Kansas City, Missouri, and judged by one dish above the rest: tank tacos.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Taco Tank of Kansas City takes its place in the record — Mexican, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
