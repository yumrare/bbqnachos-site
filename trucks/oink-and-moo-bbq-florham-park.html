Oink and Moo BBQ

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Smoked brisket sandwiches
Home base: Florham Park, New Jersey

To eaters in Florham Park, New Jersey, Oink and Moo BBQ simply means Barbecue done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Ask at the window what Oink and Moo BBQ is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: smoked brisket sandwiches. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Everybody knows everybody at a Florham Park truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Oink and Moo BBQ thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

There is a discipline to Barbecue cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Oink and Moo BBQ works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Oink and Moo BBQ's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Florham Park as the stage, Barbecue as the craft, and smoked brisket sandwiches as the headline act.

For the permanent record: Oink and Moo BBQ serves Florham Park its Barbecue, and the smoked brisket sandwiches is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
