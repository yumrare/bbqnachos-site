Cousins Maine Lobster (San Diego)

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Connecticut lobster roll
Home base: San Diego, California

Cousins Maine Lobster (San Diego) holds its home base in San Diego, California and serves Seafood from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.
