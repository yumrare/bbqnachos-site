One Guy Steak & Chicken

Cuisine: Steak and chicken
Signature: Steak and chicken plates
Home base: Jackson, Mississippi

One Guy Steak & Chicken is a Steak and chicken food truck based in Jackson, Mississippi. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Jackson, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Ask at the One Guy Steak & Chicken window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: steak and chicken plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Jackson eats above its weight because of trucks like One Guy Steak & Chicken. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Everything on the One Guy Steak & Chicken menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Steak and chicken here tastes focused.

No theme song, no mascot: One Guy Steak & Chicken's identity is written in three facts — Jackson, Steak and chicken, steak and chicken plates — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

So the record stands for One Guy Steak & Chicken of Jackson, Mississippi: Steak and chicken by trade, steak and chicken plates by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
