Athens, Georgia · Directory Entry

The Food Truck Athens

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Bulldog plates
Home base: Athens, Georgia

The Food Truck Athens keeps its home base in Athens, Georgia, and cooks American comfort the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

If The Food Truck Athens served nothing else, the record would read the same: bulldog plates. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Athens eats above its weight because of trucks like The Food Truck Athens. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Food Truck Athens: the American comfort is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No theme song, no mascot: The Food Truck Athens's identity is written in three facts — Athens, American comfort, bulldog plates — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like The Food Truck Athens matter to the places they feed. Home base Athens, Georgia; cuisine American comfort; signature: bulldog plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
