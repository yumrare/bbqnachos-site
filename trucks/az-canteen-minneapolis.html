The entry for Minneapolis, Minnesota reads AZ Canteen: a Global truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

The andrew zimmern's global plates at AZ Canteen is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Minneapolis runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and AZ Canteen holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

AZ Canteen cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

AZ Canteen's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Minneapolis as the stage, Global as the craft, and andrew zimmern's global plates as the headline act.
