Little Rock, Arkansas · Directory Entry

Excaliburger

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: The Excaliburger
Home base: Little Rock, Arkansas

Out of Little Rock, Arkansas, Excaliburger serves Burgers to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Excaliburger the dish is the excaliburger. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Little Rock eats above its weight because of trucks like Excaliburger. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Excaliburger serves Burgers stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Excaliburger's writes itself: Burgers from Little Rock, Arkansas, with the excaliburger as the dish the city attached to its name.

When the quarterly review comes back to Excaliburger, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
