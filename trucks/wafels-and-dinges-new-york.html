New York, New York · Featured Truck

Wafels & Dinges

Cuisine: Belgian waffles
Signature: Liège wafel with spekuloos
Home base: New York, New York
Rolling since 2007

Ask around New York where the Belgian waffles cooking is and somebody points to Wafels & Dinges before long. It earned its line out at the curb in New York, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Wafels & Dinges has been rolling since 2007. Thomas DeGeest's Belgian waffle truck, a Vendy Award winner.

The signature at Wafels & Dinges is liège wafel with spekuloos, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one New York crosses town for.

A truck survives in New York by being somebody's favorite, and Wafels & Dinges is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Belgian waffles from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Wafels & Dinges works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Some trucks are popular; Wafels & Dinges is foundational. In New York, its window is where a generation of eaters learned what a food truck could be, and the lesson stuck.

What separates Wafels & Dinges from a hundred good trucks is the afterglow — people leave planning the next visit and recruiting for it. That is not marketing; that is what a signature does when it lands.

Wafels & Dinges's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: New York as the stage, Belgian waffles as the craft, and liège wafel with spekuloos as the headline act.

Featured entries in this directory are the trucks other trucks get measured against, and Wafels & Dinges earned that standing in New York the only way it can be earned — year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

For the permanent record: Wafels & Dinges serves New York its Belgian waffles, and the liège wafel with spekuloos is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
