Indianapolis, Indiana · Directory Entry

The Flying Cupcake

Cuisine: Cupcakes
Signature: Cupcakes from the truck
Home base: Indianapolis, Indiana

The entry for Indianapolis, Indiana reads The Flying Cupcake: a Cupcakes truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at The Flying Cupcake the dish is cupcakes from the truck. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Trucks like The Flying Cupcake are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Indianapolis may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

There is a discipline to Cupcakes cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Flying Cupcake works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Flying Cupcake's writes itself: Cupcakes from Indianapolis, Indiana, with cupcakes from the truck as the dish the city attached to its name.

So the record stands: The Flying Cupcake, of Indianapolis, Indiana — Cupcakes by trade, cupcakes from the truck by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
