District Taco Truck

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: District tacos
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Read the Washington, District of Columbia pages and you will find District Taco Truck: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the district tacos at District Taco Truck lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

A Washington truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and District Taco Truck draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

There is a discipline to Mexican cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. District Taco Truck works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

No theme song, no mascot: District Taco Truck's identity is written in three facts — Washington, Mexican, district tacos — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

District Taco Truck stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
