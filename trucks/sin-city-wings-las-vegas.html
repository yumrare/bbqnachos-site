Sin City Wings

Cuisine: Wings
Signature: Sin City wings
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

Sin City Wings holds its home base in Las Vegas, Nevada and serves Wings from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the window what Sin City Wings is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: sin city wings. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Las Vegas has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and Sin City Wings is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

Sin City Wings serves Wings stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.
