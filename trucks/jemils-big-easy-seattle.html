Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Jemil's Big Easy

Cuisine: Cajun
Signature: Jambalaya and etouffee
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Out of Seattle, Washington, Jemil's Big Easy serves Cajun to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Ask at the Jemil's Big Easy window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: jambalaya and etouffee. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Seattle runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Jemil's Big Easy holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

At Jemil's Big Easy the Cajun is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Jemil's Big Easy's writes itself: Cajun from Seattle, Washington, with jambalaya and etouffee as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: Jemil's Big Easy serves Seattle its Cajun, and the jambalaya and etouffee is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
