Kahuku, Hawaii · Featured Truck

Giovanni's Shrimp Truck

Cuisine: Shrimp
Signature: Garlic shrimp plate
Home base: Kahuku, Hawaii
Rolling since 1993

Some trucks chase trends; Giovanni's Shrimp Truck chases its regulars. Based in Kahuku, Hawaii, the Shrimp kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Giovanni's Shrimp Truck has been rolling since 1993. A North Shore landmark since 1993, when Giovanni's set the garlic-shrimp standard on Oahu.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Giovanni's Shrimp Truck and go straight to the garlic shrimp plate. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Kahuku is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Giovanni's Shrimp Truck has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Kahuku gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Everything on the Giovanni's Shrimp Truck menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Shrimp here tastes focused.

Start with a handful of names if you want to understand Kahuku's truck scene; Giovanni's Shrimp Truck is on the first page. Its influence shows in the trucks that followed and the routes eaters plan around its window.

Ask the regulars what keeps them coming back and the answers stack up fast: the cooking, yes, but also the remembering — names, orders, how you take it. Giovanni's Shrimp Truck runs on that kind of memory.

What makes Giovanni's Shrimp Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Shrimp out of Kahuku, with garlic shrimp plate as the order its name is attached to.

There is a reason Giovanni's Shrimp Truck sits in the featured tier of this record. Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points — the name a city reaches for when it explains its food truck scene to a stranger. Giovanni's Shrimp Truck is one of those names for Kahuku.

For the permanent record: Giovanni's Shrimp Truck serves Kahuku its Shrimp, and the garlic shrimp plate is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
