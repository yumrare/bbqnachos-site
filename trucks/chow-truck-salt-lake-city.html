Salt Lake City, Utah · Directory Entry

Chow Truck

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Chow plates
Home base: Salt Lake City, Utah

In Salt Lake City, Utah, the name Chow Truck means Asian fusion done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Chow Truck holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Chow Truck the dish is chow plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Everybody knows everybody at a Salt Lake City truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Chow Truck thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

Space on the Chow Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Asian fusion here tastes so focused.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Chow Truck's is easy to write: Asian fusion from Salt Lake City, Utah, with chow plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

When the quarterly review comes back to Chow Truck, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
