Allentown, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Geakers Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Geakers tacos
Home base: Allentown, Pennsylvania

The entry for Allentown, Pennsylvania reads Geakers Tacos: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Geakers Tacos the dish is geakers tacos. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In Allentown, a good truck is town news, and Geakers Tacos has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Geakers Tacos. The Mexican cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

What makes Geakers Tacos itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Mexican out of Allentown, with geakers tacos as the order its name is attached to.

Geakers Tacos is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
