Washington, District of Columbia · Featured Truck

Fojol Bros

Cuisine: Indian and global
Signature: Butter chicken and veggie curry
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Fojol Bros holds its home base in Washington, District of Columbia and serves Indian and global from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Fojol Bros the dish is butter chicken and veggie curry. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Washington's, and Fojol Bros holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

There is a discipline to Indian and global cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Fojol Bros works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Start with a handful of names if you want to understand Washington's truck scene; Fojol Bros is on the first page. Its influence shows in the trucks that followed and the routes eaters plan around its window.

There is a small ceremony to a stop at Fojol Bros: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers who are about to agree with you. Trucks that create rituals outlive trucks that only create meals.

The regulars say the real signature is the rhythm — truck posted up, line forming, window sliding open. Fojol Bros has it down to a ritual, and rituals turn good trucks into beloved ones.

No other truck in this record runs on Fojol Bros's exact combination: Indian and global out of Washington, with butter chicken and veggie curry as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands for Fojol Bros of Washington, District of Columbia: Indian and global by trade, butter chicken and veggie curry by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
