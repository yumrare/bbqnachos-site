The Mighty Cone

Cuisine: Fried cones
Signature: Chicken-avocado cones
Home base: Austin, Texas

The Mighty Cone never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Fried cones kitchen out of Austin, Texas runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The signature at The Mighty Cone is chicken-avocado cones, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Austin crosses town for.

Survival in Austin means being somebody's favorite, and The Mighty Cone is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Mighty Cone. The Fried cones cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Mighty Cone would still stand alone on its facts: a Fried cones kitchen based in Austin, known first and last for chicken-avocado cones.

The Mighty Cone is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
