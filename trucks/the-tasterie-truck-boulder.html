Boulder, Colorado · Directory Entry

The Tasterie Truck

Cuisine: Desserts
Signature: Tasterie sweets
Home base: Boulder, Colorado

Out of Boulder, Colorado, The Tasterie Truck serves Desserts to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at The Tasterie Truck the dish is tasterie sweets. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Boulder; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what The Tasterie Truck has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

There is a discipline to Desserts cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Tasterie Truck works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and The Tasterie Truck's writes itself: Desserts from Boulder, Colorado, with tasterie sweets as the dish the city attached to its name.

The quarterly review will come back to The Tasterie Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
