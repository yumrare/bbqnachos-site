Washington, District of Columbia · Directory Entry

CapMac

Cuisine: Pasta
Signature: Mac and cheese bowls
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Some trucks chase trends; CapMac chases its regulars. Based in Washington, District of Columbia, the Pasta kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the mac and cheese bowls. At CapMac, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the Washington scene rewards, and it is the reputation CapMac carries — a stop locals give directions by.

At CapMac the Pasta is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and CapMac's writes itself: Pasta from Washington, District of Columbia, with mac and cheese bowls as the dish the city attached to its name.

When the quarterly review comes back to CapMac, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
