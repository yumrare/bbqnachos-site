Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

Bunk Sandwiches

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: Pork belly cubano
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Ask around Portland where the Sandwiches cooking is and somebody points to Bunk Sandwiches before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Oregon, service after service, the only honest way there is.

If Bunk Sandwiches served nothing else, the record would read the same: pork belly cubano. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Portland runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Bunk Sandwiches holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

Space on the Bunk Sandwiches menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Sandwiches here tastes so focused.

What makes Bunk Sandwiches itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Sandwiches out of Portland, with pork belly cubano as the order its name is attached to.

When the quarterly review comes back to Bunk Sandwiches, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
