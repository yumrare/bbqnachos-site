Boise, Idaho · Directory Entry

Il Segreto

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Secret pies
Home base: Boise, Idaho

Il Segreto of Boise, Idaho earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Pizza kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Ask at the Il Segreto window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: secret pies. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A good truck is town news in Boise, and Il Segreto has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

What Il Segreto serves is Pizza stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

What makes Il Segreto itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Pizza out of Boise, with secret pies as the order its name is attached to.

Il Segreto is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
