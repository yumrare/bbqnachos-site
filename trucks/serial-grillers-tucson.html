Serial Grillers

Cuisine: Burgers and cheesesteaks
Signature: Serial cheesesteaks
Home base: Tucson, Arizona

Serial Grillers never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Burgers and cheesesteaks kitchen out of Tucson, Arizona runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

If Serial Grillers served nothing else, the record would read the same: serial cheesesteaks. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Tucson; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Serial Grillers has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Cooking Burgers and cheesesteaks from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Serial Grillers works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

Line up every truck in this record and Serial Grillers still stands alone on its facts: a Burgers and cheesesteaks kitchen based in Tucson, known first and last for serial cheesesteaks.

So the record stands for Serial Grillers of Tucson, Arizona: Burgers and cheesesteaks by trade, serial cheesesteaks by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
