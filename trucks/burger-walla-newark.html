Burger Walla

Cuisine: Indian burgers
Signature: Walla burgers
Home base: Newark, New Jersey

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Burger Walla of Newark, New Jersey is no exception. A Indian burgers kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Newark the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Ask at the Burger Walla window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: walla burgers. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Newark; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Burger Walla has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Indian burgers was made for the truck form, and Burger Walla leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

No other truck in this record runs on Burger Walla's exact combination: Indian burgers out of Newark, with walla burgers as the order its name is attached to.

Filed under New Jersey, in the Newark pages: Burger Walla. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
