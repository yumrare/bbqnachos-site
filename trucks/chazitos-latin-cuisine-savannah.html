Chazito's Latin Cuisine

Cuisine: Puerto Rican
Signature: Chazito's plates
Home base: Savannah, Georgia

Chazito's Latin Cuisine rolls out of Savannah, Georgia, serving Puerto Rican to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Chazito's Latin Cuisine the dish is chazito's plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Chazito's Latin Cuisine brings Savannah the wider truck world without the drive: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait their turn.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Puerto Rican was made for the truck form, and Chazito's Latin Cuisine leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Line up every truck in this record and Chazito's Latin Cuisine still stands alone on its facts: a Puerto Rican kitchen based in Savannah, known first and last for chazito's plates.

That is the entry for Chazito's Latin Cuisine: Savannah's own, a Puerto Rican truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
