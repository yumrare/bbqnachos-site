The Lobos Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Wachos (waffle-fry nachos)
Home base: Los Angeles, California

To eaters in Los Angeles, California, The Lobos Truck simply means American comfort done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The wachos (waffle-fry nachos) at The Lobos Truck is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Survival in Los Angeles means being somebody's favorite, and The Lobos Truck is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Lobos Truck: the American comfort is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

The Lobos Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Los Angeles as the stage, American comfort as the craft, and wachos (waffle-fry nachos) as the headline act.

So the record stands: The Lobos Truck, of Los Angeles, California — American comfort by trade, wachos (waffle-fry nachos) by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
