Hoss' Loaded Burgers

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Loaded burgers
Home base: Nashville, Tennessee

To eaters in Nashville, Tennessee, Hoss' Loaded Burgers simply means Burgers done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

What the window at Hoss' Loaded Burgers is famous for handing out is loaded burgers — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Nashville counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Hoss' Loaded Burgers is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

At Hoss' Loaded Burgers the Burgers is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Line up every truck in this record and Hoss' Loaded Burgers still stands alone on its facts: a Burgers kitchen based in Nashville, known first and last for loaded burgers.

That is the entry for Hoss' Loaded Burgers: Nashville's own, a Burgers truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
