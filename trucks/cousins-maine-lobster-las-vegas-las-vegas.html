Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas)

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Maine lobster roll
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Seafood kitchen out of Las Vegas, Nevada runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the maine lobster roll at Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Las Vegas runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) would still stand alone on its facts: a Seafood kitchen based in Las Vegas, known first and last for maine lobster roll.

The quarterly review will come back to Cousins Maine Lobster (Las Vegas) the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
