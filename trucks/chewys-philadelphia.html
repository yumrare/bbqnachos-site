Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Chewy's

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Philly comfort plates
Home base: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Chewy's keeps its home base in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and cooks American comfort the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Chewy's is carried by its philly comfort plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck survives in Philadelphia by being somebody's favorite, and Chewy's is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: American comfort was made for the truck form, and Chewy's leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Line up every truck in this record and Chewy's still stands alone on its facts: a American comfort kitchen based in Philadelphia, known first and last for philly comfort plates.

The quarterly review will come back to Chewy's the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
