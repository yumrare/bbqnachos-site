Art City Donuts

Cuisine: Donuts
Signature: Mini donuts
Home base: Springville, Utah

Art City Donuts keeps its home base in Springville, Utah, and cooks Donuts the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

The mini donuts at Art City Donuts is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Trucks like Art City Donuts are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Springville may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

At Art City Donuts the Donuts is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Art City Donuts does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Springville, Donuts, mini donuts — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Art City Donuts of Springville takes its place in the record — Donuts, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
