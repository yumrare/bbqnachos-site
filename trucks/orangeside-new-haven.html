New Haven, Connecticut · Directory Entry

Orangeside

Cuisine: Donuts
Signature: Square donuts
Home base: New Haven, Connecticut

Orangeside holds its home base in New Haven, Connecticut and serves Donuts from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Orangeside is carried by its square donuts. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Ask in New Haven where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Orangeside's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Donuts cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Orangeside leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Orangeside's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: New Haven as the stage, Donuts as the craft, and square donuts as the headline act.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Orangeside matter to the places they feed. Home base New Haven, Connecticut; cuisine Donuts; signature: square donuts — a small, complete portrait.
