Hewtin's Dogs

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Hewtin's dogs
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Out of Providence, Rhode Island, Hewtin's Dogs serves Hot dogs to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the Hewtin's Dogs window is hewtin's dogs. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Ask in Providence where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Hewtin's Dogs's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Hewtin's Dogs cooks like it knows it: Hot dogs with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

No other truck in this record runs on Hewtin's Dogs's exact combination: Hot dogs out of Providence, with hewtin's dogs as the order its name is attached to.

The entry sits in the Providence pages, filed under Rhode Island: Hewtin's Dogs. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
