Richmond, Virginia · Directory Entry

The Dog Wagon

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: RVA dogs
Home base: Richmond, Virginia

To eaters in Richmond, Virginia, The Dog Wagon simply means Hot dogs done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

If The Dog Wagon could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: rva dogs. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

In Richmond, a good truck is town news, and The Dog Wagon has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Cooking Hot dogs from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and The Dog Wagon works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The Dog Wagon's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Richmond as the stage, Hot dogs as the craft, and rva dogs as the headline act.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. The Dog Wagon of Richmond holds its place on Hot dogs, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
