Des Moines, Iowa · Directory Entry

Big Red Food Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Big red plates
Home base: Des Moines, Iowa

Big Red Food Truck keeps its home base in Des Moines, Iowa, and cooks American comfort the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Ask at the Big Red Food Truck window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: big red plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A good truck is town news in Des Moines, and Big Red Food Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

The kitchen at Big Red Food Truck treats American comfort as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Big Red Food Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Des Moines, American comfort, big red plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Big Red Food Truck of Des Moines takes its place in the record — American comfort, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
