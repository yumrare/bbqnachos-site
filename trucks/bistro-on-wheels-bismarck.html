Bismarck, North Dakota · Directory Entry

Bistro on Wheels

Cuisine: Bistro comfort
Signature: Bistro plates
Home base: Bismarck, North Dakota

Bistro on Wheels never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Bistro comfort kitchen out of Bismarck, North Dakota runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

What the window at Bistro on Wheels is famous for handing out is bistro plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

In a place the size of Bismarck, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Bistro on Wheels is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Bistro on Wheels cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

Bistro on Wheels does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Bismarck, Bistro comfort, bistro plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Bistro on Wheels is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps.
