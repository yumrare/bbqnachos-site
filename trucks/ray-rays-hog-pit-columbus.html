Ray Ray's Hog Pit

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Smoked brisket and ribs
Home base: Columbus, Ohio

Ask around Columbus where the Barbecue cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Ray Ray's Hog Pit. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Ohio, service after service.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Ray Ray's Hog Pit and go straight to the smoked brisket and ribs. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

In Columbus, a good truck is town news, and Ray Ray's Hog Pit has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Space on the Ray Ray's Hog Pit menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Barbecue here tastes so focused.

There is a reason Ray Ray's Hog Pit sits in the featured tier of this record. Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points — the name a city reaches for when it explains its food truck scene to a stranger. Ray Ray's Hog Pit is one of those names for Columbus.

A stop at Ray Ray's Hog Pit has a small ceremony to it: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers about to agree with you. Rituals outlive meals.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Ray Ray's Hog Pit runs on that kind of memory.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Ray Ray's Hog Pit's content is Barbecue cooked in Columbus, Ohio, and judged by one dish above the rest: smoked brisket and ribs.
