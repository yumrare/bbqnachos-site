The Middle Feast

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Shawarma and falafel wraps
Home base: Los Angeles, California

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Middle Feast of Los Angeles, California is no exception. A Middle Eastern kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Los Angeles the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

If The Middle Feast served nothing else, the record would read the same: shawarma and falafel wraps. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Survival in Los Angeles means being somebody's favorite, and The Middle Feast is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and The Middle Feast cooks like it knows it: Middle Eastern with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Line up every truck in this record and The Middle Feast still stands alone on its facts: a Middle Eastern kitchen based in Los Angeles, known first and last for shawarma and falafel wraps.

So the record stands: The Middle Feast, of Los Angeles, California — Middle Eastern by trade, shawarma and falafel wraps by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
