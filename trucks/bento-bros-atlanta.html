Atlanta, Georgia · Directory Entry

Bento Bros

Cuisine: Japanese
Signature: Bento boxes
Home base: Atlanta, Georgia

Bento Bros is a Japanese food truck based in Atlanta, Georgia. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Atlanta, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The bento boxes at Bento Bros is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Atlanta's, and Bento Bros holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

Everything on the Bento Bros menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Japanese here tastes focused.

Line up every truck in this record and Bento Bros still stands alone on its facts: a Japanese kitchen based in Atlanta, known first and last for bento boxes.

Filed under Georgia, in the Atlanta pages: Bento Bros. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
