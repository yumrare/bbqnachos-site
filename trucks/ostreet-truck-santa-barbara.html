Santa Barbara, California · Directory Entry

Ostreet Truck

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: O Street plates
Home base: Santa Barbara, California

To eaters in Santa Barbara, California, Ostreet Truck simply means Global comfort done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the o street plates at Ostreet Truck lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

In Santa Barbara, a good truck is town news, and Ostreet Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Ostreet Truck cooks like it knows it: Global comfort with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Ostreet Truck's writes itself: Global comfort from Santa Barbara, California, with o street plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Ostreet Truck: Santa Barbara's own, a Global comfort truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
