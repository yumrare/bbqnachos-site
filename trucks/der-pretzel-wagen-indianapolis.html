Der Pretzel Wagen

Cuisine: German pretzels
Signature: Pretzel sandwiches
Home base: Indianapolis, Indiana

Some trucks chase trends; Der Pretzel Wagen chases its regulars. Based in Indianapolis, Indiana, the German pretzels kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the pretzel sandwiches at Der Pretzel Wagen lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Der Pretzel Wagen brings Indianapolis the wider truck world without the drive: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait their turn.

At Der Pretzel Wagen the German pretzels is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Der Pretzel Wagen would still stand alone on its facts: a German pretzels kitchen based in Indianapolis, known first and last for pretzel sandwiches.

So the record stands: Der Pretzel Wagen, of Indianapolis, Indiana — German pretzels by trade, pretzel sandwiches by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
