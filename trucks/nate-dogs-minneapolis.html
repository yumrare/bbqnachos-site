Nate Dogs

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Nate's mustard dogs
Home base: Minneapolis, Minnesota

Nate Dogs rolls out of Minneapolis, Minnesota, serving Hot dogs to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at Nate Dogs is famous for handing out is nate's mustard dogs — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the Minneapolis scene rewards, and it is the reputation Nate Dogs carries — a stop locals give directions by.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Nate Dogs. The Hot dogs cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Nate Dogs's writes itself: Hot dogs from Minneapolis, Minnesota, with nate's mustard dogs as the dish the city attached to its name.

Filed under Minnesota, in the Minneapolis pages: Nate Dogs. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
