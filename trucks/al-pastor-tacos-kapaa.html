Kapa'a, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Al Pastor Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Al pastor tacos
Home base: Kapa'a, Hawaii

Out of Kapa'a, Hawaii, Al Pastor Tacos serves Mexican to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

If Al Pastor Tacos could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: al pastor tacos. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in Kapa'a answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Al Pastor Tacos thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Al Pastor Tacos cooks like it knows it: Mexican with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

No theme song, no mascot: Al Pastor Tacos's identity is written in three facts — Kapa'a, Mexican, al pastor tacos — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Al Pastor Tacos matter to the places they feed. Home base Kapa'a, Hawaii; cuisine Mexican; signature: al pastor tacos — a small, complete portrait.
