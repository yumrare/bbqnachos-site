Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Bing of Fire

Cuisine: Chinese crepes
Signature: Jianbing on the go
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Ask around Seattle where the Chinese crepes cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Bing of Fire. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Washington, service after service.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Bing of Fire is carried by its jianbing on the go. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Competition in Seattle is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Bing of Fire has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

What Bing of Fire serves is Chinese crepes stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Bing of Fire would still stand alone on its facts: a Chinese crepes kitchen based in Seattle, known first and last for jianbing on the go.

Filed under Washington, in the Seattle pages: Bing of Fire. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
