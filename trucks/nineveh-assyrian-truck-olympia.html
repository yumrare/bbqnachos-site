Nineveh Assyrian Truck

Cuisine: Assyrian
Signature: Nineveh plates
Home base: Olympia, Washington

Nineveh Assyrian Truck holds its home base in Olympia, Washington and serves Assyrian from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the Nineveh Assyrian Truck window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: nineveh plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

In a place the size of Olympia, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Nineveh Assyrian Truck is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Assyrian was made for the truck form, and Nineveh Assyrian Truck leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Nineveh Assyrian Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Olympia as the stage, Assyrian as the craft, and nineveh plates as the headline act.

The entry sits in the Olympia pages, filed under Washington: Nineveh Assyrian Truck. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
