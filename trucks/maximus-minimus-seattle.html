Maximus Minimus

Cuisine: Pulled pork
Signature: Pulled pork from the pig truck
Home base: Seattle, Washington

In Seattle, Washington, the name Maximus Minimus means Pulled pork done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Maximus Minimus holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The signature at Maximus Minimus is pulled pork from the pig truck, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Seattle crosses town for.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Seattle, and Maximus Minimus has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Space on the Maximus Minimus menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Pulled pork here tastes so focused.

This directory's front row is the featured tier, and Seattle put Maximus Minimus in it — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Maximus Minimus runs on that kind of memory.

Consistency keeps Maximus Minimus in the record: the signature tastes like itself every time, the welcome is the same, the truck shows up where it says it will. Reliability is its own kind of fame.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Maximus Minimus's content is specific — Pulled pork cooked in Seattle, Washington, judged by one dish above the rest: pulled pork from the pig truck.

Ask a Seattle local to name the trucks that matter and Maximus Minimus comes early in the list — not out of habit, but out of a decade of lunches that landed right.

That is the entry for Maximus Minimus — Seattle's own, a Pulled pork truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
