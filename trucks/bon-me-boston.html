Boston, Massachusetts · Featured Truck

Bon Me

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Bon Me banh mi
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts
Rolling since 2011

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Bon Me of Boston, Massachusetts is no exception. A Vietnamese kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Boston the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Bon Me has been rolling since 2011. Won Boston's food truck contest to launch, and became a fixture of the city's lunch map.

If Bon Me served nothing else, the record would read the same: bon me banh mi. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck survives in Boston by being somebody's favorite, and Bon Me is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Vietnamese from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Bon Me works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

To understand Boston's truck scene, you start with a handful of names, and Bon Me is on that first page. Its influence shows up in the trucks that came after it and in the eaters who plan their routes around its window.

Consistency keeps Bon Me in the record: the signature tastes like itself every time, the welcome is the same, the truck shows up where it says it will. Reliability is its own kind of fame.

No theme song, no mascot: Bon Me's identity is written in three facts — Boston, Vietnamese, bon me banh mi — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points. Bon Me is one for Boston — the name the city reaches for when it explains its truck scene to a stranger.

That is the entry for Bon Me — Boston's own, a Vietnamese truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
