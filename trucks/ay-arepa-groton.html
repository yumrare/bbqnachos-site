Groton, Connecticut · Directory Entry

Ay! Arepa

Cuisine: Venezuelan
Signature: Arepas
Home base: Groton, Connecticut

In Groton, Connecticut, the name Ay! Arepa means Venezuelan done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Ay! Arepa holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the arepas at Ay! Arepa lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Everybody knows everybody at a Groton truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Ay! Arepa thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

What Ay! Arepa serves is Venezuelan stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Ay! Arepa's is easy to write: Venezuelan from Groton, Connecticut, with arepas as the dish the city attached to its name.

So the record stands: Ay! Arepa, of Groton, Connecticut — Venezuelan by trade, arepas by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
