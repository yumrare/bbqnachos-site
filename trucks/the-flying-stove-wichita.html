The Flying Stove

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: ICT street plates
Home base: Wichita, Kansas

The entry for Wichita, Kansas reads The Flying Stove: a Global comfort truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Ask at the The Flying Stove window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: ict street plates. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Ask in Wichita where to eat and the directions often end at a window: The Flying Stove's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

What The Flying Stove serves is Global comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points. The Flying Stove is one for Wichita — the name the city reaches for when it explains its truck scene to a stranger.
