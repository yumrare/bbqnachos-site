Washington, District of Columbia · Directory Entry

Basil Thyme

Cuisine: Italian
Signature: Lasagna cups
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Basil Thyme keeps its home base in Washington, District of Columbia, and cooks Italian the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Basil Thyme is carried by its lasagna cups. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

In a scene as crowded as Washington's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Basil Thyme draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Basil Thyme. The Italian cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Line up every truck in this record and Basil Thyme still stands alone on its facts: a Italian kitchen based in Washington, known first and last for lasagna cups.

So the record stands for Basil Thyme of Washington, District of Columbia: Italian by trade, lasagna cups by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
