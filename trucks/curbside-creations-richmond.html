Richmond, Virginia · Directory Entry

Curbside Creations

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Curbside plates
Home base: Richmond, Virginia

Curbside Creations rolls out of Richmond, Virginia, serving American comfort to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Curbside Creations the dish is curbside plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

A truck in Richmond answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Curbside Creations thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

What Curbside Creations serves is American comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No theme song, no mascot: Curbside Creations's identity is written in three facts — Richmond, American comfort, curbside plates — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Curbside Creations matter to the places they feed. Home base Richmond, Virginia; cuisine American comfort; signature: curbside plates — a small, complete portrait.
