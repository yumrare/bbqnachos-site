Palmetto Smoke

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Palmetto smoked plates
Home base: Columbia, South Carolina

To eaters in Columbia, South Carolina, Palmetto Smoke simply means Barbecue done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Palmetto Smoke is carried by its palmetto smoked plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Trucks like Palmetto Smoke are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Columbia may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Palmetto Smoke cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.
