Blue Ridge Bowls

Cuisine: Rice bowls
Signature: Blue Ridge bowls
Home base: Asheville, North Carolina

Blue Ridge Bowls never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Rice bowls kitchen out of Asheville, North Carolina runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the Blue Ridge Bowls window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: blue ridge bowls. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Ask in Asheville where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Blue Ridge Bowls's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Blue Ridge Bowls. The Rice bowls cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Line up every truck in this record and Blue Ridge Bowls still stands alone on its facts: a Rice bowls kitchen based in Asheville, known first and last for blue ridge bowls.

The quarterly review will come back to Blue Ridge Bowls the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
