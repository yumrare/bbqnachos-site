Provo, Utah · Directory Entry

Waffle Love

Cuisine: Waffles
Signature: The Red Wonder waffle
Home base: Provo, Utah

Read the Provo, Utah pages and you will find Waffle Love: a Waffles truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

If Waffle Love served nothing else, the record would read the same: the red wonder waffle. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in a place the size of Provo becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Waffle Love is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Space on the Waffle Love menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Waffles here tastes so focused.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Waffle Love's writes itself: Waffles from Provo, Utah, with the red wonder waffle as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Waffle Love: Provo's own, a Waffles truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
