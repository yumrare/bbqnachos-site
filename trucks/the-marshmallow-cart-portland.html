The Marshmallow Cart

Cuisine: Desserts
Signature: Torched marshmallows
Home base: Portland, Maine

The entry for Portland, Maine reads The Marshmallow Cart: a Desserts truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

torched marshmallows: that is the signature at The Marshmallow Cart, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Portland crosses town for.

Portland runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and The Marshmallow Cart holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

Space on the The Marshmallow Cart menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Desserts here tastes so focused.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and The Marshmallow Cart's content is specific — Desserts cooked in Portland, Maine, judged by one dish above the rest: torched marshmallows.

The entry sits in the Portland pages, filed under Maine: The Marshmallow Cart. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
