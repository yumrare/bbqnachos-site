Tillamook Melt Wagon

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Coastal melts
Home base: Tillamook, Oregon

Tillamook Melt Wagon keeps its home base in Tillamook, Oregon, and cooks Grilled cheese the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Tillamook Melt Wagon the dish is coastal melts. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Tillamook; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Tillamook Melt Wagon has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

There is a discipline to Grilled cheese cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Tillamook Melt Wagon works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

What makes Tillamook Melt Wagon itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Grilled cheese out of Tillamook, with coastal melts as the order its name is attached to.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Tillamook Melt Wagon matter to the places they feed. Home base Tillamook, Oregon; cuisine Grilled cheese; signature: coastal melts — a small, complete portrait.
