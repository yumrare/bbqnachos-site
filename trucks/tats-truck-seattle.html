Seattle, Washington · Directory Entry

Tat's Truck

Cuisine: Deli
Signature: Tat'strami sandwiches
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Some trucks chase trends; Tat's Truck chases its regulars. Based in Seattle, Washington, the Deli kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The signature at Tat's Truck is tat'strami sandwiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Seattle crosses town for.

A truck survives in Seattle by being somebody's favorite, and Tat's Truck is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Cooking Deli from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Tat's Truck works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Tat's Truck's writes itself: Deli from Seattle, Washington, with tat'strami sandwiches as the dish the city attached to its name.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Tat's Truck matter to the places they feed. Home base Seattle, Washington; cuisine Deli; signature: tat'strami sandwiches. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
