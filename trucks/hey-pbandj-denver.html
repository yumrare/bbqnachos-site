Denver, Colorado · Directory Entry

Hey PB&J

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: Gourmet PB&J sandwiches
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Read the Denver, Colorado pages and you will find Hey PB&J: a Sandwiches truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Hey PB&J is carried by its gourmet pb&j sandwiches. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

A truck in Denver is a neighborhood before it is a business — Hey PB&J has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

There is a discipline to Sandwiches cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Hey PB&J works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The originality of Hey PB&J is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Denver as the stage, Sandwiches as the craft, and gourmet pb&j sandwiches as the headline.

The quarterly review will come back to Hey PB&J the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
