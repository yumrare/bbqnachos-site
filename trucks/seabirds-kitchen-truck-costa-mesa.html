Seabirds Kitchen Truck

Cuisine: Vegan
Signature: Beer-battered avocado tacos
Home base: Costa Mesa, California

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Seabirds Kitchen Truck of Costa Mesa, California is no exception. A Vegan kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Costa Mesa the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

What the window at Seabirds Kitchen Truck is famous for handing out is beer-battered avocado tacos — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Ask in Costa Mesa where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Seabirds Kitchen Truck's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Seabirds Kitchen Truck. The Vegan cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Plant-based is the whole point at Seabirds Kitchen Truck: a vegan menu built from the ground up, which is why its line includes plenty of people who never thought they would queue for one.

No other truck in this record runs on Seabirds Kitchen Truck's exact combination: Vegan out of Costa Mesa, with beer-battered avocado tacos as the order its name is attached to.

Seabirds Kitchen Truck is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
