Roxy's Grilled Cheese

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Green Muenster melt
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts
Rolling since 2011

Ask around Boston where the Grilled cheese cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Roxy's Grilled Cheese. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Massachusetts, service after service.

Roxy's Grilled Cheese has been rolling since 2011. Boston's grilled cheese pioneers, raced on the first season of The Great Food Truck Race.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Roxy's Grilled Cheese is carried by its green muenster melt. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Competition in Boston is a compliment — it means the eaters are serious — and Roxy's Grilled Cheese has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Grilled cheese cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Roxy's Grilled Cheese leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Ask a Boston local to name the trucks that matter and Roxy's Grilled Cheese comes early in the list — not out of habit, but out of a decade of lunches that landed right.

Regulars will tell you the real signature is the rhythm: the truck posted up, the line forming, the window sliding open. Roxy's Grilled Cheese has that rhythm down to a ritual, and rituals are what turn a good truck into a beloved one.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Roxy's Grilled Cheese's content is Grilled cheese cooked in Boston, Massachusetts, and judged by one dish above the rest: green muenster melt.

Plenty of trucks cook well; fewer become reference points. Roxy's Grilled Cheese is one for Boston — the name the city reaches for when it explains its truck scene to a stranger.

So the record stands for Roxy's Grilled Cheese of Boston, Massachusetts: Grilled cheese by trade, green muenster melt by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
