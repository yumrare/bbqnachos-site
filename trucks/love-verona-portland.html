Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

Love Verona

Cuisine: Italian
Signature: Wood-fired pizza
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Some trucks chase trends; Love Verona chases its regulars. Based in Portland, Oregon, the Italian kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The wood-fired pizza at Love Verona is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Portland's, and Love Verona holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

Love Verona serves Italian stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No other truck in this record runs on Love Verona's exact combination: Italian out of Portland, with wood-fired pizza as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Love Verona — Portland's own, a Italian truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
