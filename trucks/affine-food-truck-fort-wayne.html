Fort Wayne, Indiana · Directory Entry

Affine Food Truck

Cuisine: New American
Signature: Chef plates on wheels
Home base: Fort Wayne, Indiana

Affine Food Truck keeps its home base in Fort Wayne, Indiana, and cooks New American the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Affine Food Truck the dish is chef plates on wheels. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

A good truck is town news in Fort Wayne, and Affine Food Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Space on the Affine Food Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the New American here tastes so focused.

Line up every truck in this record and Affine Food Truck still stands alone on its facts: a New American kitchen based in Fort Wayne, known first and last for chef plates on wheels.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Affine Food Truck matter to the places they feed. Home base Fort Wayne, Indiana; cuisine New American; signature: chef plates on wheels — a small, complete portrait.
