Sacramento, California · Directory Entry

Chando's Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Chando's mulitas
Home base: Sacramento, California

Read the Sacramento, California pages and you will find Chando's Tacos: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

If Chando's Tacos could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: chando's mulitas. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

In a place the size of Sacramento, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Chando's Tacos is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Mexican cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Chando's Tacos leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Chando's Tacos's content is Mexican cooked in Sacramento, California, and judged by one dish above the rest: chando's mulitas.

That is the entry for Chando's Tacos — Sacramento's own, a Mexican truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
