FoodHeads

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: FoodHeads stacks
Home base: Austin, Texas

FoodHeads of Austin, Texas earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Sandwiches kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at FoodHeads the dish is foodheads stacks. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Survival in Austin means being somebody's favorite, and FoodHeads is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at FoodHeads. The Sandwiches cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The originality of FoodHeads is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Austin as the stage, Sandwiches as the craft, and foodheads stacks as the headline.

For the permanent record: FoodHeads serves Austin its Sandwiches, and the foodheads stacks is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
