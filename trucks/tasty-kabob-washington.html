Tasty Kabob

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Kabob plates
Home base: Washington, District of Columbia

Some trucks chase trends; Tasty Kabob chases its regulars. Based in Washington, District of Columbia, the Middle Eastern kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the window what Tasty Kabob is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: kabob plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A Washington truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Tasty Kabob draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Everything on the Tasty Kabob menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Middle Eastern here tastes focused.

No theme song, no mascot: Tasty Kabob's identity is written in three facts — Washington, Middle Eastern, kabob plates — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

So the record stands for Tasty Kabob of Washington, District of Columbia: Middle Eastern by trade, kabob plates by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
