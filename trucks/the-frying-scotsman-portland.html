Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

The Frying Scotsman

Cuisine: Fish and chips
Signature: Scotsman fish supper
Home base: Portland, Oregon

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and The Frying Scotsman of Portland, Oregon is no exception. A Fish and chips kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Portland the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Ask at the The Frying Scotsman window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: scotsman fish supper. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Portland has trucks the way other cities have landmarks, and The Frying Scotsman is counted among them. Its corner of the scene is the dependable kind: regulars who bring out-of-towners here first, because this is the stop that explains the city.

There is a discipline to Fish and chips cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. The Frying Scotsman works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The Frying Scotsman does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Portland, Fish and chips, scotsman fish supper — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. The Frying Scotsman of Portland holds its place on Fish and chips, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
