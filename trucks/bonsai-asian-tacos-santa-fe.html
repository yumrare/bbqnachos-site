Santa Fe, New Mexico · Directory Entry

Bonsai Asian Tacos

Cuisine: Asian-Mexican
Signature: Bonsai tacos
Home base: Santa Fe, New Mexico

Bonsai Asian Tacos keeps its home base in Santa Fe, New Mexico, and cooks Asian-Mexican the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Bonsai Asian Tacos is carried by its bonsai tacos. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Ask in Santa Fe where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Bonsai Asian Tacos's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Bonsai Asian Tacos: the Asian-Mexican is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Bonsai Asian Tacos's content is Asian-Mexican cooked in Santa Fe, New Mexico, and judged by one dish above the rest: bonsai tacos.

The entry sits in the Santa Fe pages, filed under New Mexico: Bonsai Asian Tacos. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
