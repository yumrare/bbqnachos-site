Steel City Smoke

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Steel City smoked plates
Home base: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

In Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the name Steel City Smoke means Barbecue done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Steel City Smoke holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Steel City Smoke the dish is steel city smoked plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

A truck in Pittsburgh answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Steel City Smoke thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Barbecue was made for the truck form, and Steel City Smoke leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Steel City Smoke would still stand alone on its facts: a Barbecue kitchen based in Pittsburgh, known first and last for steel city smoked plates.

So the record stands: Steel City Smoke, of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania — Barbecue by trade, steel city smoked plates by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
