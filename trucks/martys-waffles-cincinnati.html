Marty's Waffles

Cuisine: Waffles
Signature: Marty's waffle sandwiches
Home base: Cincinnati, Ohio

To eaters in Cincinnati, Ohio, Marty's Waffles simply means Waffles done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

What the window at Marty's Waffles is famous for handing out is marty's waffle sandwiches — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

A truck in a place the size of Cincinnati becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Marty's Waffles is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

At Marty's Waffles, Waffles is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Marty's Waffles's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Cincinnati as the stage, Waffles as the craft, and marty's waffle sandwiches as the headline act.
