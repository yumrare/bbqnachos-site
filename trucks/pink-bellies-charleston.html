Charleston, South Carolina · Directory Entry

Pink Bellies

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Pink bellies bowls
Home base: Charleston, South Carolina

Home base Charleston, South Carolina; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Pink Bellies cooks Vietnamese the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the pink bellies bowls at Pink Bellies lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Charleston eats above its weight because of trucks like Pink Bellies. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Vietnamese cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Pink Bellies leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

What makes Pink Bellies itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Vietnamese out of Charleston, with pink bellies bowls as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Pink Bellies: Charleston's own, a Vietnamese truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
