Charlottesville, Virginia · Directory Entry

SpiceSea Gourmet

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: SpiceSea plates
Home base: Charlottesville, Virginia

SpiceSea Gourmet of Charlottesville, Virginia earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Seafood kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

The signature at SpiceSea Gourmet is spicesea plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Charlottesville crosses town for.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Charlottesville; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what SpiceSea Gourmet has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at SpiceSea Gourmet. The Seafood cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and SpiceSea Gourmet's is easy to write: Seafood from Charlottesville, Virginia, with spicesea plates as the dish the city attached to its name.

The entry sits in the Charlottesville pages, filed under Virginia: SpiceSea Gourmet. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
