Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas)

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Maine lobster roll
Home base: Dallas, Texas

Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas) keeps its home base in Dallas, Texas, and cooks Seafood the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas) the dish is maine lobster roll. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In a scene as crowded as Dallas's, a truck's address is wherever its line forms, and Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas) draws one. Lunch crowds, market days, the after-game drift — it catches them all without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas) cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas)'s originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Dallas as the stage, Seafood as the craft, and maine lobster roll as the headline act.

So the record stands: Cousins Maine Lobster (Dallas), of Dallas, Texas — Seafood by trade, maine lobster roll by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
