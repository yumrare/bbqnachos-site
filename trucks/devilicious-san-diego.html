San Diego, California · Featured Truck

Devilicious

Cuisine: Comfort fusion
Signature: Butter-poached lobster grilled cheese
Home base: San Diego, California
Rolling since 2010

Home base San Diego, California; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Devilicious cooks Comfort fusion the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

Devilicious has been rolling since 2010. San Diego's butter-poached lobster grilled cheese raced on The Great Food Truck Race.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the butter-poached lobster grilled cheese at Devilicious lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the San Diego scene rewards, and it is the reputation Devilicious carries — a stop locals give directions by.

Everything on the Devilicious menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Comfort fusion here tastes focused.

Popular is one thing; foundational is another. A generation of San Diego eaters learned what a food truck could be at the Devilicious window, and the lesson stuck.

The regulars say the real signature is the rhythm — truck posted up, line forming, window sliding open. Devilicious has it down to a ritual, and rituals turn good trucks into beloved ones.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Devilicious would still stand alone on its facts: a Comfort fusion kitchen based in San Diego, known first and last for butter-poached lobster grilled cheese.

The featured tier holds the trucks other trucks get measured against. Devilicious earned its standing the only way it can be earned: year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

This directory's front row is the featured tier, and San Diego put Devilicious in it — by lining up, by coming back, and by telling everybody else to do the same.

Devilicious is recorded here with its home base, its cuisine, and its signature, as part of the permanent record this directory keeps. Trucks hold their lines the same way they earned them — by still being the trucks their cities say they are — and the quarterly review keeps the record honest.
