Costa Mesa, California · Directory Entry

Taco Maria

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Alta-California tacos
Home base: Costa Mesa, California

Taco Maria of Costa Mesa, California earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Mexican kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

The alta-california tacos at Taco Maria is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Costa Mesa; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Taco Maria has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Mexican cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Taco Maria leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Taco Maria's is easy to write: Mexican from Costa Mesa, California, with alta-california tacos as the dish the city attached to its name.

Filed under California, in the Costa Mesa pages: Taco Maria. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
