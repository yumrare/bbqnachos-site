Worcester, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

El Patron

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Worcester tacos
Home base: Worcester, Massachusetts

Home base Worcester, Massachusetts; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. El Patron cooks Mexican the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at El Patron the dish is worcester tacos. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Everybody knows everybody at a Worcester truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. El Patron thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Mexican was made for the truck form, and El Patron leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and El Patron's writes itself: Mexican from Worcester, Massachusetts, with worcester tacos as the dish the city attached to its name.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. El Patron of Worcester takes its place in the record — Mexican, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
