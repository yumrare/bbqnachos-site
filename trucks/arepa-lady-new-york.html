Cuisine: Colombian
Signature: Arepa de choclo with cheese
Home base: New York, New York
Rolling since 1990

Arepa Lady never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Colombian kitchen out of New York, New York runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.
