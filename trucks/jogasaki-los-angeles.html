Los Angeles, California · Directory Entry

Jogasaki

Cuisine: Japanese-Mexican
Signature: Sushi burrito
Home base: Los Angeles, California

To eaters in Los Angeles, California, Jogasaki simply means Japanese-Mexican done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The famous hand-out at the Jogasaki window is sushi burrito. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Los Angeles runs one of the deepest food truck scenes in the country, and Jogasaki holds its corner of it. Office crowds at lunch, families at the evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out — the truck works the full rhythm of the city's street food life, and its regulars treat its stops like standing appointments.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Jogasaki. The Japanese-Mexican cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Jogasaki's is easy to write: Japanese-Mexican from Los Angeles, California, with sushi burrito as the dish the city attached to its name.

The entry sits in the Los Angeles pages, filed under California: Jogasaki. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
