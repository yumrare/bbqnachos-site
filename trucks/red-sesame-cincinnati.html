Cincinnati, Ohio · Directory Entry

Red Sesame

Cuisine: Korean barbecue
Signature: Korean tacos and bowls
Home base: Cincinnati, Ohio

Red Sesame is a Korean barbecue food truck based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Cincinnati, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Red Sesame is carried by its korean tacos and bowls. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Trucks like Red Sesame are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Cincinnati may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

What Red Sesame serves is Korean barbecue stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

What makes Red Sesame itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Korean barbecue out of Cincinnati, with korean tacos and bowls as the order its name is attached to.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Red Sesame of Cincinnati holds its place on Korean barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
