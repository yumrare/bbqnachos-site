Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck

Cuisine: Ice cream
Signature: Sweet cow scoops
Home base: Boulder, Colorado

Read the Boulder, Colorado pages and you will find Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck: a Ice cream truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck the dish is sweet cow scoops. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Everybody knows everybody at a Boulder truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

What Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck serves is Ice cream stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Line up every truck in this record and Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck still stands alone on its facts: a Ice cream kitchen based in Boulder, known first and last for sweet cow scoops.

That is the entry for Sweet Cow Ice Cream Truck — Boulder's own, a Ice cream truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
