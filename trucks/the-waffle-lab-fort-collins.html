The Waffle Lab

Cuisine: Waffles
Signature: Liege waffle plates
Home base: Fort Collins, Colorado

The Waffle Lab is a Waffles food truck based in Fort Collins, Colorado. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Fort Collins, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The signature at The Waffle Lab is liege waffle plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Fort Collins crosses town for.

In Fort Collins, a good truck is town news, and The Waffle Lab has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

The Waffle Lab serves Waffles stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Waffle Lab would still stand alone on its facts: a Waffles kitchen based in Fort Collins, known first and last for liege waffle plates.

So the record stands: The Waffle Lab, of Fort Collins, Colorado — Waffles by trade, liege waffle plates by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
