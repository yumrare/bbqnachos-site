Jersey City, New Jersey · Directory Entry

Two Crepes

Cuisine: Crepes
Signature: Downtown crepes
Home base: Jersey City, New Jersey

To eaters in Jersey City, New Jersey, Two Crepes simply means Crepes done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the downtown crepes. At Two Crepes, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck in a place the size of Jersey City becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Two Crepes is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Cooking Crepes from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Two Crepes works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Two Crepes's is easy to write: Crepes from Jersey City, New Jersey, with downtown crepes as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Two Crepes: Jersey City's own, a Crepes truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
