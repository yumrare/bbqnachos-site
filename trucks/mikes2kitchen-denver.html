Mikes2Kitchen

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Mikes plates
Home base: Denver, Colorado

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Mikes2Kitchen of Denver, Colorado is no exception. A Global comfort kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Denver the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Mikes2Kitchen the dish is mikes plates. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Survival in Denver means being somebody's favorite, and Mikes2Kitchen is exactly that: a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

At Mikes2Kitchen the Global comfort is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Mikes2Kitchen's content is Global comfort cooked in Denver, Colorado, and judged by one dish above the rest: mikes plates.

Filed under Colorado, in the Denver pages: Mikes2Kitchen. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
