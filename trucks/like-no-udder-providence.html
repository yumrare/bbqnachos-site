Like No Udder

Cuisine: Vegan ice cream
Signature: Coconut-based scoops
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Like No Udder of Providence, Rhode Island earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Vegan ice cream kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Like No Udder is carried by its coconut-based scoops. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Ask in Providence where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Like No Udder's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

Speed is part of the recipe at Like No Udder — Vegan ice cream cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Every dish at Like No Udder is plant-based. It is a vegan kitchen by design rather than substitution, and its following drives past a dozen other windows to reach this one.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Like No Udder's content is specific — Vegan ice cream cooked in Providence, Rhode Island, judged by one dish above the rest: coconut-based scoops.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Like No Udder of Providence holds its place on Vegan ice cream, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
