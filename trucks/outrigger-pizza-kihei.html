Outrigger Pizza

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Outrigger pies
Home base: Kihei, Hawaii

Outrigger Pizza keeps its home base in Kihei, Hawaii, and cooks Pizza the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Ask at the window what Outrigger Pizza is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: outrigger pies. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

What Outrigger Pizza gives Kihei is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Outrigger Pizza cooks like it knows it: Pizza with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

What makes Outrigger Pizza itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Pizza out of Kihei, with outrigger pies as the order its name is attached to.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Outrigger Pizza matter to the places they feed. Home base Kihei, Hawaii; cuisine Pizza; signature: outrigger pies — a small, complete portrait.
