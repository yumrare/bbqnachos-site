St. Louis, Missouri · Directory Entry

Cha Cha Chow

Cuisine: Global tacos
Signature: Rotating world tacos
Home base: St. Louis, Missouri

Cha Cha Chow of St. Louis, Missouri earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Global tacos kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the rotating world tacos at Cha Cha Chow lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

Cha Cha Chow brings St. Louis the wider truck world without the drive: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait their turn.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Cha Cha Chow: the Global tacos is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Cha Cha Chow's content is specific — Global tacos cooked in St. Louis, Missouri, judged by one dish above the rest: rotating world tacos.

That is the entry for Cha Cha Chow: St. Louis's own, a Global tacos truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
