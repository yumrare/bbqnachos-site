Worcester, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

The Dogfather

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Loaded Worcester dogs
Home base: Worcester, Massachusetts

The Dogfather holds its home base in Worcester, Massachusetts and serves Hot dogs from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the The Dogfather window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: loaded worcester dogs. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck in a place the size of Worcester becomes a landmark with a schedule, and The Dogfather is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

At The Dogfather the Hot dogs is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and The Dogfather's is easy to write: Hot dogs from Worcester, Massachusetts, with loaded worcester dogs as the dish the city attached to its name.

The entry sits in the Worcester pages, filed under Massachusetts: The Dogfather. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
