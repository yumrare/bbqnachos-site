San Diego, California · Directory Entry

MIHO Gastrotruck

Cuisine: Farm-to-table
Signature: The MIHO burger
Home base: San Diego, California

MIHO Gastrotruck rolls out of San Diego, California, serving Farm-to-table to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The signature at MIHO Gastrotruck is the miho burger, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one San Diego crosses town for.

A truck in San Diego is a neighborhood before it is a business — MIHO Gastrotruck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

There is a discipline to Farm-to-table cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. MIHO Gastrotruck works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

MIHO Gastrotruck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: San Diego as the stage, Farm-to-table as the craft, and the miho burger as the headline act.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like MIHO Gastrotruck matter to the places they feed. Home base San Diego, California; cuisine Farm-to-table; signature: the miho burger. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
