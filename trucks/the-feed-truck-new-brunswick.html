New Brunswick, New Jersey · Directory Entry

The Feed Truck

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Rutgers-night plates
Home base: New Brunswick, New Jersey

Some trucks chase trends; The Feed Truck chases its regulars. Based in New Brunswick, New Jersey, the American comfort kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The famous hand-out at the The Feed Truck window is rutgers-night plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A good truck is town news in New Brunswick, and The Feed Truck has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

The Feed Truck serves American comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and The Feed Truck's content is specific — American comfort cooked in New Brunswick, New Jersey, judged by one dish above the rest: rutgers-night plates.

The entry sits in the New Brunswick pages, filed under New Jersey: The Feed Truck. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
