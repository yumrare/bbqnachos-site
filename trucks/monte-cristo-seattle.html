Monte Cristo

Cuisine: Sandwiches
Signature: Monte cristo sandwiches
Home base: Seattle, Washington

Monte Cristo is a Sandwiches food truck based in Seattle, Washington. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Seattle, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The monte cristo sandwiches at Monte Cristo is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Seattle's, and Monte Cristo holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Sandwiches was made for the truck form, and Monte Cristo leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Monte Cristo's content is Sandwiches cooked in Seattle, Washington, and judged by one dish above the rest: monte cristo sandwiches.

For the permanent record: Monte Cristo serves Seattle its Sandwiches, and the monte cristo sandwiches is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
