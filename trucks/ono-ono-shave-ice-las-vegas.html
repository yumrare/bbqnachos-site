Ono Ono Shave Ice

Cuisine: Shave ice
Signature: Ono shave ice
Home base: Las Vegas, Nevada

Out of Las Vegas, Nevada, Ono Ono Shave Ice serves Shave ice to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the ono shave ice. At Ono Ono Shave Ice, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Las Vegas, and Ono Ono Shave Ice has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Shave ice cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Ono Ono Shave Ice leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Ono Ono Shave Ice's writes itself: Shave ice from Las Vegas, Nevada, with ono shave ice as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: Ono Ono Shave Ice serves Las Vegas its Shave ice, and the ono shave ice is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
