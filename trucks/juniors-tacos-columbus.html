Junior's Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Junior's tacos
Home base: Columbus, Ohio

Junior's Tacos holds its home base in Columbus, Ohio and serves Mexican from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

If Junior's Tacos could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: junior's tacos. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

In Columbus, a good truck is town news, and Junior's Tacos has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Junior's Tacos cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Junior's Tacos would still stand alone on its facts: a Mexican kitchen based in Columbus, known first and last for junior's tacos.

Junior's Tacos stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
