Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

BRGR Truck

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: BRGR burgers
Home base: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Read the Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania pages and you will find BRGR Truck: a Burgers truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Ask at the window what BRGR Truck is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: brgr burgers. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Pittsburgh eats above its weight because of trucks like BRGR Truck. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Speed is part of the recipe at BRGR Truck — Burgers cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. BRGR Truck's content is Burgers cooked in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and judged by one dish above the rest: brgr burgers.

That is the entry for BRGR Truck: Pittsburgh's own, a Burgers truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
