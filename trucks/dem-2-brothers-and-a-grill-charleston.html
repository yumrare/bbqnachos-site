Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: WV smoked ribs
Home base: Charleston, West Virginia

Out of Charleston, West Virginia, Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill serves Barbecue to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Ask at the Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: wv smoked ribs. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

Ask in Charleston where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill. The Barbecue cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

No other truck in this record runs on Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill's exact combination: Barbecue out of Charleston, with wv smoked ribs as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: Dem 2 Brothers and a Grill serves Charleston its Barbecue, and the wv smoked ribs is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
