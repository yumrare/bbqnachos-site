The Maine Lobster Lady

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Maine lobster rolls in the desert
Home base: Scottsdale, Arizona

In Scottsdale, Arizona, the name The Maine Lobster Lady means Seafood done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. The Maine Lobster Lady holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The signature at The Maine Lobster Lady is maine lobster rolls in the desert, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Scottsdale crosses town for.

Trucks like The Maine Lobster Lady are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Scottsdale may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

The kitchen at The Maine Lobster Lady treats Seafood as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No other truck in this record runs on The Maine Lobster Lady's exact combination: Seafood out of Scottsdale, with maine lobster rolls in the desert as the order its name is attached to.

So the record stands: The Maine Lobster Lady, of Scottsdale, Arizona — Seafood by trade, maine lobster rolls in the desert by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
