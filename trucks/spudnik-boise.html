Boise, Idaho · Directory Entry

Spudnik

Cuisine: Potatoes
Signature: Loaded potato plates
Home base: Boise, Idaho

Some trucks chase trends; Spudnik chases its regulars. Based in Boise, Idaho, the Potatoes kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The signature at Spudnik is loaded potato plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Boise crosses town for.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Boise; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Spudnik has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Spudnik. The Potatoes cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Spudnik's content is specific — Potatoes cooked in Boise, Idaho, judged by one dish above the rest: loaded potato plates.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Spudnik matter to the places they feed. Home base Boise, Idaho; cuisine Potatoes; signature: loaded potato plates — a small, complete portrait.
