Lil Cheezers

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Derby City melts
Home base: Louisville, Kentucky

To eaters in Louisville, Kentucky, Lil Cheezers simply means Grilled cheese done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Lil Cheezers is carried by its derby city melts. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck in a place the size of Louisville becomes a landmark with a schedule, and Lil Cheezers is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

Speed is part of the recipe at Lil Cheezers — Grilled cheese cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Lil Cheezers's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Louisville as the stage, Grilled cheese as the craft, and derby city melts as the headline act.
