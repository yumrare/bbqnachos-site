Lucky Slice Truck

Cuisine: Pizza
Signature: Lucky slices
Home base: Ogden, Utah

Lucky Slice Truck of Ogden, Utah earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Pizza kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Ask at the Lucky Slice Truck window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: lucky slices. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

In Ogden, a good truck is town news, and Lucky Slice Truck has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

Space on the Lucky Slice Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Pizza here tastes so focused.

Lucky Slice Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Ogden as the stage, Pizza as the craft, and lucky slices as the headline act.

The quarterly review will come back to Lucky Slice Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
