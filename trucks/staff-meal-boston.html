Staff Meal

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Staff-meal plates
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts

Ask around Boston where the Global comfort cooking is and somebody points to Staff Meal before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Massachusetts, service after service, the only honest way there is.

The staff-meal plates at Staff Meal is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

A Boston truck's real address is wherever its line forms, and Staff Meal draws one at lunch, on market days, and in the after-game drift — without ever hurrying anybody through the window.

At Staff Meal, Global comfort is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Staff Meal's content is Global comfort cooked in Boston, Massachusetts, and judged by one dish above the rest: staff-meal plates.

For the permanent record: Staff Meal serves Boston its Global comfort, and the staff-meal plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
