Emerald Coast Eats

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Emerald Coast plates
Home base: Destin, Florida

Emerald Coast Eats holds its home base in Destin, Florida and serves Seafood from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The signature at Emerald Coast Eats is emerald coast plates, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Destin crosses town for.

In a place the size of Destin, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Emerald Coast Eats is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Emerald Coast Eats serves Seafood stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Emerald Coast Eats's content is specific — Seafood cooked in Destin, Florida, judged by one dish above the rest: emerald coast plates.

The entry sits in the Destin pages, filed under Florida: Emerald Coast Eats. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
