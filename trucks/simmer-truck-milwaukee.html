Milwaukee, Wisconsin · Directory Entry

Simmer Truck

Cuisine: Soups and comfort
Signature: Simmer bowls
Home base: Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Simmer Truck is a Soups and comfort food truck based in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Milwaukee, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

simmer bowls: that is the signature at Simmer Truck, and it makes the whole argument for the truck in a single order. This is the dish Milwaukee crosses town for.

Milwaukee eats above its weight because of trucks like Simmer Truck. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

At Simmer Truck the Soups and comfort is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and Simmer Truck's content is specific — Soups and comfort cooked in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, judged by one dish above the rest: simmer bowls.

That is the entry for Simmer Truck: Milwaukee's own, a Soups and comfort truck with a signature worth the trip. It takes its place in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite truck in America.
