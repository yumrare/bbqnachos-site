New Orleans, Louisiana · Directory Entry

Rollin' Fatties

Cuisine: Burritos
Signature: Fat burritos
Home base: New Orleans, Louisiana

Rollin' Fatties rolls out of New Orleans, Louisiana, serving Burritos to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the fat burritos. At Rollin' Fatties, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck survives in New Orleans by being somebody's favorite, and Rollin' Fatties is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

There is no freezer-to-fryer shrug at Rollin' Fatties. The Burritos cooking is prepped each morning, seasoned like somebody's reputation depends on it — because at a truck window, it does.

Line up every truck in this record and Rollin' Fatties still stands alone on its facts: a Burritos kitchen based in New Orleans, known first and last for fat burritos.

For the permanent record: Rollin' Fatties serves New Orleans its Burritos, and the fat burritos is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
