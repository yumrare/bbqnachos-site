Big Sky Bowls

Cuisine: Rice bowls
Signature: Big Sky rice bowls
Home base: Billings, Montana

Big Sky Bowls keeps its home base in Billings, Montana, and cooks Rice bowls the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

If Big Sky Bowls could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: big sky rice bowls. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A good truck is town news in Billings, and Big Sky Bowls has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

At Big Sky Bowls, Rice bowls is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No theme song, no mascot: Big Sky Bowls's identity is written in three facts — Billings, Rice bowls, big sky rice bowls — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

So the record stands: Big Sky Bowls, of Billings, Montana — Rice bowls by trade, big sky rice bowls by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
