The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges

Cuisine: American
Signature: Museum-lawn plates
Home base: Bentonville, Arkansas

Home base Bentonville, Arkansas; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges cooks American the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the museum-lawn plates. At The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A good truck is town news in Bentonville, and The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges has made the headline more than once. It is woven into how the town feeds itself — farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair.

Speed is part of the recipe at The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges — American cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Line up every truck in this record and The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges still stands alone on its facts: a American kitchen based in Bentonville, known first and last for museum-lawn plates.

That is the entry for The Food Truck at Crystal Bridges — Bentonville's own, a American truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
