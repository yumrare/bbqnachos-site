Tucson, Arizona · Directory Entry

El Rapido

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Rapido burritos
Home base: Tucson, Arizona

Read the Tucson, Arizona pages and you will find El Rapido: a Mexican truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

If El Rapido could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: rapido burritos. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Ask in Tucson where to eat and the directions often end at a window: El Rapido's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

Space on the El Rapido menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Mexican here tastes so focused.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. El Rapido's content is Mexican cooked in Tucson, Arizona, and judged by one dish above the rest: rapido burritos.

For the permanent record: El Rapido serves Tucson its Mexican, and the rapido burritos is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
