Wilmington, Delaware · Directory Entry

KOI on the Go

Cuisine: Thai fusion
Signature: KOI truck plates
Home base: Wilmington, Delaware

KOI on the Go is a Thai fusion food truck based in Wilmington, Delaware. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Wilmington, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at KOI on the Go it is koi truck plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Trucks like KOI on the Go are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Wilmington may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Cooking Thai fusion from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and KOI on the Go works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

KOI on the Go does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Wilmington, Thai fusion, koi truck plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Filed under Delaware, in the Wilmington pages: KOI on the Go. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
