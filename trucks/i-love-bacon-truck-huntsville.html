Huntsville, Alabama · Directory Entry

I Love Bacon Truck

Cuisine: Bacon-centric
Signature: Bacon plates
Home base: Huntsville, Alabama

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and I Love Bacon Truck of Huntsville, Alabama is no exception. A Bacon-centric kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Huntsville the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

The famous hand-out at the I Love Bacon Truck window is bacon plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Huntsville; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what I Love Bacon Truck has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at I Love Bacon Truck: the Bacon-centric is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No other truck in this record runs on I Love Bacon Truck's exact combination: Bacon-centric out of Huntsville, with bacon plates as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: I Love Bacon Truck serves Huntsville its Bacon-centric, and the bacon plates is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
