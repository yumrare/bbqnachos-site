Brothers Just Wingin It

Cuisine: Wings
Signature: Brothers' wings
Home base: Minneapolis, Minnesota

Brothers Just Wingin It holds its home base in Minneapolis, Minnesota and serves Wings from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Ask at the window what Brothers Just Wingin It is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: brothers' wings. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A truck survives in Minneapolis by being somebody's favorite, and Brothers Just Wingin It is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Space on the Brothers Just Wingin It menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Wings here tastes so focused.

Line up every truck in this record and Brothers Just Wingin It still stands alone on its facts: a Wings kitchen based in Minneapolis, known first and last for brothers' wings.

For the permanent record: Brothers Just Wingin It serves Minneapolis its Wings, and the brothers' wings is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
