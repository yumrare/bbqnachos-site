Mexicue

Cuisine: Mexican-barbecue
Signature: Smoked short rib tacos
Home base: New York, New York

Ask around New York where the Mexican-barbecue cooking is and somebody points to Mexicue before long. It earned its line out at the curb in New York, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the smoked short rib tacos. At Mexicue, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

A truck survives in New York by being somebody's favorite, and Mexicue is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

At Mexicue the Mexican-barbecue is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

Mexicue's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: New York as the stage, Mexican-barbecue as the craft, and smoked short rib tacos as the headline act.

Mexicue stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
