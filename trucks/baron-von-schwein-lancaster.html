Baron Von Schwein

Cuisine: Barbecue pork
Signature: Pulled pork and bacon desserts
Home base: Lancaster, Pennsylvania

Baron Von Schwein rolls out of Lancaster, Pennsylvania, serving Barbecue pork to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at Baron Von Schwein is famous for handing out is pulled pork and bacon desserts — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Ask in Lancaster where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Baron Von Schwein's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

Everything on the Baron Von Schwein menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Barbecue pork here tastes focused.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Baron Von Schwein's content is Barbecue pork cooked in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, and judged by one dish above the rest: pulled pork and bacon desserts.

Baron Von Schwein stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
