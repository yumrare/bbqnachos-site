Tucson, Arizona · Directory Entry

Kababeque

Cuisine: Indian barbecue
Signature: Kababeque plates
Home base: Tucson, Arizona

Kababeque holds its home base in Tucson, Arizona and serves Indian barbecue from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the kababeque plates. At Kababeque, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Ask in Tucson where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Kababeque's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

At Kababeque, Indian barbecue is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Kababeque's content is Indian barbecue cooked in Tucson, Arizona, and judged by one dish above the rest: kababeque plates.

Filed under Arizona, in the Tucson pages: Kababeque. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
