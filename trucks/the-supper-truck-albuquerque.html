Albuquerque, New Mexico · Directory Entry

The Supper Truck

Cuisine: New American
Signature: Supper plates
Home base: Albuquerque, New Mexico

The entry for Albuquerque, New Mexico reads The Supper Truck: a New American truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

Ask at the window what The Supper Truck is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: supper plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

A truck in a place the size of Albuquerque becomes a landmark with a schedule, and The Supper Truck is one: the stop people name when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect.

What The Supper Truck serves is New American stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. The Supper Truck's content is New American cooked in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and judged by one dish above the rest: supper plates.

The quarterly review will come back to The Supper Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
