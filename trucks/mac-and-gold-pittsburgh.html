Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Mac & Gold

Cuisine: Mac and cheese
Signature: Gold-standard mac
Home base: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Mac & Gold is a Mac and cheese food truck based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Pittsburgh, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

What the window at Mac & Gold is famous for handing out is gold-standard mac — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Ask in Pittsburgh where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Mac & Gold's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

At Mac & Gold, Mac and cheese is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Mac & Gold does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — Pittsburgh, Mac and cheese, gold-standard mac — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Entries close the way services end: with the window still warm. Mac & Gold of Pittsburgh takes its place in the record — Mac and cheese, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
