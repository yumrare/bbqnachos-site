Burlington, Vermont · Featured Truck

The Skinny Pancake

Cuisine: Crepes
Signature: Savory and sweet crepes
Home base: Burlington, Vermont

The Skinny Pancake is a Crepes food truck based in Burlington, Vermont. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Burlington, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at The Skinny Pancake it is savory and sweet crepes. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Burlington; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what The Skinny Pancake has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

At The Skinny Pancake the Crepes is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The featured tier holds the trucks other trucks get measured against. The Skinny Pancake earned its standing the only way it can be earned: year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

Regulars will tell you the real signature is the rhythm: the truck posted up, the line forming, the window sliding open. The Skinny Pancake has that rhythm down to a ritual, and rituals are what turn a good truck into a beloved one.

There is a small ceremony to a stop at The Skinny Pancake: the chalkboard read, the order called, the wait spent trading notes with strangers who are about to agree with you. Trucks that create rituals outlive trucks that only create meals.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Skinny Pancake would still stand alone on its facts: a Crepes kitchen based in Burlington, known first and last for savory and sweet crepes.

So the record stands for The Skinny Pancake of Burlington, Vermont: Crepes by trade, savory and sweet crepes by reputation. Every line here is earned, never given — and this one was earned in full.
