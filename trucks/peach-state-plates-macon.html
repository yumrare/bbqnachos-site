Macon, Georgia · Directory Entry

Peach State Plates

Cuisine: Southern comfort
Signature: Peach State plates
Home base: Macon, Georgia

Out of Macon, Georgia, Peach State Plates serves Southern comfort to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Peach State Plates and go straight to the peach state plates. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Macon; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Peach State Plates has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Peach State Plates: the Southern comfort is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

Peach State Plates's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Macon as the stage, Southern comfort as the craft, and peach state plates as the headline act.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Peach State Plates matter to the places they feed. Home base Macon, Georgia; cuisine Southern comfort; signature: peach state plates — a small, complete portrait.
