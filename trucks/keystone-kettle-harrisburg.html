Keystone Kettle

Cuisine: Soups and stews
Signature: Keystone kettle bowls
Home base: Harrisburg, Pennsylvania

Keystone Kettle of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Soups and stews kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Keystone Kettle is carried by its keystone kettle bowls. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

What Keystone Kettle gives Harrisburg is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Keystone Kettle cooks like it knows it: Soups and stews with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.
