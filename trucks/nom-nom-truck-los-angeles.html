Los Angeles, California · Featured Truck

Nom Nom Truck

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Banh mi and lemongrass tacos
Home base: Los Angeles, California
Rolling since 2009

Every truck in this directory earned its line, and Nom Nom Truck of Los Angeles, California is no exception. A Vietnamese kitchen on wheels, it built its name in Los Angeles the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out.

Nom Nom Truck has been rolling since 2009. A finalist on the first season of The Great Food Truck Race with its banh mi.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Nom Nom Truck is carried by its banh mi and lemongrass tacos. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

A truck survives in Los Angeles by being somebody's favorite, and Nom Nom Truck is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Nom Nom Truck cooks like a truck kitchen forgives nothing — because it doesn't. Confident seasoning, honest portions, window service quick without ever feeling rushed: that is the house standard.

Featured entries in this directory are the trucks other trucks get measured against, and Nom Nom Truck earned that standing in Los Angeles the only way it can be earned — year after year of services where the food matched the reputation.

The afterglow separates Nom Nom Truck from a hundred good trucks: people leave planning the next visit and recruiting companions for it. That is not marketing — that is a signature landing.

The originality of Nom Nom Truck is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Los Angeles as the stage, Vietnamese as the craft, and banh mi and lemongrass tacos as the headline.
