Omaha, Nebraska · Directory Entry

Maria Bonita

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Bonita tacos
Home base: Omaha, Nebraska

Ask around Omaha where the Mexican cooking is, and sooner or later somebody points you to Maria Bonita. The truck holds a line in this directory's national record because it earned one first — out at the curb in Nebraska, service after service.

The signature at Maria Bonita is bonita tacos, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Omaha crosses town for.

Trucks like Maria Bonita are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Omaha may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Maria Bonita serves Mexican stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Line up every truck in this record and Maria Bonita still stands alone on its facts: a Mexican kitchen based in Omaha, known first and last for bonita tacos.

For the permanent record: Maria Bonita serves Omaha its Mexican, and the bonita tacos is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
