Cleveland, Ohio · Directory Entry

Touch Supper Truck

Cuisine: New American
Signature: Supper club plates
Home base: Cleveland, Ohio

Touch Supper Truck of Cleveland, Ohio earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A New American kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Ask at the window what Touch Supper Truck is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: supper club plates. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Everybody knows everybody at a Cleveland truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Touch Supper Truck thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

At Touch Supper Truck the New American is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

What makes Touch Supper Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: New American out of Cleveland, with supper club plates as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Touch Supper Truck — Cleveland's own, a New American truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
