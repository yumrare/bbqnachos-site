Middletown, Connecticut · Directory Entry

The Whey Station

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Whey-out melts
Home base: Middletown, Connecticut

Ask around Middletown where the Grilled cheese cooking is and somebody points to The Whey Station before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Connecticut, service after service, the only honest way there is.

If The Whey Station could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: whey-out melts. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

Middletown eats above its weight because of trucks like The Whey Station. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

Everything on the The Whey Station menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Grilled cheese here tastes focused.

Entries here share a shape; the content is what differs, and The Whey Station's content is specific — Grilled cheese cooked in Middletown, Connecticut, judged by one dish above the rest: whey-out melts.

The quarterly review will come back to The Whey Station the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
