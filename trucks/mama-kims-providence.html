Providence, Rhode Island · Directory Entry

Mama Kim's

Cuisine: Korean
Signature: Korean BBQ tacos
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Read the Providence, Rhode Island pages and you will find Mama Kim's: a Korean truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Mama Kim's it is korean bbq tacos. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Providence is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Mama Kim's has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Providence gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Korean cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Mama Kim's leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

Line up every truck in this record and Mama Kim's still stands alone on its facts: a Korean kitchen based in Providence, known first and last for korean bbq tacos.

For the permanent record: Mama Kim's serves Providence its Korean, and the korean bbq tacos is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
