Natchez Steam Wagon

Cuisine: Southern comfort
Signature: River comfort plates
Home base: Natchez, Mississippi

To eaters in Natchez, Mississippi, Natchez Steam Wagon simply means Southern comfort done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the river comfort plates at Natchez Steam Wagon lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

In a place the size of Natchez, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Natchez Steam Wagon is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

The kitchen at Natchez Steam Wagon treats Southern comfort as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Natchez Steam Wagon's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Natchez as the stage, Southern comfort as the craft, and river comfort plates as the headline act.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Natchez Steam Wagon matter to the places they feed. Home base Natchez, Mississippi; cuisine Southern comfort; signature: river comfort plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
