Los Angeles, California · Directory Entry

India Jones Chow Truck

Cuisine: Indian
Signature: Butter chicken and naan wraps
Home base: Los Angeles, California

Ask around Los Angeles where the Indian cooking is and somebody points to India Jones Chow Truck before long. It earned its line out at the curb in California, service after service, the only honest way there is.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the butter chicken and naan wraps at India Jones Chow Truck lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

A truck in Los Angeles is a neighborhood before it is a business — India Jones Chow Truck has always understood that. Offices claim it at noon, families claim it at dusk, and both are right.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at India Jones Chow Truck: the Indian is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and India Jones Chow Truck's is easy to write: Indian from Los Angeles, California, with butter chicken and naan wraps as the dish the city attached to its name.

The entry sits in the Los Angeles pages, filed under California: India Jones Chow Truck. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
