Kahului, Hawaii · Directory Entry

Geste Shrimp Truck

Cuisine: Shrimp
Signature: Maui garlic shrimp
Home base: Kahului, Hawaii

Ask around Kahului where the Shrimp cooking is and somebody points to Geste Shrimp Truck before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Hawaii, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Geste Shrimp Truck the dish is maui garlic shrimp. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

Ask in Kahului where to eat and the directions often end at a window: Geste Shrimp Truck's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

There is a discipline to Shrimp cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Geste Shrimp Truck works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

What makes Geste Shrimp Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Shrimp out of Kahului, with maui garlic shrimp as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Geste Shrimp Truck — Kahului's own, a Shrimp truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
