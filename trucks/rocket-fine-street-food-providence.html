Rocket Fine Street Food

Cuisine: Burgers
Signature: Rocket burgers
Home base: Providence, Rhode Island

Rocket Fine Street Food holds its home base in Providence, Rhode Island and serves Burgers from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking; at Rocket Fine Street Food the dish is rocket burgers. It built the line, and it is ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In a place the size of Providence, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Rocket Fine Street Food is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Cooking Burgers from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Rocket Fine Street Food works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

No other truck in this record runs on Rocket Fine Street Food's exact combination: Burgers out of Providence, with rocket burgers as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: Rocket Fine Street Food serves Providence its Burgers, and the rocket burgers is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
