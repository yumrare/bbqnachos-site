San Diego, California · Directory Entry

Two for the Road

Cuisine: American diner
Signature: Cap'n Crunch french toast
Home base: San Diego, California

Ask around San Diego where the American diner cooking is and somebody points to Two for the Road before long. It earned its line out at the curb in California, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Ask at the window what Two for the Road is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: cap'n crunch french toast. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

San Diego counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Two for the Road is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

At Two for the Road, American diner is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

No other truck in this record runs on Two for the Road's exact combination: American diner out of San Diego, with cap'n crunch french toast as the order its name is attached to.

That is the entry for Two for the Road — San Diego's own, a American diner truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
