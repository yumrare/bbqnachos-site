Bend, Oregon · Directory Entry

Cascadia Bowls

Cuisine: Rice bowls
Signature: High-desert rice bowls
Home base: Bend, Oregon

In Bend, Oregon, the name Cascadia Bowls means Rice bowls done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Cascadia Bowls holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Regulars skip the whole menu discussion at Cascadia Bowls and go straight to the high-desert rice bowls. It is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Trucks like Cascadia Bowls are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Bend may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Cascadia Bowls: the Rice bowls is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No other truck in this record runs on Cascadia Bowls's exact combination: Rice bowls out of Bend, with high-desert rice bowls as the order its name is attached to.

Filed under Oregon, in the Bend pages: Cascadia Bowls. The directory keeps the entry the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and on purpose.
