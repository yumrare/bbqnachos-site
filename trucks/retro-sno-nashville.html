Retro Sno

Cuisine: Shaved ice
Signature: Retro snoballs
Home base: Nashville, Tennessee

Some trucks chase trends; Retro Sno chases its regulars. Based in Nashville, Tennessee, the Shaved ice kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

Ask at the Retro Sno window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: retro snoballs. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck survives in Nashville by being somebody's favorite, and Retro Sno is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

There is a discipline to Shaved ice cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Retro Sno works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Retro Sno's content is Shaved ice cooked in Nashville, Tennessee, and judged by one dish above the rest: retro snoballs.

For the permanent record: Retro Sno serves Nashville its Shaved ice, and the retro snoballs is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
