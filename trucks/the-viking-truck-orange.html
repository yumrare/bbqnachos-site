Orange, California · Directory Entry

The Viking Truck

Cuisine: Sausages
Signature: Giant corndogs and sausages
Home base: Orange, California

The entry for Orange, California reads The Viking Truck: a Sausages truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and The Viking Truck is carried by its giant corndogs and sausages. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Ask in Orange where to eat and the directions often end at one window: The Viking Truck's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

The Viking Truck serves Sausages stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, The Viking Truck would still stand alone on its facts: a Sausages kitchen based in Orange, known first and last for giant corndogs and sausages.

The entry sits in the Orange pages, filed under California: The Viking Truck. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
