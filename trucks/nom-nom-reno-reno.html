Reno, Nevada · Directory Entry

Nom Nom Reno

Cuisine: Global comfort
Signature: Nom plates
Home base: Reno, Nevada

To eaters in Reno, Nevada, Nom Nom Reno simply means Global comfort done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

At some point a signature stops being a menu item and becomes an address, and that is where the nom plates at Nom Nom Reno lives: people do not say the truck, they say the dish.

What Nom Nom Reno gives Reno is a taste of the wider truck world without leaving home: big-city cooking, small-town welcome, and a line of neighbors trading recommendations while they wait.

Nom Nom Reno serves Global comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No other truck in this record runs on Nom Nom Reno's exact combination: Global comfort out of Reno, with nom plates as the order its name is attached to.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like Nom Nom Reno matter to the places they feed. Home base Reno, Nevada; cuisine Global comfort; signature: nom plates. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
