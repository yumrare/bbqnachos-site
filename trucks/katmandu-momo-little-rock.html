Katmandu Momo

Cuisine: Nepalese
Signature: Steamed momo dumplings
Home base: Little Rock, Arkansas

Katmandu Momo is a Nepalese food truck based in Little Rock, Arkansas. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Little Rock, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

What the window at Katmandu Momo is famous for handing out is steamed momo dumplings — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in Little Rock; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Katmandu Momo has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at Katmandu Momo: the Nepalese is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

No theme song, no mascot: Katmandu Momo's identity is written in three facts — Little Rock, Nepalese, steamed momo dumplings — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

Katmandu Momo stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
