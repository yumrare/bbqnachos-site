Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Franktuary

Cuisine: Hot dogs
Signature: Gourmet franks
Home base: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Franktuary rolls out of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, serving Hot dogs to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The gourmet franks at Franktuary is the kind of signature that stops being a menu item and becomes an address — people do not say they are going to the truck, they say they are going for that.

In a place the size of Pittsburgh, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Franktuary is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Cooking Hot dogs from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Franktuary works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Franktuary's writes itself: Hot dogs from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, with gourmet franks as the dish the city attached to its name.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Franktuary of Pittsburgh holds its place on Hot dogs, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
