The Lunch Box Sioux Falls

Cuisine: American comfort
Signature: Lunch box plates
Home base: Sioux Falls, South Dakota

Out of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, The Lunch Box Sioux Falls serves American comfort to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The famous hand-out at the The Lunch Box Sioux Falls window is lunch box plates. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

A truck in Sioux Falls answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and The Lunch Box Sioux Falls thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Space on the The Lunch Box Sioux Falls menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the American comfort here tastes so focused.

What makes The Lunch Box Sioux Falls itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: American comfort out of Sioux Falls, with lunch box plates as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: The Lunch Box Sioux Falls serves Sioux Falls its American comfort, and the lunch box plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
