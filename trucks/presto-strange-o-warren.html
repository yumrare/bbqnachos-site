Warren, Rhode Island · Directory Entry

Presto Strange O

Cuisine: Coffee
Signature: Strange O coffee
Home base: Warren, Rhode Island

Read the Warren, Rhode Island pages and you will find Presto Strange O: a Coffee truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the strange o coffee. At Presto Strange O, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Ask in Warren where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Presto Strange O's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

A truck kitchen forgives nothing, and Presto Strange O cooks like it knows it: Coffee with the seasoning confident, the portions honest, and the window service quick without ever feeling rushed.

Line up every truck in this record and Presto Strange O still stands alone on its facts: a Coffee kitchen based in Warren, known first and last for strange o coffee.

The quarterly review will come back to Presto Strange O the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
