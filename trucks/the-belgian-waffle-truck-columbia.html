The Belgian Waffle Truck

Cuisine: Waffles
Signature: Belgian waffles
Home base: Columbia, South Carolina

The Belgian Waffle Truck of Columbia, South Carolina earned its line the way the best trucks do: a short menu, a hot grill, and a line that forms because the word got out. A Waffles kitchen on wheels, and no exception to the rule.

Ask at the window what The Belgian Waffle Truck is known for and the answer comes back the same every time: belgian waffles. That is the order the regulars build their visit around — the dish on the truck's name in the local memory, the one first-timers get pointed to and old hands order without looking up.

Columbia is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and The Belgian Waffle Truck has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Columbia gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

Space on the The Belgian Waffle Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Waffles here tastes so focused.

The Belgian Waffle Truck's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Columbia as the stage, Waffles as the craft, and belgian waffles as the headline act.

When the quarterly review comes back to The Belgian Waffle Truck, it will check what it checks on every truck: window open, line forming, signature singing. No one expects the answer to change.
