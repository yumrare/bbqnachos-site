Portland, Oregon · Directory Entry

The People's Pig

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Porchetta sandwich
Home base: Portland, Oregon

In Portland, Oregon, the name The People's Pig means Barbecue done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. The People's Pig holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

Regulars shortcut the whole menu discussion with two words: the porchetta sandwich. At The People's Pig, that single order is the introduction, the main event, and the reason the line bends around the corner on good days.

Serious eaters make for serious competition in Portland, and The People's Pig has held its place in that company for good reason. The city's whole truck culture runs on trucks exactly like this one.

Speed is part of the recipe at The People's Pig — Barbecue cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

What makes The People's Pig itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Barbecue out of Portland, with porchetta sandwich as the order its name is attached to.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like The People's Pig matter to the places they feed. Home base Portland, Oregon; cuisine Barbecue; signature: porchetta sandwich. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
