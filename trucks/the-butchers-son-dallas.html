The Butcher's Son

Cuisine: Sausages and burgers
Signature: Gourmet sausage sandwiches
Home base: Dallas, Texas

To eaters in Dallas, Texas, The Butcher's Son simply means Sausages and burgers done the truck way: cooked to order and handed through a window. The standing was earned the slow way, one service at a time.

The signature at The Butcher's Son is gourmet sausage sandwiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Dallas crosses town for.

Show up, season after season, with the same quality and the same welcome: that is what the Dallas scene rewards, and it is the reputation The Butcher's Son carries — a stop locals give directions by.

No freezer-to-fryer shrug at The Butcher's Son: the Sausages and burgers is prepped each morning and seasoned like a reputation depends on it. At a truck window, one does.

What makes The Butcher's Son itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Sausages and burgers out of Dallas, with gourmet sausage sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

The Butcher's Son stands in the permanent record with its home base, cuisine, and signature stated plainly. Holding a line here means still being the truck your city says you are when the quarterly review comes around.
