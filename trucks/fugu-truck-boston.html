Boston, Massachusetts · Directory Entry

Fugu Truck

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Fugu rice bowls
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts

Read the Boston, Massachusetts pages and you will find Fugu Truck: a Asian fusion truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Fugu Truck is carried by its fugu rice bowls. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

Few cities run a truck scene as deep as Boston's, and Fugu Truck holds its corner of it through every rhythm the city keeps — office lunches, evening lots, festival weekends when the whole fleet turns out.

Asian fusion cooking suits the truck form — bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up — and Fugu Truck leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around what the kitchen does best, because a truck's reputation is only ever as wide as its window and as deep as its signature.

The honest entry is the distinct one, and Fugu Truck's writes itself: Asian fusion from Boston, Massachusetts, with fugu rice bowls as the dish the city attached to its name.

The quarterly review will come back to Fugu Truck the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
