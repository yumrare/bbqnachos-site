Cleveland, Ohio · Directory Entry

The Proper Pig

Cuisine: Barbecue
Signature: Proper pig smoked meats
Home base: Cleveland, Ohio

The Proper Pig rolls out of Cleveland, Ohio, serving Barbecue to a following that found it the honest way — at the curb. It stands in this record among America's most popular food trucks, where a truck keeps its place only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The signature at The Proper Pig is proper pig smoked meats, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Cleveland crosses town for.

In Cleveland, a good truck is town news, and The Proper Pig has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

The kitchen at The Proper Pig treats Barbecue as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. The Proper Pig's content is Barbecue cooked in Cleveland, Ohio, and judged by one dish above the rest: proper pig smoked meats.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. The Proper Pig of Cleveland holds its place on Barbecue, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
