San Francisco, California · Directory Entry

Adam's Grub Truck

Cuisine: Asian-American
Signature: Grub plates
Home base: San Francisco, California

Read the San Francisco, California pages and you will find Adam's Grub Truck: a Asian-American truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters, day after day.

There is always a dish that carries a truck, and Adam's Grub Truck is carried by its grub plates. First-timers order it because they were told to; everybody else orders it because they already know.

San Francisco counts its trucks among its landmarks, and Adam's Grub Truck is on that list: the dependable stop regulars bring out-of-towners to first, because this is the one that explains the city.

Everything on the Adam's Grub Truck menu earns its space the hard way: if a dish cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen, it does not get served. That discipline is why the Asian-American here tastes focused.

Adam's Grub Truck does not need a theme song or a mascot. Its identity is written in three facts — San Francisco, Asian-American, grub plates — and in the regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

For the permanent record: Adam's Grub Truck serves San Francisco its Asian-American, and the grub plates is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
