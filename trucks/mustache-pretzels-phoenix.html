Phoenix, Arizona · Directory Entry

Mustache Pretzels

Cuisine: Pretzels
Signature: Mustache pretzels
Home base: Phoenix, Arizona

Some trucks chase trends; Mustache Pretzels chases its regulars. Based in Phoenix, Arizona, the Pretzels kitchen has the loyalty that matters in this business — the kind measured in repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

What the window at Mustache Pretzels is famous for handing out is mustache pretzels — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

What Mustache Pretzels understands about Phoenix is that a truck here is a neighborhood before it is a business. It feeds offices at noon and families at dusk, and both crowds claim it as their own.

Speed is part of the recipe at Mustache Pretzels — Pretzels cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

Line up every truck in this record and Mustache Pretzels still stands alone on its facts: a Pretzels kitchen based in Phoenix, known first and last for mustache pretzels.

For the permanent record: Mustache Pretzels serves Phoenix its Pretzels, and the mustache pretzels is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
