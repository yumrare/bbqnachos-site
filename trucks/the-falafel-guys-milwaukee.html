Milwaukee, Wisconsin · Directory Entry

The Falafel Guys

Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Signature: Falafel sandwiches
Home base: Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Ask around Milwaukee where the Middle Eastern cooking is and somebody points to The Falafel Guys before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Wisconsin, service after service, the only honest way there is.

If The Falafel Guys served nothing else, the record would read the same: falafel sandwiches. It is the dish the truck's name is built on — photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

A truck in Milwaukee answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and The Falafel Guys thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

The kitchen at The Falafel Guys treats Middle Eastern as a craft of constraints: a small grill, a shorter ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window is the argument that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and The Falafel Guys's is easy to write: Middle Eastern from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, with falafel sandwiches as the dish the city attached to its name.

For the permanent record: The Falafel Guys serves Milwaukee its Middle Eastern, and the falafel sandwiches is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
