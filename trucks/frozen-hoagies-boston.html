Frozen Hoagies

Cuisine: Ice cream
Signature: Hoagie-style ice cream sandwiches
Home base: Boston, Massachusetts

Frozen Hoagies never chased a trend; it chased its regulars, and caught them. The Ice cream kitchen out of Boston, Massachusetts runs on repeat orders and first-name greetings at the window.

The signature at Frozen Hoagies is hoagie-style ice cream sandwiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Boston crosses town for.

The Boston scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation Frozen Hoagies carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

Cooking Ice cream from a truck window is a discipline — prep tight, cook to order, hide nothing — and Frozen Hoagies works it daily. What lands in your hands has the directness that makes street food a cuisine of its own.

No other truck in this record runs on Frozen Hoagies's exact combination: Ice cream out of Boston, with hoagie-style ice cream sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Frozen Hoagies of Boston holds its place on Ice cream, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
