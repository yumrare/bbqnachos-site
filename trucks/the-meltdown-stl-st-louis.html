St. Louis, Missouri · Directory Entry

The Meltdown STL

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Meltdown melts
Home base: St. Louis, Missouri

The Meltdown STL keeps its home base in St. Louis, Missouri, and cooks Grilled cheese the truck way: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. That is the combination that landed it in this record of America's most popular food trucks.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at The Meltdown STL it is meltdown melts. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

St. Louis eats above its weight because of trucks like The Meltdown STL. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

At The Meltdown STL the Grilled cheese is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

No theme song, no mascot: The Meltdown STL's identity is written in three facts — St. Louis, Grilled cheese, meltdown melts — and in regulars who would spot a substitute in one bite.

The entry sits in the St. Louis pages, filed under Missouri: The Meltdown STL. The directory keeps it the way the truck keeps its regulars — carefully, and very much on purpose.
