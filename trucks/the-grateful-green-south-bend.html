South Bend, Indiana · Directory Entry

The Grateful Green

Cuisine: Vegetarian
Signature: Green and grateful bowls
Home base: South Bend, Indiana

Home base South Bend, Indiana; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. The Grateful Green cooks Vegetarian the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

The famous hand-out at the The Grateful Green window is green and grateful bowls. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Ask in South Bend where to eat and the directions often end at a window: The Grateful Green's. That is the standing this truck holds — the first answer, given without hesitation.

The Grateful Green serves Vegetarian stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

The menu at The Grateful Green is vegetarian at heart — a meatless kitchen that has never treated that as a limitation, and it draws a following well beyond vegetarian diners for exactly that reason.

What makes The Grateful Green itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Vegetarian out of South Bend, with green and grateful bowls as the order its name is attached to.

For the permanent record: The Grateful Green serves South Bend its Vegetarian, and the green and grateful bowls is the order that proves it. Trucks like this are the whole reason the record exists.
