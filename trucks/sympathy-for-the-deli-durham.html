Sympathy for the Deli

Cuisine: Deli
Signature: Sympathy sandwiches
Home base: Durham, North Carolina

Home base Durham, North Carolina; method: small menu, big flavor, no shortcuts at the window. Sympathy for the Deli cooks Deli the truck way, and the combination speaks for itself.

Ask at the Sympathy for the Deli window what the truck is known for and you get one answer, every time: sympathy sandwiches. First-timers get pointed to it; old hands order it without looking up at all.

A truck in Durham answers to everybody — the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders — and Sympathy for the Deli thrives in that closeness. It is the kind of local institution that never needs a billboard.

Sympathy for the Deli serves Deli stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No other truck in this record runs on Sympathy for the Deli's exact combination: Deli out of Durham, with sympathy sandwiches as the order its name is attached to.

The quarterly review will come back to Sympathy for the Deli the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
