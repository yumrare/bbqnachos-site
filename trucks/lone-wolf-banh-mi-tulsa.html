Lone Wolf Banh Mi

Cuisine: Vietnamese
Signature: Banh mi and kimchi fries
Home base: Tulsa, Oklahoma

Lone Wolf Banh Mi holds its home base in Tulsa, Oklahoma and serves Vietnamese from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

If Lone Wolf Banh Mi could only serve one thing, the record would still read the same: banh mi and kimchi fries. It is the dish the truck built its name on, the one photographed, recommended, and argued about in the best way.

In a place the size of Tulsa, a food truck becomes a landmark with a schedule, and that is what Lone Wolf Banh Mi is: the stop people mention when they tell you their town eats better than you would expect. Its following is local, loyal, and earned.

Bold flavors, fast fire, food that eats well standing up: Vietnamese was made for the truck form, and Lone Wolf Banh Mi leans into all of it. The menu stays tight around the best of the kitchen, which is how a reputation stays honest.

Entries here share a shape; it is the content that differs. Lone Wolf Banh Mi's content is Vietnamese cooked in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and judged by one dish above the rest: banh mi and kimchi fries.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Lone Wolf Banh Mi of Tulsa holds its place on Vietnamese, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
