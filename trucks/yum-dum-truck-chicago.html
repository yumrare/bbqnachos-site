Yum Dum Truck

Cuisine: Asian fusion
Signature: Kimchi rice balls and baowiches
Home base: Chicago, Illinois

Ask around Chicago where the Asian fusion cooking is and somebody points to Yum Dum Truck before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Illinois, service after service, the only honest way there is.

The signature at Yum Dum Truck is kimchi rice balls and baowiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Chicago crosses town for.

A truck survives in Chicago by being somebody's favorite, and Yum Dum Truck is. Between the downtown lunch rush, the brewery lots, and the late-night corners, it does the quiet work of a neighborhood institution that happens to have wheels, feeding the same faces week after week.

Space on the Yum Dum Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Asian fusion here tastes so focused.

What makes Yum Dum Truck itself, and no other truck in this record, is the combination it runs on: Asian fusion out of Chicago, with kimchi rice balls and baowiches as the order its name is attached to.

Records like this one exist because trucks like Yum Dum Truck matter to the places they feed. Home base Chicago, Illinois; cuisine Asian fusion; signature: kimchi rice balls and baowiches — a small, complete portrait.
