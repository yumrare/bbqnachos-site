Fryborg

Cuisine: Fries
Signature: Hand-cut fries and poutine
Home base: New Haven, Connecticut

Fryborg is a Fries food truck based in New Haven, Connecticut. Its kitchen works out of a home base in New Haven, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The famous hand-out at the Fryborg window is hand-cut fries and poutine. Regulars defend it like a hometown team; newcomers understand why after exactly one bite.

Novelty does not keep a truck alive in New Haven; feeding the neighbors well does, and that is precisely what Fryborg has done — at market days, ball games, and lunch hours downtown.

Fryborg serves Fries stripped to its essentials and done with care. A great meal needs no dining room — just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Fryborg would still stand alone on its facts: a Fries kitchen based in New Haven, known first and last for hand-cut fries and poutine.

For the permanent record: Fryborg serves New Haven its Fries, and the hand-cut fries and poutine is the order that proves the whole entry. The record exists for trucks exactly like this one.
