Magic Carpet Foods

Cuisine: Vegetarian
Signature: Magic carpet falafel and tofu plates
Home base: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Rolling since 1984

Magic Carpet Foods holds its home base in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and serves Vegetarian from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

Magic Carpet Foods has been rolling since 1984. Rolling at the University of Pennsylvania since 1984 — one of America's longest-running food trucks.

What the window at Magic Carpet Foods is famous for handing out is magic carpet falafel and tofu plates — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

The Philadelphia scene rewards trucks that show up — same quality, same welcome, season after season — and that is the reputation Magic Carpet Foods carries. It is part of the city's rolling geography now, the kind of stop locals give directions by.

What Magic Carpet Foods serves is Vegetarian stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Magic Carpet Foods cooks vegetarian without apology or imitation — a meatless menu that stands on its own flavors, and a line that proves the point daily.

Ask a Philadelphia local to name the trucks that matter and Magic Carpet Foods comes early in the list — not out of habit, but out of a decade of lunches that landed right.

Ask the regulars why they come back and the answers stack up: the cooking, yes, and the remembering — names, usual orders, how you take it. Magic Carpet Foods runs on that kind of memory.
