Columbus, Ohio · Directory Entry

Pitabilities

Cuisine: Mediterranean
Signature: Pita abilities
Home base: Columbus, Ohio

Out of Columbus, Ohio, Pitabilities serves Mediterranean to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

What the window at Pitabilities is famous for handing out is pita abilities — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Columbus eats above its weight because of trucks like Pitabilities. It may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and its regulars would not trade the window for a dining room.

There is a discipline to Mediterranean cooking from a truck window: everything prepped tight, everything cooked to order, nothing hiding. Pitabilities works that discipline daily, and the result is food with the directness that makes street food its own cuisine.

The originality of Pitabilities is not a gimmick — it is a pairing: Columbus as the stage, Mediterranean as the craft, and pita abilities as the headline.

So the record stands: Pitabilities, of Columbus, Ohio — Mediterranean by trade, pita abilities by reputation. Its line in the national record was earned the way every line here is earned.
