Cape Elizabeth, Maine · Directory Entry

Salt Box Café

Cuisine: Café comfort
Signature: Tiny-truck breakfast sandwiches
Home base: Cape Elizabeth, Maine

Salt Box Café is a Café comfort food truck based in Cape Elizabeth, Maine. Its kitchen works out of a home base in Cape Elizabeth, and its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth, by lunch lines, and by the regulars who plan their week around its stops.

The signature at Salt Box Café is tiny-truck breakfast sandwiches, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Cape Elizabeth crosses town for.

Cape Elizabeth is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Salt Box Café has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Cape Elizabeth gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

What Salt Box Café serves is Café comfort stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

Salt Box Café's originality is a pairing, not a gimmick: Cape Elizabeth as the stage, Café comfort as the craft, and tiny-truck breakfast sandwiches as the headline act.
