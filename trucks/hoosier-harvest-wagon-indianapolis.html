Hoosier Harvest Wagon

Cuisine: Farm-to-table
Signature: Hoosier harvest plates
Home base: Indianapolis, Indiana

Ask around Indianapolis where the Farm-to-table cooking is and somebody points to Hoosier Harvest Wagon before long. It earned its line out at the curb in Indiana, service after service, the only honest way there is.

Every great truck has a dish that does the talking, and at Hoosier Harvest Wagon it is hoosier harvest plates. It is the order that built the line — the one talked about, written about, and ordered in the committed voice of someone who has had it before.

In Indianapolis, a good truck is town news, and Hoosier Harvest Wagon has been the headline more than once. Farmers markets, Friday nights, the county fair when it comes — the truck is woven into how the town feeds itself.

At Hoosier Harvest Wagon, Farm-to-table is a craft of constraints: a small grill, a short ticket, nowhere to hide. What comes through the window argues that constraints, respected, make cooking better.

If you lined up every truck in this record, Hoosier Harvest Wagon would still stand alone on its facts: a Farm-to-table kitchen based in Indianapolis, known first and last for hoosier harvest plates.
