Biloxi, Mississippi · Directory Entry

Gulf Coast Grill

Cuisine: Seafood
Signature: Coast grill plates
Home base: Biloxi, Mississippi

In Biloxi, Mississippi, the name Gulf Coast Grill means Seafood done the food truck way — cooked to order, handed through a window, and eaten wherever the day finds you. Gulf Coast Grill holds a place in this directory's record of the nation's most popular trucks, a standing earned the slow way, one service at a time.

A truck is carried by one dish more than any other, and Gulf Coast Grill is carried by its coast grill plates. First-timers order it on a tip; everybody else orders it from pure memory.

Everybody knows everybody at a Biloxi truck window: the regulars know the cook, the cook knows the orders. Gulf Coast Grill thrives in that closeness, a local institution that never needs a billboard.

What Gulf Coast Grill serves is Seafood stripped to its essentials and done with care — the kind of cooking that reminds you a great meal does not need a dining room, just a cook who respects the dish and a window to pass it through.

No other truck in this record runs on Gulf Coast Grill's exact combination: Seafood out of Biloxi, with coast grill plates as the order its name is attached to.

The quarterly review will come back to Gulf Coast Grill the way it comes back to every truck in this book — checking that the window is still open, the line still forms, and the signature still sings. Nobody around here expects a different result.
