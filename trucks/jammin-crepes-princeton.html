Jammin' Crepes

Cuisine: Crepes
Signature: Sweet and savory crepes
Home base: Princeton, New Jersey

The entry for Princeton, New Jersey reads Jammin' Crepes: a Crepes truck whose reputation was built the unglamorous way, by showing up and cooking like it matters. Directories record outcomes like that, and this is one of them.

What the window at Jammin' Crepes is famous for handing out is sweet and savory crepes — the order regulars defend like a hometown team and newcomers understand after exactly one bite.

Princeton is not a city that keeps a truck alive on novelty; a truck lasts here by feeding its neighbors well, and Jammin' Crepes has done exactly that. Market days, ball games, lunch hours downtown — wherever Princeton gathers, the truck is part of the gathering.

At Jammin' Crepes the Crepes is cooked to order and handed out hot, built for curbs, tailgates, and park benches. Speed is part of the recipe, and fast is never confused with careless.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Jammin' Crepes's is easy to write: Crepes from Princeton, New Jersey, with sweet and savory crepes as the dish the city attached to its name.

Services end with the window still warm, and so does this entry. Jammin' Crepes of Princeton holds its place on Crepes, a signature worth crossing town for, and a following that keeps it there.
