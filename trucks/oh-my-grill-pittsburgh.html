Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Directory Entry

Oh My Grill

Cuisine: Grilled cheese
Signature: Oh my melts
Home base: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

Oh My Grill holds its home base in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and serves Grilled cheese from a window that regulars plan their week around. Its name travels the way good truck names do: by word of mouth and by lunch lines.

The signature at Oh My Grill is oh my melts, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Pittsburgh crosses town for.

Ask in Pittsburgh where to eat and the directions often end at one window: Oh My Grill's. It is the first answer people give, and they give it without hesitation.

Speed is part of the recipe at Oh My Grill — Grilled cheese cooked to order and handed out hot, built for people eating on a curb, a tailgate, or a park bench. The truck never confuses fast with careless.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and Oh My Grill's is easy to write: Grilled cheese from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, with oh my melts as the dish the city attached to its name.

That is the entry for Oh My Grill — Pittsburgh's own, a Grilled cheese truck with a signature worth the trip. It stands in the national record alongside the rest of the thousand, each one somebody's favorite.
