Hoboken, New Jersey · Directory Entry

The Taco Truck

Cuisine: Mexican
Signature: Authentic tacos al pastor
Home base: Hoboken, New Jersey

Out of Hoboken, New Jersey, The Taco Truck serves Mexican to a following found the honest way — at the curb. A truck keeps a following like that only as long as the cooking keeps its people coming back.

The signature at The Taco Truck is authentic tacos al pastor, and it is the whole argument for the truck in a single order. Trucks live and die by the dish people cross town for, and this is the one Hoboken crosses town for.

Trucks like The Taco Truck are how smaller cities eat above their weight. Hoboken may not have a thousand kitchens, but it has this one on wheels, and the regulars who line up at its window would not trade it for a dining room.

Space on the The Taco Truck menu is earned the hard way: a dish that cannot be cooked brilliantly in a truck kitchen does not get served. That is why the Mexican here tastes so focused.

The distinct entry is the honest one, and The Taco Truck's is easy to write: Mexican from Hoboken, New Jersey, with authentic tacos al pastor as the dish the city attached to its name.

Directory records like this one exist because trucks like The Taco Truck matter to the places they feed. Home base Hoboken, New Jersey; cuisine Mexican; signature: authentic tacos al pastor. A small, complete portrait of one of America's most popular food trucks.
